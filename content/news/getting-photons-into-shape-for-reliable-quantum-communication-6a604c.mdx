# Getting photons into shape for reliable quantum communication

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-photons-reliable-quantum-communication.html) on September 14, 2026.

The vast majority of modern quantum technologies—from quantum cryptography to the quantum internet to the quantum computer—rely on one essential element: the transmission of photons. Two qubits (two atoms, for example) exchange information: One qubit emits a photon, and the other qubit absorbs it.
