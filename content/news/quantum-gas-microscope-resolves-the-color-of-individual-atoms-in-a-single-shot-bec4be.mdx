# Quantum-gas microscope resolves the ''color'' of individual atoms in a single shot

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-quantum-gas-microscope-individual-atoms.html) on November 18, 2026.

In 2024, researchers at the Ultracold Quantum Gases group at ICFO built a quantum-gas microscope capable of imaging individual atoms in strontium quantum gases for the first time, marking a key enabling step for quantum simulation. At that time, the researchers imaged strontium-84, a boson that has no spin.
