# Xanadu Partners with GlobalFoundries to Scale Photonic Quantum Component Manufacturing on 300 mm Semiconductor Lines

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/xanadu-partners-with-globalfoundries-to-scale-photonic-quantum-component-manufacturing-on-300-mm-semiconductor-lines/) on October 6, 2026.

Xanadu and GlobalFoundries have partnered to mass-produce photonic quantum computing components. This collaboration will transition Xanadu's designs for ultra-low loss Silicon Nitride optical circuits and Superconducting Nanowire Single-Photon Detectors (SNSPDs) to GlobalFoundries' 300 mm commercial semiconductor fabrication facility. This aims to scale up the manufacturing of these components for fault-tolerant quantum computers and future quantum data centers.
