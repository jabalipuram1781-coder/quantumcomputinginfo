# Changing magnetic dimensionality boosts spin-wave frequency in Cr₂₋δTe₂

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-magnetic-dimensionality-boosts-frequency-crte.html) on November 10, 2026.

Magnons are quasiparticles that represent quantized spin waves in magnetic materials. They can carry information without moving electric charge, making them promising for future information technologies that could use less energy than conventional electronics. The frequency, stability and propagation distance of these waves are all important for their potential applications.
