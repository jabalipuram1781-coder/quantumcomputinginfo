# Universal Quantum Details Iterative Redecode Scheme for Fast Transversal Fault-Tolerant Logic

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/universal-quantum-details-iterative-redecode-scheme-for-fast-transversal-fault-tolerant-logic/) on November 3, 2026.

Universal Quantum's new Iterative Redecode Scheme (IRS) addresses the error-correlation bottleneck in fast transversal quantum logic. This decoding architecture, detailed in a paper by Mark Webber and colleagues, uses a propagation-assisted local (PAL) framework to coordinate corrections across logical blocks, resulting in significantly lower logical error rates compared to other decoders. The IRS achieves this by alternating local Minimum-Weight Perfect Matching (MWPM) with deterministic Pauli-frame propagation, allowing for efficient and accurate error correction in fault-tolerant quantum hardware.
