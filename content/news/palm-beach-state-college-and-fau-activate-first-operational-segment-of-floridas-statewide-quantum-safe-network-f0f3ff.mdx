# Palm Beach State College and FAU Activate First Operational Segment of Florida’s Statewide Quantum-Safe Network

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/palm-beach-state-college-and-fau-activate-first-operational-segment-of-floridas-statewide-quantum-safe-network/) on October 24, 2026.

Palm Beach State College (PBSC) and Florida Atlantic University (FAU) have activated Florida’s first operational quantum-safe network link, connecting their campuses using IonQ’s quantum key distribution (QKD) hardware. This initiative, supported by a $1 million defense infrastructure grant, is part of a larger plan to create a quantum-safe corridor across South Florida and develop a skilled workforce through PBSC's new Quantum Innovation Center and academic programs.
