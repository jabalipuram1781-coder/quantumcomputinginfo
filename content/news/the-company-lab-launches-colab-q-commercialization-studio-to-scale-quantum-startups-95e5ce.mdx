# The Company Lab Launches CO.LAB Q Commercialization Studio to Scale Quantum Startups

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/the-company-lab-launches-co-lab-q-commercialization-studio-to-scale-quantum-startups/) on August 17, 2026.

Chattanooga-based startup accelerator The Company Lab (CO.LAB) has launched CO.LAB Q, a 12-month quantum commercialization studio designed to translate early-stage quantum research into market-ready companies. Set to begin its inaugural cohort in November 2026, the program offers individualized commercialization pathways, pilot access, and technical infrastructure across quantum computing, networking, cybersecurity, sensing, and control hardware.
