# Adelaide University Engineers Synthetic Area Spread to Unlock High-Sensitivity Absolute Quantum Magnetometers

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/adelaide-university-engineers-synthetic-area-spread-to-unlock-high-sensitivity-absolute-quantum-magnetometers/) on November 7, 2026.

Engineers at Adelaide University have developed a new technique called "synthetic area spread" for superconducting quantum magnetometers. This method allows for absolute magnetic field sensing without sacrificing detector sensitivity, by using uniform loop geometries in two-dimensional Superconducting Quantum Interference Filter (SQIF) arrays to create a zero-field voltage anti-peak. This breakthrough simplifies fabrication and has implications for various quantum technologies, including MEG brain imaging and quantum hardware readout.
