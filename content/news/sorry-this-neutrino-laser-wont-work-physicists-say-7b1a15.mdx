# Sorry, this neutrino laser won''t work, physicists say

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-neutrino-laser-wont-physicists.html) on September 2, 2026.

Neutrinos are pervasive yet intangible particles that permeate the universe, streaming through whole planets, stars and our bodies by the trillions each second. The elementary particles are often described as "ghostly" for their near-zero mass and elusive nature, as they have very little interaction with normal matter.
