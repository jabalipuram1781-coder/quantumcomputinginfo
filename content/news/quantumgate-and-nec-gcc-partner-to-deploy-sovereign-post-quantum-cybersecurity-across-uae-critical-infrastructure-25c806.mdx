# QuantumGate and NEC GCC Partner to Deploy Sovereign Post-Quantum Cybersecurity Across UAE Critical Infrastructure

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantumgate-and-nec-gcc-partner-to-deploy-sovereign-post-quantum-cybersecurity-across-uae-critical-infrastructure/) on October 6, 2026.

QuantumGate and NEC GCC are partnering to implement sovereign post-quantum cybersecurity solutions across critical infrastructure and telecommunications in the UAE. The collaboration will deploy QuantumGate's cryptographic software, including QSphere, with NEC GCC leading systems integration. This initiative supports national quantum security directives to protect government, energy, transport, and telecommunications sectors.
