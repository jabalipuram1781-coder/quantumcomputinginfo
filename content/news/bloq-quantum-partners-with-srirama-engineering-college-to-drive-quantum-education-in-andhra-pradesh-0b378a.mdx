# BLOQ Quantum Partners with Srirama Engineering College to Drive Quantum Education in Andhra Pradesh

Kerala-based quantum technology developer BLOQ Quantum Pvt Ltd has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Srirama Engineering College (Sree Rama Engineering College) in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh. The partnership marks the first dedicated institutional quantum computing initiative in the Tirupati region, aiming to build regional capacity in quantum software development, workforce training, and research.
