# PsiQuantum and AMD Expand Partnership with Equity Investment to Co-Design Utility-Scale Quantum Infrastructure

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/psiquantum-and-amd-expand-partnership-with-equity-investment-to-co-design-utility-scale-quantum-infrastructure/) on November 13, 2026.

PsiQuantum and AMD are expanding their partnership with an equity investment from AMD to co-design the infrastructure for fault-tolerant quantum systems. The collaboration integrates AMD's GPUs, CPUs, and software with PsiQuantum's silicon photonics architecture to enhance real-time error correction and classical computing for hybrid workloads. They are prioritizing pharmaceutical research and molecular simulation, with plans to present further technical details at the SC26 supercomputing conference in Chicago.
