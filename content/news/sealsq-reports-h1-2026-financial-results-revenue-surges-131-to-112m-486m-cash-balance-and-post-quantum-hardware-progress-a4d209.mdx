# SEALSQ Reports H1 2026 Financial Results: Revenue Surges 131% to $11.2M, $486M Cash Balance, and Post-Quantum Hardware Progress

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/sealsq-reports-h1-2026-financial-results-revenue-surges-131-to-11-2m-486m-cash-balance-and-post-quantum-hardware-progress/) on September 11, 2026.

SEALSQ reported a significant surge in H1 2026 revenue to $11.15 million, a 131.1% increase year-over-year, driven by demand for secure-element products, PKI services, and ASIC design. The company also made substantial progress in post-quantum hardware development, achieving NIST certification for its QS7001 chip and advancing customer sampling of its QVault TPM, while reaffirming its FY 2026 revenue guidance.
