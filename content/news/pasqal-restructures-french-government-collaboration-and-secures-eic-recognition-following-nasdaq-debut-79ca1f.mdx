# Pasqal Restructures French Government Collaboration and Secures EIC Recognition Following Nasdaq Debut

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/pasqal-restructures-french-government-collaboration-and-secures-eic-recognition-following-nasdaq-debut/) on September 29, 2026.

Pasqal, a neutral-atom quantum hardware developer, has restructured its collaboration with French authorities to focus on civil, commercial-led fault-tolerant quantum computing, moving away from defense initiatives. This strategic shift follows its Nasdaq debut and aims to accelerate commercial deployments. Additionally, Pasqal received an EIC Fund Award, recognizing its transition from a deep-tech startup to a publicly traded quantum computer manufacturer.
