# U.S. Quantum Primacy Commission Outlines Industrial Strategy to Counter Global Supply-Chain Risks

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/u-s-quantum-primacy-commission-outlines-industrial-strategy-to-counter-global-supply-chain-risks/) on November 11, 2026.

A U.S. Quantum Primacy Commission report warns that despite a current U.S. lead in quantum computing, China's higher public funding and lower manufacturing costs threaten long-term competitiveness. The report outlines a four-pillar strategy (Discovery, Development, Deployment, Scale) to maintain U.S. quantum primacy, emphasizing federal intervention to address supply chain vulnerabilities and domestic disparities in funding and infrastructure. A final report with detailed proposals is expected in Spring 2027.
