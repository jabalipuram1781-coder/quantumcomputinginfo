# Aalborg University Orders ConScience Plug’N’Teach Superconducting Quantum Computer for Hands-On Engineering Training

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/aalborg-university-orders-conscience-plugnteach-superconducting-quantum-computer-for-hands-on-engineering-training/) on November 20, 2026.

Aalborg University in Denmark is set to acquire a superconducting quantum computer from ConScience AB, a Swedish quantum hardware company. The Plug’N’Teach system, scheduled for installation in 2027, will initially feature two qubits, expanding to seven, and will be used for hands-on engineering education and faculty research. This initiative aims to train students directly on quantum hardware operation and support research into component miniaturization and energy modeling.
