# University of Chicago and Tohoku University Establish Joint Quantum Materials Hub in Japan

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/university-of-chicago-and-tohoku-university-establish-joint-quantum-materials-hub-in-japan/) on November 11, 2026.

The University of Chicago and Tohoku University have partnered to establish a five-year Quantum Hub in Sendai, Japan, funded by the Japanese government. This collaboration, building on a 2023 alliance, combines UChicago's theoretical expertise with Tohoku's experimental capabilities to advance quantum materials research. The hub will also foster workforce development and entrepreneurship through joint mentorship and a quantum startup incubator.
