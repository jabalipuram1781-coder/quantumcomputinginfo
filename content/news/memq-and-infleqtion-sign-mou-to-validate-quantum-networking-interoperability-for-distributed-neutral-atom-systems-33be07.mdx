# memQ and Infleqtion Sign MoU to Validate Quantum Networking Interoperability for Distributed Neutral-Atom Systems

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/memq-and-infleqtion-sign-mou-to-validate-quantum-networking-interoperability-for-distributed-neutral-atom-systems/) on November 20, 2026.

memQ and Infleqtion have signed an MoU to validate interoperability between memQ’s quantum network architecture and Infleqtion’s quantum computing platform. This collaboration aims to enable modular multi-processor scaling by connecting separate quantum processing units. The initiative is crucial for enterprise and high-performance computing data centers to adopt standardized QPU-to-QPU networking.
