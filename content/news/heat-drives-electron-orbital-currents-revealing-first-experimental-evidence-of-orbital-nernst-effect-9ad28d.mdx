# Heat drives electron orbital currents, revealing first experimental evidence of orbital Nernst effect

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-electron-orbital-currents-revealing-experimental.html) on November 13, 2026.

Researchers at National Taiwan University (NTU) have reported the first experimental evidence of heat-current-driven transverse accumulation of electrons' orbital angular momentum, a phenomenon known as the orbital Nernst effect. The study was recently published in Nature Communications.
