# Quantum probability can''t be derived without assuming it first

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-quantum-probability-derived-assuming.html) on October 20, 2026.

For nearly a century, physicists have relied on the Born rule to calculate the chances of different outcomes when they measure a quantum system. Yet despite decades of effort, nobody has managed to derive the rule from more basic mathematical principles without making assumptions about probability along the way.
