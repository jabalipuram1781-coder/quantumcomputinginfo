# Scientists redetermine a key strontium frequency with 50 times better precision for quantum technologies

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-scientists-redetermine-key-strontium-frequency.html) on October 19, 2026.

The Korea Research Institute of Standards and Science (KRISS) has remeasured a key transition frequency of the strontium (Sr) atom for the first time in 88 years, since 1938, using a photonic grating chip. It has established a new absolute frequency value with a measurement uncertainty reduced by more than 50 times. By providing an accurate reference value for next-generation quantum technologies, this achievement is expected to contribute to the precise control and miniaturization of quantum devices, including atomic clocks and neutral-atom quantum computers.
