# Florida Atlantic University Awarded $400,000 NSF Grant to Expand Post-Quantum Cryptographic Protocols

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/florida-atlantic-university-awarded-400000-nsf-grant-to-expand-post-quantum-cryptographic-protocols/) on October 23, 2026.

Florida Atlantic University received a nearly $400,000 NSF grant to advance post-quantum cryptographic protocols. Led by Dr. Edoardo Persichetti and Dr. Veronika Kuchta, the project will develop advanced functionalities beyond basic primitives to secure decentralized systems against future quantum attacks. The initiative also supports student mentoring and cybersecurity outreach, building on FAU's leadership in quantum-safe security.
