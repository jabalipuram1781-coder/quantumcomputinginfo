# Anderon Releases First Superconducting Quantum PDK for 300mm Wafer Fabrication

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/anderon-releases-first-superconducting-quantum-pdk-for-300mm-wafer-fabrication/) on October 15, 2026.

Anderon, a quantum foundry operator, has released its first Superconducting Quantum Process Design Kit (PDK) for 300mm wafer fabrication. This PDK provides standardized design rules and building blocks for manufacturing superconducting quantum devices on their 300mm wafer line at the Albany NanoTech Complex. Anderon, backed by $2 billion in funding, offers various superconducting wafer types, including qubit, I/O signal, and amplifier/filter wafers.
