# Measurements reveal link between quantum spin and sound-carrying vibrations in an ultrathin semiconductor

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-reveal-link-quantum-vibrations-ultrathin.html) on November 16, 2026.

Researchers at the UCLA Samueli School of Engineering and Auburn University have achieved a scientific first: They measured atomic vibrations that carry heat and sound waves, and connected them to a quantum property called spin, which is a way of characterizing a subatomic particle's built-in magnetism.
