# California Awards $5.7 Million in Workforce Grants to Establish Sub-Doctoral Quantum Hardware and Technician Pipelines

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/california-awards-5-7-million-in-workforce-grants-to-establish-sub-doctoral-quantum-hardware-and-technician-pipelines/) on October 24, 2026.

The California Workforce Development Board has granted $5.74 million in Workforce Accelerator Fund 14 grants to establish sub-doctoral quantum hardware and technician pipelines. These grants aim to address the talent bottleneck in the quantum industry by funding programs across five educational and regional partnerships. The programs focus on stackable, industry-recognized certificates and vocational training to prepare individuals for roles in quantum hardware engineering, manufacturing, and related fields.
