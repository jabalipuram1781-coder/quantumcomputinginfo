# UK NQCC Advances Industrial Strategy with SparQ Access Rollout, ProQure Procurement, and 13 PoC Awards

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/uk-nqcc-advances-industrial-strategy-with-sparq-access-rollout-proqure-procurement-and-13-poc-awards/) on November 7, 2026.

The UK's National Quantum Computing Centre (NQCC) has shifted its strategy from technology development to active procurement and industrial adoption, as detailed in its 2026 Annual Report. Key initiatives include the SparQ Access Industry Channel, providing commercial quantum processor access to nine organizations, and awarding 13 grants for enterprise-led Proof of Concept projects. The NQCC is also transitioning its Quantum Computing Testbed Initiative (QCTI) into the ProQure framework for government procurement of large-scale quantum machines.
