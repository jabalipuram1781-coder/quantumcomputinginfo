# UK National Physical Laboratory Secures £230,000 ($307.5K USD) GOTT Grant to Scale Quantum Ion-Trap Manufacturing

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/uk-national-physical-laboratory-secures-230000-307-5k-usd-gott-grant-to-scale-quantum-ion-trap-manufacturing/) on October 22, 2026.

The National Physical Laboratory (NPL) in the UK has received a £230,000 ($307.5K USD) grant from the Government Office for Technology Transfer (GOTT). This funding will be used to accelerate the commercial development and scalable manufacturing of trapped-ion quantum computing hardware. The grant aims to improve fabrication yield and packaging processes for ion traps, crucial for scaling quantum computing systems.
