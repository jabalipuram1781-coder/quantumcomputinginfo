# University of Pennsylvania Team Demonstrates On-Chip Photonic Quantum Skyrmion Transfer in Nature Physics

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/university-of-pennsylvania-team-demonstrates-on-chip-photonic-quantum-skyrmion-transfer-in-nature-physics/) on October 13, 2026.

A University of Pennsylvania-led team has successfully demonstrated the on-chip transfer of photonic quantum skyrmions, topological light structures, from single photons to entangled states. This was achieved using integrated nanophotonic chips and established a noise-resilient quantum state transfer mechanism. The research, published in Nature Physics, shows that the skyrmion number, a robust quantum observable, remains intact even when entanglement vanishes, offering a new approach for noise-tolerant quantum optical interconnects.
