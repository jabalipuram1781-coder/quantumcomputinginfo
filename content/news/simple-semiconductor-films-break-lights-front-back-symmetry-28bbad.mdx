# Simple semiconductor films break light's front-back symmetry

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-07-simple-semiconductor-front-symmetry.html) on July 27, 2026.

Light typically interacts with a material the same way whether it enters through the front or the back—like polarized sunglasses that work the same from either side. Cornell researchers have demonstrated a simple route to breaking that symmetry, opening new possibilities for photonics and quantum information processing.
