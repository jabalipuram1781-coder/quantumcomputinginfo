# Florida Atlantic University Receives NIST Grant to Build South Florida Quantum Cybersecurity Workforce Ecosystem

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/florida-atlantic-university-receives-nist-grant-to-build-south-florida-quantum-cybersecurity-workforce-ecosystem/) on October 6, 2026.

Florida Atlantic University (FAU) received a $200,000 NIST grant to establish a quantum cybersecurity workforce consortium in South Florida. This initiative, in collaboration with D-Wave Quantum Inc., will focus on integrating quantum computing, cybersecurity, and critical energy infrastructure protection. The program will utilize FAU's new D-Wave Advantage2 quantum system and aims to train a workforce to secure critical infrastructure against emerging post-quantum threats.
