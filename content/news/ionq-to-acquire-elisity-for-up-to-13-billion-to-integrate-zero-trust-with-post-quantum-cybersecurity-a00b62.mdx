# IonQ to Acquire Elisity for Up to $1.3 Billion to Integrate Zero Trust with Post-Quantum Cybersecurity

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/ionq-to-acquire-elisity-for-up-to-1-3-billion-to-integrate-zero-trust-with-post-quantum-cybersecurity/) on October 12, 2026.

IonQ is set to acquire Elisity for up to $1.3 billion in an all-stock deal, integrating Elisity's Zero Trust cybersecurity with IonQ's post-quantum cryptography. This acquisition, expected to close in Q4 2026, aims to enhance IonQ's security offerings, addressing "harvest now, decrypt later" vulnerabilities and federal PQC mandates. Elisity will operate as a subsidiary, retaining its brand and management.
