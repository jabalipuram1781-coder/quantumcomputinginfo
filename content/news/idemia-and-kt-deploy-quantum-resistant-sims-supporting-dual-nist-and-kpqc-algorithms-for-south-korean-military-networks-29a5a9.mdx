# IDEMIA and KT Deploy Quantum-Resistant SIMs Supporting Dual NIST and KpqC Algorithms for South Korean Military Networks

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/idemia-and-kt-deploy-quantum-resistant-sims-supporting-dual-nist-and-kpqc-algorithms-for-south-korean-military-networks/) on October 27, 2026.

IDEMIA Secure Transactions and KT Corporation are deploying quantum-resistant SIMs in South Korea's military networks, supporting both NIST and Korean Post-Quantum Cryptography (KpqC) algorithms. This initiative, part of South Korea's 2026 Post-Quantum Cryptography Pilot Conversion Project, aims to enhance crypto-agility and secure military communications against future quantum threats. The SIMs act as a hardware root of trust, offloading intensive cryptographic handshakes from host devices and ensuring operational resilience across various military applications.
