# Graphene measurements reveal energy-loss and quantum-coherence exponents diverge under gate control

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-graphene-reveal-energy-loss-quantum.html) on September 23, 2026.

Scientists have shown that the two exponents (inelastic scattering exponent and dephasing exponent) commonly used to describe electron scattering in graphene do not necessarily follow the same behavior. Using gated epitaxial graphene, a multi-institutional team of researchers independently extracted the two exponents through current-heating measurements and weak-localization analysis. The contrasting gate-voltage dependence provides evidence that energy relaxation and loss of quantum phase coherence can be governed by different microscopic processes....
