# Google Quantum AI Launches $150K Kaggle Competition to Compress Fault-Tolerant Quantum Circuits Using AI Agents

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/google-quantum-ai-launches-150k-kaggle-competition-to-compress-fault-tolerant-quantum-circuits-using-ai-agents/) on October 23, 2026.

Google Quantum AI has launched a $150,000 Kaggle competition running from October 22, 2026, to January 20, 2027. The competition challenges participants to use AI agents to compress fault-tolerant quantum circuits, focusing on "kickmix" circuits which are verifiable by classical computers. This initiative aims to optimize quantum algorithms for applications in fusion energy, optimization, and quantum chemistry.
