# Quantum sensor could measure how Earth drags spacetime

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-quantum-sensor-earth-spacetime.html) on October 15, 2026.

Einstein's theory of general relativity describes gravity as the bending of spacetime, a mathematical model that combines three-dimensional space and time into a single, four-dimensional continuum. The theory also predicts that a rotating object, such as Earth, slightly drags the surrounding spacetime as it rotates, a phenomenon known as frame-dragging.
