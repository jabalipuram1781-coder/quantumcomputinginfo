# IonQ and Synopsys Accelerate CAE Computer-Aided Engineering Workloads by 14.6% via Trapped-Ion Hardware

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/ionq-and-synopsys-accelerate-cae-computer-aided-engineering-workloads-by-14-6-via-trapped-ion-hardware/) on September 17, 2026.

IonQ, Synopsys, and NVIDIA have successfully demonstrated quantum-accelerated performance gains in large-scale Computer-Aided Engineering (CAE) workflows, reducing total execution time by up to 14.6%. Their joint research, awarded 1st Place Best Paper at IEEE Quantum Week 2026, utilized a quantum matrix reordering algorithm embedded in classical simulation suites like Ansys LS-DYNA. This innovation optimizes matrix equations, leading to significant time and memory savings in industrial design pipelines and offering substantial reductions in HPC energy consumption and compute costs.
