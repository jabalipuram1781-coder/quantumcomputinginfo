# Physicists help uncover ''spooky'' quantum effect in the Large Hadron Collider

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-physicists-uncover-spooky-quantum-effect.html) on September 14, 2026.

Physicists at the University of Oxford have helped confirm that one of the strangest phenomena in physics—quantum entanglement—occurs even among some of the heaviest and most fleeting particles ever created. The discovery, made using the world's most powerful particle collider at CERN, has been published in Physical Review Letters.
