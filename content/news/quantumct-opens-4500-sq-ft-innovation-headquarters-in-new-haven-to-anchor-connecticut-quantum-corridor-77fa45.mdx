# QuantumCT Opens 4,500 Sq. Ft. Innovation Headquarters in New Haven to Anchor Connecticut Quantum Corridor

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantumct-opens-4500-sq-ft-innovation-headquarters-in-new-haven-to-anchor-connecticut-quantum-corridor/) on October 7, 2026.

QuantumCT has opened its 4,500-square-foot innovation headquarters in New Haven, serving as a central hub for quantum computing research, talent development, and startup incubation. This initiative, backed by significant public and private funding, aims to establish Connecticut as a leading U.S. quantum commercialization center. Key funding includes a $50.5 million state allocation and a $15 million NSF grant, alongside institutional research from Yale and UConn, and industry investment from D-Wave.
