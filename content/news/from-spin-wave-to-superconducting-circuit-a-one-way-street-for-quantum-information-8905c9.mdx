# From spin wave to superconducting circuit: A one-way street for quantum information

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-superconducting-circuit-street-quantum.html) on October 14, 2026.

Quantum information systems could one day shape everyday life through faster computing, sharper sensing and more secure communication. A new result from the U.S. Department of Energy's (DOE) Argonne National Laboratory and the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (U. of I.) marks an important step toward enabling magnetic spins—a quantum property of matter that can be used to store and manipulate information—to play a role in the future of quantum information processing.
