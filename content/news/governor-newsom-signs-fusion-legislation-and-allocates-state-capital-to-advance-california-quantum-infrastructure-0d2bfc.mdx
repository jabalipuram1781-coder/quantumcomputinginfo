# Governor Newsom Signs Fusion Legislation and Allocates State Capital to Advance California Quantum Infrastructure

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/governor-newsom-signs-fusion-legislation-and-allocates-state-capital-to-advance-california-quantum-infrastructure/) on October 1, 2026.

California Governor Gavin Newsom has signed legislation and allocated $30 million for quantum and deep-space research to advance the state's technology infrastructure. This initiative, known as Quantum California, supports university research, startup incubators, and hardware supply chains. Key legislation includes bills to commercialize fusion energy and integrate advanced nuclear technologies, along with a proposed $7.5 billion bond measure for research funding.
