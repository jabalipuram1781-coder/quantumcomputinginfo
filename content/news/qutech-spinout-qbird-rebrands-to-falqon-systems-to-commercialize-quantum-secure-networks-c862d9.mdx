# QuTech Spinout Q*Bird Rebrands to Falqon Systems to Commercialize Quantum Secure Networks

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/qutech-spinout-qbird-rebrands-to-falqon-systems-to-commercialize-quantum-secure-networks/) on September 17, 2026.

Q*Bird has rebranded to Falqon Systems, signaling its expansion from hardware manufacturing to deploying end-to-end Quantum Secure Networks (QSN). The company, a spinout from QuTech and TU Delft, will continue with its existing leadership and technological roadmaps, focusing on its integrated platform built upon Measurement-Device-Independent Quantum Key Distribution (MDI-QKD) for enhanced security and scalability in quantum communication. Falqon Systems currently operates across several European countries, aligning with the European Quantum Communication Infrastructure (EuroQCI) initiative.
