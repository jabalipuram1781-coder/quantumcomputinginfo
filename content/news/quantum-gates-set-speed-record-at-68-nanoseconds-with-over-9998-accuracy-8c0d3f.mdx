# Quantum gates set speed record at 6.8 nanoseconds with over 99.98% accuracy

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-quantum-gates-nanoseconds-accuracy.html) on October 27, 2026.

Researchers from Jülich, Aachen and Cologne have performed a quantum operation on a qubit in just 6.8 nanoseconds—with more than 99.98% accuracy. Such operations typically take several tens of nanoseconds in superconducting quantum computers. The paper was published in Nature Communications.
