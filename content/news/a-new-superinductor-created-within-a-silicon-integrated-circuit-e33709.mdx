# A new superinductor created within a silicon integrated circuit

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-superinductor-silicon-circuit.html) on November 4, 2026.

A recent study published in Nature Electronics and carried out by researchers from nanoGUNE's Quantum Hardware research group, Quantum Motion, London Center for Nanotechnology, Imperial College London and the University of Cambridge introduces a new superinductor created within a silicon integrated circuit fabricated in a commercial complementary metal–oxide–semiconductor (CMOS) process.
