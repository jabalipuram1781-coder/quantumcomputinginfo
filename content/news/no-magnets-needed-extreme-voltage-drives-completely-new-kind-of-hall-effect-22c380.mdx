# No magnets needed: Extreme voltage drives completely new kind of Hall effect

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-magnets-extreme-voltage-kind-hall.html) on November 23, 2026.

The Hall effect, a physical phenomenon in which a current arises perpendicular to a voltage applied across a material, has been of particular interest to physicists since it was discovered more than a century ago. Over time, physicists have broadened their understanding of the Hall effect, describing quantum and anomalous versions that appear under nonclassical conditions. Recently, Rice University's Qimiao Si published a paper in Physical Review Letters describing a new variant called the fully nonequilibrium response that once again expands how physicists understand the Hall effect....
