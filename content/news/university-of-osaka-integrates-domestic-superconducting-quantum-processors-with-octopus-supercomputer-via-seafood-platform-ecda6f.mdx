# University of Osaka Integrates Domestic Superconducting Quantum Processors with OCTOPUS Supercomputer via SEAFOOD Platform

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/university-of-osaka-integrates-domestic-superconducting-quantum-processors-with-octopus-supercomputer-via-seafood-platform/) on November 14, 2026.

The University of Osaka has launched SEAFOOD, a hybrid computing platform integrating its OCTOPUS supercomputer with domestic superconducting quantum processors. This platform, the first of its kind in Japan, aims to tackle complex computational problems in fields like drug discovery and materials engineering by distributing workloads across classical and quantum systems. The project, supported by various Japanese initiatives, will offer trial access to research teams in 2027.
