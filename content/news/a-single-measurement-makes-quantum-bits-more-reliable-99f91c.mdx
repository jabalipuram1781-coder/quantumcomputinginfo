# A single measurement makes quantum bits more reliable

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-quantum-bits-reliable.html) on November 3, 2026.

Researchers at the University of Regensburg and their international partners have found a way to significantly reduce errors in quantum bits. In an experiment, they demonstrated that a single additional measurement before a series of quantum operations can cut the error rate by more than half. The results have been published in Applied Physics Reviews.
