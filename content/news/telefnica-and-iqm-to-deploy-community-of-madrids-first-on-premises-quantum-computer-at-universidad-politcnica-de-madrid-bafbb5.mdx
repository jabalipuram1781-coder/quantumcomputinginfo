# Telefónica and IQM to Deploy Community of Madrid’s First On-Premises Quantum Computer at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/telefonica-and-iqm-to-deploy-community-of-madrids-first-on-premises-quantum-computer-at-universidad-politecnica-de-madrid/) on October 23, 2026.

Telefónica and IQM Quantum Computers are deploying the first quantum computer in the Community of Madrid, an IQM Spark system at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid (UPM) by late 2026. This is their third quantum processor in Spain, aiming to provide local access to quantum hardware for research and education. Telefónica is also expanding its quantum technology portfolio, including QKD networks and post-quantum cryptography.
