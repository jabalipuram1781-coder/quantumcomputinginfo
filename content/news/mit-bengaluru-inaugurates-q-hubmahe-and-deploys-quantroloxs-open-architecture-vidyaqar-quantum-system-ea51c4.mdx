# MIT Bengaluru Inaugurates Q-HUB@MAHE and Deploys QuantrolOx’s Open-Architecture VIDYAQAR Quantum System

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/mit-bengaluru-inaugurates-q-hubmahe-and-deploys-quantroloxs-open-architecture-vidyaqar-quantum-system/) on October 17, 2026.

Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT Bengaluru) has inaugurated the Manipal House of Quantum (Q-HUB@MAHE) in Bengaluru, India, on October 13, 2026. This facility features QuantrolOx’s VIDYAQAR open-architecture quantum system, a 25-qubit-capable dilution refrigeration platform that is part of a national roadmap to scale to over 1,000 qubits. The initiative aims to train 100 quantum engineers by December 2026 and allows researchers to test and benchmark quantum components using custom Python workflows.
