# Quantum teleportation method uses weaker entanglement to transfer encoded information

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-quantum-teleportation-method-weaker-entanglement.html) on November 16, 2026.

Over the past several decades, researchers have demonstrated quantum teleportation, the transfer of one quantum system's state to another, in a range of systems. However, many quantum computers encode logical qubits (units of quantum information) across groups of physical qubits, which makes teleporting the encoded state without losing information more challenging.
