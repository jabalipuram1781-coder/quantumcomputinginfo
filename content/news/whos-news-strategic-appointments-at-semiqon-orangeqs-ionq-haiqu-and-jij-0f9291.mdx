This article announces several strategic appointments in the quantum technology sector. SemiQon appointed Dr. Thierry Botter as CCO, OrangeQS named Raj Badoni as CCO, and IonQ expanded its finance leadership with Gary Merrill as CFO and Thomas A. Austin as CAO. Additionally, Haiqu appointed Kortny Rolston-Duce as Head of Ecosystem Development and Marketing, and Jij named Oren Bernstein as VP of Business Development for North America.
