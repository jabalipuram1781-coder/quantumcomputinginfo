# Optical experiment demonstrates the strong Mpemba effect in a single qubit

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-optical-strong-mpemba-effect-qubit.html) on November 9, 2026.

Typically, after a physical system is disturbed, it can gradually return toward a state of thermal equilibrium by exchanging heat with its surroundings. Thermal equilibrium is a state in which there is no net transfer of heat between the system and its surroundings.
