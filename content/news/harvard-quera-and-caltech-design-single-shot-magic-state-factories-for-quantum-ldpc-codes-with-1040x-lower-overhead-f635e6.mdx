# Harvard, QuEra, and Caltech Design Single-Shot Magic State Factories for Quantum LDPC Codes with 10–40x Lower Overhead

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/harvard-quera-and-caltech-design-single-shot-magic-state-factories-for-quantum-ldpc-codes-with-10-40x-lower-overhead/) on November 21, 2026.

Researchers from Harvard, QuEra, and Caltech have developed a new fault-tolerant quantum magic state factory architecture for qLDPC codes. This new design reduces space-time resource costs by 10x to 40x compared to existing methods. The factory utilizes 3D and 4D balanced-product codes and can achieve logical error rates as low as 10^-9.
