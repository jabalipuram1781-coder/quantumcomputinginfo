# QuEra Computing Joins Maryland’s Capital of Quantum Initiative and Opens Facility in Discovery District

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quera-computing-joins-marylands-capital-of-quantum-initiative-and-opens-facility-in-discovery-district/) on September 23, 2026.

QuEra Computing has opened an office in Maryland's Discovery District, joining the Capital of Quantum initiative. This expansion includes a partnership with UMD's QLab, providing cloud access to QuEra's 256-qubit Aquila neutral-atom quantum computer.
