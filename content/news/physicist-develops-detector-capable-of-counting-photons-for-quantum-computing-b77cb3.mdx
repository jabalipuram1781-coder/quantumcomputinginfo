# Physicist develops detector capable of counting photons for quantum computing

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-physicist-detector-capable-photons-quantum.html) on November 5, 2026.

Detecting a single photon is a challenge in itself. But what if you also want to know exactly how many photons arrive at a particular point at the same time? Physicist Erik Baalbergen is working on light detectors that can do just that. They form a key building block for the optical quantum computer of the future.
