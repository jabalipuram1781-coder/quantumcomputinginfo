# Roadrunner Quantum Lab Opens 35,000-Square-Foot Albuquerque Facility with Initial Cohort of Quantum Startups, National Labs, and Ecosystem Partners

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/roadrunner-quantum-lab-opens-35000-square-foot-albuquerque-facility-with-initial-cohort-of-quantum-startups-national-labs-and-ecosystem-partners/) on September 30, 2026.

The Roadrunner Quantum Lab (RQL) has opened a 35,000-square-foot facility in Albuquerque, New Mexico, serving as a hub for quantum technology testing and commercialization. Supported by $25 million in state funding, it houses 15 quantum hardware, software, sensing, and capital partners, facilitating the transition of quantum physics research into commercial hardware. The RQL integrates various quantum technologies and provides access to national lab scientists, aiming to be a key launchpad for utility-scale quantum hardware commercialization.
