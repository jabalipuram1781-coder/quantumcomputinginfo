# D-Wave Demonstrates 3D Analog-Digital Quantum Simulation of Higher-Order Topological Insulators Using 728 Qubits

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/d-wave-demonstrates-3d-analog-digital-quantum-simulation-of-higher-order-topological-insulators-using-728-qubits/) on October 17, 2026.

D-Wave has successfully demonstrated 3D analog-digital quantum simulation of higher-order topological insulators using up to 728 qubits on an Advantage2 superconducting quantum annealing processor. This achievement, detailed in a research study on arXiv, involved new analog-digital quantum control protocols to measure localized boundary modes and extract 3D bulk topological invariants. This breakthrough confirms the utility of analog-digital quantum annealing hardware as a programmable simulator for condensed matter physics and topological materials.
