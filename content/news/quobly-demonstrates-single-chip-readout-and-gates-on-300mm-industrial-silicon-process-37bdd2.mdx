# Quobly Demonstrates Single-Chip Readout and Gates on 300mm Industrial Silicon Process

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quobly-demonstrates-single-chip-readout-and-gates-on-300mm-industrial-silicon-process/) on September 16, 2026.

Quobly has successfully demonstrated single-chip execution of qubit readout, single-qubit gates, and two-qubit gates on its QSOI® architecture, fabricated on an industrial 300mm silicon process at STMicroelectronics. This achievement validates their technology transfer strategy, allowing co-integration of quantum and classical circuitry. With plans for cloud access to their Alloy Pioneer system by late 2026 and a long-term target of one million qubits by 2032, Quobly is advancing its commercial and scaling roadmap, supported by significant Series A funding and strategic partnerships.
