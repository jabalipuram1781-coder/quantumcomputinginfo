# Scientists uncover recurrent patterns within chaotic quantum behavior

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-scientists-uncover-recurrent-patterns-chaotic.html) on October 5, 2026.

Many complex quantum systems rapidly lose the recognizable patterns of their initial states as their components interact. To describe patterns of regular and chaotic motion in specific systems, physicists can construct a mathematical map called an effective phase space.
