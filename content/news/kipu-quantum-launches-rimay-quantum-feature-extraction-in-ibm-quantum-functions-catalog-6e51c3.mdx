# Kipu Quantum Launches Rimay Quantum Feature Extraction in IBM Quantum Functions Catalog

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/kipu-quantum-launches-rimay-quantum-feature-extraction-in-ibm-quantum-functions-catalog/) on November 13, 2026.

Kipu Quantum has launched its Rimay quantum feature extraction service on the IBM Quantum Functions Catalog, allowing IBM Quantum users to access quantum-enhanced machine learning for feature engineering. This service, which operates as a preprocessing layer for classical machine learning models, has demonstrated improved recall and AUC scores in credit risk classification. It's designed to provide enriched data matrices for use with standard frameworks like scikit-learn.
