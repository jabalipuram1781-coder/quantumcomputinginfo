# Quandela and Quanova Partner to Deploy Vietnam’s First Quantum Computer at Quang Trung University

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quandela-and-quanova-partner-to-deploy-vietnams-first-quantum-computer-at-quang-trung-university/) on October 23, 2026.

Quandela and Quanova are partnering to install Vietnam's first quantum computer, a Quandela MosaiQ photonic system, at Quang Trung University. This collaboration, supported by Nam A Bank, will establish a Quantum Research, Innovation, Service, and Entrepreneurship (quantum-RISE) program and a Quantum Innovator Residency Program, marking the first France-Vietnam quantum computing collaboration. The initiative aims to provide Vietnamese researchers and students with direct access to quantum computing and foster local talent in the field.
