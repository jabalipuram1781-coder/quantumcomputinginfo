# IBM quantum computer solves problem classical computers can’t in just 15 minutes

> **Source:** Originally published on [ScienceDaily Quantum Computers](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/11/261123231244.htm) on November 24, 2026.

IBM and University of Chicago researchers have demonstrated that a quantum computer can complete an extraordinarily complex calculation in just 15 minutes, beyond the practical reach of leading classical computing methods. A novel error-detection technique also allows scientists to establish confidence in the accuracy of the results, overcoming a major hurdle in quantum computing.
