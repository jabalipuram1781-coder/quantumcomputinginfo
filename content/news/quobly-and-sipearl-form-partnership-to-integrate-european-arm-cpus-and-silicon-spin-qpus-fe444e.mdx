# Quobly and SiPearl Form Partnership to Integrate European ARM CPUs and Silicon Spin QPUs

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quobly-and-sipearl-form-partnership-to-integrate-european-arm-cpus-and-silicon-spin-qpus/) on October 9, 2026.

Quobly and SiPearl have partnered to integrate SiPearl's ARM-based Rhea1 CPU with Quobly's silicon QPU hardware, aiming for hybrid CPU-QPU co-design and HPC-quantum workload optimization. This collaboration will establish a European-sourced hardware pipeline connecting supercomputing nodes with quantum coprocessors. The companies plan to seek joint European funding to advance sovereign HPC-quantum infrastructure in Europe.
