# IonQ and Classiq Outline Architectural Trade-Offs in Fault-Tolerant Resource Estimation

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/ionq-and-classiq-outline-architectural-trade-offs-in-fault-tolerant-resource-estimation/) on November 9, 2026.

IonQ and Classiq's analysis shows that minimizing non-Clifford gates is no longer the primary factor in fault-tolerant quantum computing performance. Advances in magic state preparation mean that routing, Clifford gates, and memory retention now significantly impact the overall physical spacetime volume. Therefore, a full-stack compilation approach considering physical layouts and error correction is crucial for accurate evaluation of fault-tolerant algorithms.
