# EPFL Demonstrates Millimeter-Wave-to-Optical Transducer with 67% Internal Efficiency

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/epfl-demonstrates-millimeter-wave-to-optical-transducer-with-67-internal-efficiency/) on November 10, 2026.

Researchers at EPFL have developed a millimeter-wave-to-optical transducer with 67% internal efficiency. This device uses a thin-film lithium niobate photonic molecule coupled to a metallic resonator to bridge the gap between millimeter-wave and telecom optical domains. This advancement is crucial for distributed quantum architectures, enabling coherent frequency conversion between high-frequency electric fields and optical carriers without thermal load.
