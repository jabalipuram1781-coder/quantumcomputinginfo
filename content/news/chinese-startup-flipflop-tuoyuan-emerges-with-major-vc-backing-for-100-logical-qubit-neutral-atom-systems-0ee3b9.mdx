# Chinese Startup FlipFlop Tuoyuan Emerges with Major VC Backing for 100-Logical-Qubit Neutral-Atom Systems

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/chinese-startup-flipflop-tuoyuan-emerges-with-major-vc-backing-for-100-logical-qubit-neutral-atom-systems/) on October 19, 2026.

FlipFlop Tuoyuan, a Chinese quantum hardware startup, has secured over $14 million in early-stage funding to develop neutral-atom quantum processing units. Founded in July 2026 by Dr. Qiao Mu, the company aims to build a 100-logical-qubit fault-tolerant quantum computer and utilize AI for experimental design and hardware control.
