# Fujitsu and Yaqumo Begin Hardware Validation of STAR Architecture on Neutral-Atom QPUs

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/fujitsu-and-yaqumo-begin-hardware-validation-of-star-architecture-on-neutral-atom-qpus/) on September 10, 2026.

Fujitsu and Yaqumo have partnered to experimentally validate Fujitsu's STAR architecture on Yaqumo's neutral-atom quantum processors. This collaboration aims to reduce physical qubit overhead and improve quantum error correction by leveraging the strengths of neutral-atom systems. They are also integrating Fujitsu's Open Quantum Toolchain to enable external users to access diverse hardware modalities through a unified cloud interface.
