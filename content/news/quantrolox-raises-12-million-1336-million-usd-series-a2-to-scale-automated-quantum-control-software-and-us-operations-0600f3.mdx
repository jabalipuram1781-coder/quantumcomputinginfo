# QuantrolOx Raises €12 Million ($13.36 Million USD) Series A2 to Scale Automated Quantum Control Software and U.S. Operations

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantrolox-raises-e12-million-13-36-million-usd-series-a2-to-scale-automated-quantum-control-software-and-u-s-operations/) on October 27, 2026.

QuantrolOx has secured €12 million ($13.36 million USD) in Series A2 funding, bringing its total funding to approximately €28 million. The company, which specializes in automated quantum control software, will use the investment to expand commercial deployments of its Quantum EDGE™ software, grow its educational offerings, and strengthen its North American presence. This funding will help QuantrolOx address the challenges of manual calibration in quantum systems by automating the process with machine-learning-driven solutions.
