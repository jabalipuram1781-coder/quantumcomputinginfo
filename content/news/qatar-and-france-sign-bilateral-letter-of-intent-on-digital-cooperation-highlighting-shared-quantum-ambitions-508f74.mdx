# Qatar and France Sign Bilateral Letter of Intent on Digital Cooperation Highlighting Shared Quantum Ambitions

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/qatar-and-france-sign-bilateral-letter-of-intent-on-digital-cooperation-highlighting-shared-quantum-ambitions/) on November 18, 2026.

Qatar and France have signed a Letter of Intent to enhance cooperation in AI, digital transformation, and quantum technologies. This agreement, formalized at the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference 2026, aims to strengthen their digital sectors and foster strategic autonomy. It also builds on existing commercial ties, including a recent deal for quantum computing deployment in Qatar by French company Quandela.
