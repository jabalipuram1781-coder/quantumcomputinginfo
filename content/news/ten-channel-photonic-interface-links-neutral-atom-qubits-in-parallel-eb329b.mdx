# Ten-channel photonic interface links neutral-atom qubits in parallel

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-ten-channel-photonic-interface-links.html) on September 1, 2026.

A research group in Japan has demonstrated a world-record 10-channel multiplexed quantum photonic interface based on an integrated waveguide array, a key technology for optically interconnecting multiple quantum computers. The study is published in the journal Optica.
