# Individual atomic collisions can now be detected in the lab, potentially opening up pathways to new physics

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-individual-atomic-collisions-lab-potentially.html) on October 20, 2026.

Tiny atoms and molecules are constantly bombarding each other all around us. While we regularly measure the large-scale effects of these interactions, measuring the effect of each individual collision is far more difficult. But now, researchers have managed to measure how tiny gas particles affect a single silica particle in the lab. In their new study, published in Physical Review Letters, the team describes how they detected the momentum transfers of individual collisions of gas particles with a nanoparticle levitated in laser light....
