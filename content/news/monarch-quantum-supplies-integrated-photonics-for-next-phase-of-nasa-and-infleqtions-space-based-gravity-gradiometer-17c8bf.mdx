# Monarch Quantum Supplies Integrated Photonics for Next Phase of NASA and Infleqtion’s Space-Based Gravity Gradiometer

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/monarch-quantum-supplies-integrated-photonics-for-next-phase-of-nasa-and-infleqtions-space-based-gravity-gradiometer/) on October 24, 2026.

Monarch Quantum is providing integrated photonics hardware to Infleqtion for NASA's Quantum Gravity Gradiometer Pathfinder (QGGPf) mission. This mission aims to deploy the world's first space-based Earth quantum gravity gradient sensor in low-Earth orbit by 2030, measuring gravitational variations using ultracold rubidium atoms to track environmental changes. Monarch Quantum's role involves supplying compact photonics subsystems crucial for the sensor's operation in space.
