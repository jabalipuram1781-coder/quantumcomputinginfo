> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/chicago-state-university-launches-cquest-center-to-anchor-south-side-quantum-workforce-pipeline/) on September 11, 2026.

Chicago State University (CSU) has launched the CQuEST Center, a research and workforce hub on Chicago's South Side, unifying its quantum and semiconductor credentials. The center offers academic pathways, including a QISE minor and certificates, providing students with access to research facilities, internships, and job opportunities while also engaging in regional STEM outreach.
