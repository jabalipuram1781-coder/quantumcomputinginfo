# German Aerospace Center (DLR) Researchers Demonstrate Low-Cost Benchmarked-Noise Error Mitigation Across 100-Qubit IBM and IQM Processors

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/german-aerospace-center-dlr-researchers-demonstrate-low-cost-benchmarked-noise-error-mitigation-across-100-qubit-ibm-and-iqm-processors/) on November 13, 2026.

Researchers at the German Aerospace Center (DLR) have developed an improved quantum error mitigation protocol, randomized benchmarked-noise zero-noise extrapolation (bnZNE). This method enhances expectation-value accuracy without increasing measurement shot budgets, validated on 100-qubit IBM and IQM processors. The bnZNE protocol incorporates Pauli twirling, dynamical decoupling, and twirled readout error extinction (TREX), outperforming standard zero-noise extrapolation across various benchmarks.
