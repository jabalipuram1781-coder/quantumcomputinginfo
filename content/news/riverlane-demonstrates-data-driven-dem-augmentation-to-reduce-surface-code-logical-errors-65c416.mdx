# Riverlane Demonstrates Data-Driven DEM Augmentation to Reduce Surface-Code Logical Errors

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/riverlane-demonstrates-data-driven-dem-augmentation-to-reduce-surface-code-logical-errors/) on November 8, 2026.

Riverlane has developed a data-driven method to improve quantum error correction in surface-code architectures. By identifying unmodeled correlated error mechanisms from syndrome measurement data, their technique augments standard Detector Error Models (DEMs). This approach has shown significant reductions in logical error rates, up to 75% in simulations and 14% on physical hardware.
