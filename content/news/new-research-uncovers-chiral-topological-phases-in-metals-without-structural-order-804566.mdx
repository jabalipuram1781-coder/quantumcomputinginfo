# New research uncovers chiral topological phases in metals without structural order

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-uncovers-chiral-topological-phases-metals.html) on November 4, 2026.

Fermions are among the most important particles in physics and include both everyday electrons and more exotic variants. Chiral fermions, like gloves, come in left- and right-handed versions and were proposed over a century ago. While high-energy neutrinos—the leading candidates for massless chiral fermions—proved to have mass, nearly (semi-) metallic solid-state crystals were discovered 15 years ago to host electronic versions of massless chiral fermions.
