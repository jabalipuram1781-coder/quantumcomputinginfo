# Coherence Quantum Launches with €2.78 Million ($3.1 Million USD) Seed Round to Scale Cryogenic Quantum Control

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/coherence-quantum-launches-with-e2-78-million-3-1-million-usd-seed-round-to-scale-cryogenic-quantum-control/) on October 21, 2026.

Coherence Quantum has launched with €2.78 million in seed funding to develop cryogenic quantum control infrastructure for solid-state quantum processors. The company aims to overcome thermal, wiring, and density bottlenecks in large-scale quantum computers by creating modular, low-noise signal-conditioning components and high-density interfaces. This funding will be used to establish a lab, accelerate prototype fabrication, and expand their engineering team in Espoo, Finland.
