# IonQ Announces Pricing of $2.0 Billion Equity Offering Purchased by Heights Capital Management

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/ionq-announces-pricing-of-2-0-billion-equity-offering-purchased-by-heights-capital-management/) on October 14, 2026.

IonQ announced a $2.0 billion equity offering purchased by Heights Capital Management, consisting of common stock and pre-funded warrants priced at a 15% premium. The deal also includes seven-year warrants for additional shares at a 100% premium. IonQ plans to use the capital to accelerate the commercialization and deployment of its quantum platform.
