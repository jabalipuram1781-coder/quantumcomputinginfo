# IBM, Fermilab, and NVIDIA Unveil Modular Quantum Co-Design Platform, Slurm HRES Integration, and GPU QEC Tools at SC26

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/ibm-fermilab-and-nvidia-unveil-modular-quantum-co-design-platform-slurm-hres-integration-and-gpu-qec-tools-at-sc26/) on November 16, 2026.

At SC26, IBM, Fermilab, and NVIDIA unveiled a modular quantum-classical co-design platform, integrating GPUs with quantum processors. They also announced native quantum resource scheduling in Slurm 26.11 and new GPU-accelerated quantum error-correction (QEC) software, including the TCBP-JOBS algorithm and CUDA-Q QEC 0.9.0. These advancements aim to improve quantum computing workflows, error correction, and resource management in supercomputing environments.
