# UTM Technovation Park Becomes R&D Base for AQSolotl, Malaysia’s First Quantum Computing Hardware Company

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/utm-technovation-park-becomes-rd-base-for-aqsolotl-malaysias-first-quantum-computing-hardware-company/) on September 25, 2026.

AQSolotl, Malaysia's first quantum computing hardware company, has established its R&D operations at UTM Technovation Park in Johor Bahru. This Singapore-based startup will focus on designing and producing high-precision quantum computer control electronics, leveraging UTM's research programs and creating a cross-border talent and commercialization corridor between Malaysia and Singapore. The company's CTO, Dr. Yap Yung Szen, is a UTM researcher recognized in UNESCO’s global Quantum 100 initiative.
