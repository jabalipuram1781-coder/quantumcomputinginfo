# UK and Singapore Align on Post-Quantum Cryptography Migration and Quantum Security Standards in Financial Sector

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/uk-and-singapore-align-on-post-quantum-cryptography-migration-and-quantum-security-standards-in-financial-sector/) on October 24, 2026.

During the 11th UK-Singapore Financial Dialogue in October 2026, both nations agreed to strengthen cooperation on quantum security in the financial sector. They plan to harmonize post-quantum cryptography (PQC) migration roadmaps and share insights from Singapore's Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) sandbox. The UK and Singapore will also use their 2027 G20 Presidency and ASEAN Chairmanship, respectively, to promote international quantum cyber resilience standards.
