# Quantum observers can ''agree to disagree,'' but within limits, new theorem shows

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-quantum-limits-theorem.html) on November 17, 2026.

Two observers can start with the same description of a quantum system, follow the same probability rules and still disagree about the chance of the same outcome—even while being certain of each other's assessments.
