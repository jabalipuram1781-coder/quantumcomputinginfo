# Pasqal Transitions €50 Million Q-PLANET Pilot Line into Execution Phase for European Neutral-Atom Chips

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/pasqal-transitions-e50-million-q-planet-pilot-line-into-execution-phase-for-european-neutral-atom-chips/) on October 6, 2026.

Pasqal has launched the operational phase of Q-PLANET, a €50 million initiative to develop a European supply chain for neutral-atom quantum computing chips. Leading a 28-partner consortium, Pasqal aims to industrialize chip-scale components, including integrated laser sources and atom chips, over three years. This project will standardize manufacturing processes to accelerate the deployment of quantum technologies in Europe.
