# PsiQuantum Unveils 8×8 Optical Circuit Switch Demonstration Vehicle Derived from Silicon Photonics Quantum Architecture

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/psiquantum-unveils-8x8-optical-circuit-switch-demonstration-vehicle-derived-from-silicon-photonics-quantum-architecture/) on October 28, 2026.

PsiQuantum has developed an 8x8 optical circuit switch (OCS) using their silicon photonics platform, originally for quantum computing, but now repurposed for AI cluster interconnects. This OCS offers low insertion loss and high channel isolation. This dual-use strategy allows PsiQuantum to commercialize its photonic manufacturing while continuing to develop its quantum computing technology.
