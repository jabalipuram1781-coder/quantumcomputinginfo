# NVIDIA Commits $1 Billion over Five Years to Accelerate U.S. Scientific Computing and Quantum Infrastructure

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/nvidia-commits-1-billion-over-five-years-to-accelerate-u-s-scientific-computing-and-quantum-infrastructure/) on October 8, 2026.

NVIDIA is investing $1 billion over five years to advance U.S. scientific computing and quantum infrastructure. This commitment supports research institutions, cloud providers, and national laboratories, focusing on hybrid quantum-classical hardware integration, fault-tolerant quantum software, fusion energy, and microelectronics design. A key aspect is expanding access to high-performance computing and NVIDIA's open-source CUDA-Q platform for quantum processor simulation and control.
