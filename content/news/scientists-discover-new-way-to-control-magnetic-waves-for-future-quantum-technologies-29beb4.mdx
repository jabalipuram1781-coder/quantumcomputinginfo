# Scientists discover new way to control magnetic waves for future quantum technologies

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-scientists-magnetic-future-quantum-technologies.html) on November 10, 2026.

Researchers at the U.S. Department of Energy's (DOE) Ames National Laboratory have discovered a way to control the handedness of magnons, tiny magnetic waves that can carry information through materials. By changing the material's magnetic state with an external magnetic field, they were able to switch magnon behavior, a finding with potential applications in quantum information and quantum computing technologies. Their findings are published in Physical Review Letters.
