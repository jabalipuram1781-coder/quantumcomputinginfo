# Scientists achieve record-breaking low temperature on China''s space station, paving the way for new physics experiments

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-scientists-temperature-china-space-station.html) on November 6, 2026.

Matter starts to behave differently at extremely low temperatures, creating conditions ideal for studying quantum physics and new states of matter. Now, researchers from China have achieved a new low-temperature record aboard their Tiangong space station. Their research, recently published in Science, might lead to a new generation of experiments in quantum physics and precision measurements in space.
