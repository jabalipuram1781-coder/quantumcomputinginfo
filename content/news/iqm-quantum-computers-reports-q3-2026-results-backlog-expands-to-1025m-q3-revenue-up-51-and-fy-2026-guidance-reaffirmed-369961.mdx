# IQM Quantum Computers Reports Q3 2026 Results: Backlog Expands to €102.5M, Q3 Revenue Up 51%, and FY 2026 Guidance Reaffirmed

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/iqm-quantum-computers-reports-q3-2026-results-backlog-expands-to-e102-5m-q3-revenue-up-51-and-fy-2026-guidance-reaffirmed/) on November 6, 2026.

IQM Quantum Computers reported strong Q3 2026 results with revenue up 50.7% to €8.86 million, and a record order intake of €42.2 million, expanding their backlog to €102.5 million. They finalized a €33 million contract with LUMI AI Factory, expanded into South America, and reaffirmed their full-year 2026 revenue guidance of €42 million to €47 million. The company also made significant advancements in hardware and error-correction roadmaps, demonstrating high qubit fidelities and real-time quantum error correction.
