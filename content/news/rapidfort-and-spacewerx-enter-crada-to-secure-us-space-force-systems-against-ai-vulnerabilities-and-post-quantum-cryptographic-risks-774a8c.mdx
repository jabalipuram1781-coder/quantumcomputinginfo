# RapidFort and SpaceWERX Enter CRADA to Secure U.S. Space Force Systems Against AI Vulnerabilities and Post-Quantum Cryptographic Risks

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/rapidfort-and-spacewerx-enter-crada-to-secure-u-s-space-force-systems-against-ai-vulnerabilities-and-post-quantum-cryptographic-risks/) on October 6, 2026.

RapidFort and SpaceWERX have partnered to secure U.S. Space Force systems against AI vulnerabilities and post-quantum cryptographic risks through a Cooperative Research and Development Agreement (CRADA). This collaboration will focus on mitigating AI software risks, accelerating Authority to Operate (ATO) approvals, and preparing for post-quantum cryptographic transitions. The partnership aims to deploy hardened, quantum-resilient software across mission-critical Space Force infrastructure.
