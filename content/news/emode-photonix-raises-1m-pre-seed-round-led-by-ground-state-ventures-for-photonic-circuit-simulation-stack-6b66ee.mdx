# EMode Photonix Raises $1M Pre-Seed Round Led by Ground State Ventures for Photonic Circuit Simulation Stack

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/emode-photonix-raises-1m-pre-seed-round-led-by-ground-state-ventures-for-photonic-circuit-simulation-stack/) on October 16, 2026.

EMode Photonix, a quantum integrated photonics software startup, has successfully raised $1 million in pre-seed funding. This investment, led by Ground State Ventures, will enable the company to expand its engineering team and accelerate the development of its enterprise software. EMode Photonix specializes in a multi-physics simulation platform for non-linear integrated photonic circuits, offering significantly faster modeling capabilities for various applications including quantum computing and optical sensing.
