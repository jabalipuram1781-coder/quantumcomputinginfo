# NVision Demonstrates Addressable Molecular Spin-Photon Interface and Partners with HyperLight for Integrated Photonic Hardware

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/nvision-demonstrates-addressable-molecular-spin-photon-interface-and-partners-with-hyperlight-for-integrated-photonic-hardware/) on October 28, 2026.

NVision Quantum Technologies has demonstrated a new quantum computing platform using organic carbene molecules as qubits, achieving single-molecule coherent control and optical readout. They've partnered with HyperLight Corporation to integrate these molecular qubits onto photonic chips, aiming to overcome the limitations of traditional solid-state systems. This technology offers a promising path towards scalable quantum computers, with a prototype expected by late 2026 and fault-tolerant systems by 2030.
