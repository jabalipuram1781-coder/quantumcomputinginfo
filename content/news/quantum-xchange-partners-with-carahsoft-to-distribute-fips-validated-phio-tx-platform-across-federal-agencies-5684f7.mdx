# Quantum XChange Partners with Carahsoft to Distribute FIPS-Validated Phio TX Platform Across Federal Agencies

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantum-xchange-partners-with-carahsoft-to-distribute-fips-validated-phio-tx-platform-across-federal-agencies/) on September 23, 2026.

Quantum XChange partnered with Carahsoft to distribute its FIPS-validated Phio TX platform to federal agencies, helping them meet post-quantum migration deadlines. The platform provides a crypto-agile management overlay, allowing agencies to hot-swap post-quantum algorithms and protocols without re-architecting applications.
