# UK Government and UKRI Unveil £78M ($103.8M USD) Quantum Commercialization, Networking, and Skills Package

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/uk-government-and-ukri-unveil-78m-103-8m-usd-quantum-commercialization-networking-and-skills-package/) on October 19, 2026.

The UK government and UK Research and Innovation (UKRI) have announced a £78 million (approximately $103.8 million USD) funding package to advance quantum sensing, networking, and workforce development. This initiative, part of the National Quantum Strategy, includes grants for commercial applications, supply-chain development, and an expansion of the QuEST Apprentices Programme. An additional £17 million has been allocated for integrating AI and high-performance computing with quantum platforms, aiming to solidify the UK's global leadership in quantum technologies.
