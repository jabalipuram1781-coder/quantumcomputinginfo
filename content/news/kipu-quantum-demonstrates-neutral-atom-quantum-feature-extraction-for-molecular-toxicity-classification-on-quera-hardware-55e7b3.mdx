# Kipu Quantum Demonstrates Neutral-Atom Quantum Feature Extraction for Molecular Toxicity Classification on QuEra Hardware

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/kipu-quantum-demonstrates-neutral-atom-quantum-feature-extraction-for-molecular-toxicity-classification-on-quera-hardware/) on October 17, 2026.

Kipu Quantum demonstrated a novel method for molecular toxicity classification using analog quantum feature extraction on QuEra's 256-qubit Aquila neutral-atom quantum processor. This approach, detailed in their technical report, utilizes 200-qubit neutral-atom arrays to generate high-dimensional quantum features. These quantum features significantly improved performance over classical methods, particularly on imbalanced datasets, doubling the F1-score and raising balanced accuracy for drug discovery and chemical safety.
