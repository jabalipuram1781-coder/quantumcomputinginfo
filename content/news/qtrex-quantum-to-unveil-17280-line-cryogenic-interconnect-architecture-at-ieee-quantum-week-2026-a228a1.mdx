# QTREX Quantum to Unveil 17,280-Line Cryogenic Interconnect Architecture at IEEE Quantum Week 2026

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/qtrex-quantum-to-unveil-17280-line-cryogenic-interconnect-architecture-at-ieee-quantum-week-2026/) on September 1, 2026.

Cryogenic interconnect developer QTREX Quantum Ltd. (Nasdaq: QTEX) has announced plans to publicly unveil an ultra-high-density interconnect architecture capable of supporting up to 17,280 coaxial lines per cryogenic stage inside commercial dilution refrigerators . The architecture will be demonstrated physically at IEEE Quantum Week 2026 (Booth 712) in Toronto, Canada, from September 13–18, 2026.
