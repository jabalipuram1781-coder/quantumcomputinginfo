# Physicists predict a new form of quantum matter that holds itself together

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-08-physicists-quantum.html) on August 17, 2026.

Researchers at Monash University have predicted a new type of quantum matter that challenges decades of thinking about how ultracold particles behave. The paper, "Quantum droplets in a resonant Bose-Fermi mixture," is published in Physical Review Letters.
