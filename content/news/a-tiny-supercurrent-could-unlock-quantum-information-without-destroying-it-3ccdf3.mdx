# A tiny supercurrent could unlock quantum information without destroying it

> **Source:** Originally published on [ScienceDaily Quantum Computers](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/11/261102202831.htm) on November 5, 2026.

Researchers have proposed a breakthrough technique for reading quantum information without destroying the delicate states that make quantum computers work. By detecting an electrical signal generated by the qubit itself, their method achieved up to 99.85% accuracy in simulations and could make future quantum computers simpler and more efficient.
