# Google Quantum AI Introduces Virtual Error Cancellation for Universal Logical Circuits

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/google-quantum-ai-introduces-virtual-error-cancellation-for-universal-logical-circuits/) on October 13, 2026.

Google Quantum AI has developed Virtual Error Cancellation (VEC), a classical post-processing technique that significantly reduces logical errors in fault-tolerant quantum circuits. VEC uses a dual-decoder approach, combining a fast real-time decoder with a high-accuracy offline decoder, to reweight experimental outcomes and suppress logical error rates by over 1,600-fold. This method offers a more efficient alternative to previous error mitigation techniques, with a significantly lower sampling overhead.
