# Against all expectations, researchers control the magnetic core of a single atom

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-magnetic-core-atom.html) on October 28, 2026.

Researchers at Delft University of Technology (the Netherlands) have succeeded in controlling the magnetism in the nucleus of a single atom with radio waves. This came as a surprise: until now, physicists believed that this nuclear spin could not be manipulated directly. The discovery could open up new possibilities for storing and reading quantum information. The research is published in the journal Nature Communications.
