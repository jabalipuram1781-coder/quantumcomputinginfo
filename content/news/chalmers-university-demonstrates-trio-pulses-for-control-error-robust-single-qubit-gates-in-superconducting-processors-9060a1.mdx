# Chalmers University Demonstrates TRIO Pulses for Control-Error-Robust Single-Qubit Gates in Superconducting Processors

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/chalmers-university-demonstrates-trio-pulses-for-control-error-robust-single-qubit-gates-in-superconducting-processors/) on October 23, 2026.

Chalmers University of Technology has developed TRIO pulses, a new quantum control framework for superconducting processors. These pulses enable single-qubit gates to maintain high fidelity even with significant control-amplitude miscalibrations and frequency detuning errors. This breakthrough offers a way to reduce recalibration overheads in large-scale quantum processors and has been demonstrated with an X-gate achieving 99.90% fidelity.
