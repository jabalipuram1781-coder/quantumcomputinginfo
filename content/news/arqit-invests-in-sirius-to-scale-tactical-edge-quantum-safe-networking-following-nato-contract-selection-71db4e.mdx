# Arqit Invests in Sirius to Scale Tactical Edge Quantum-Safe Networking Following NATO Contract Selection

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/arqit-invests-in-sirius-to-scale-tactical-edge-quantum-safe-networking-following-nato-contract-selection/) on October 24, 2026.

Arqit, a quantum-safe encryption provider, has invested in Sirius Computer, a tactical communications developer, to enhance post-quantum security for defense and intelligence. This partnership follows Arqit's selection for a NATO contract and aims to provide quantum-safe encryption for military communications without hardware overhauls. The collaboration addresses limitations of existing quantum-safe technologies in tactical environments and will fund Sirius's engineering expansion.
