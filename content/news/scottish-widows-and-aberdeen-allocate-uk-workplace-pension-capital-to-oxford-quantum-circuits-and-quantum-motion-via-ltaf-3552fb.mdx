# Scottish Widows and Aberdeen Allocate UK Workplace Pension Capital to Oxford Quantum Circuits and Quantum Motion via LTAF

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/scottish-widows-and-aberdeen-allocate-uk-workplace-pension-capital-to-oxford-quantum-circuits-and-quantum-motion-via-ltaf/) on November 7, 2026.

Scottish Widows and Aberdeen Investments have allocated UK workplace pension capital to Oxford Quantum Circuits and Quantum Motion through the CG Scottish Widows Growth LTAF. This initiative aims to connect defined-contribution retirement assets with venture-stage technology firms, fostering domestic growth in deep tech. The fund will support hardware developers like OQC and Quantum Motion, as well as battery technology developer Nyobolt and genomic medicine firm Genomics Limited.
