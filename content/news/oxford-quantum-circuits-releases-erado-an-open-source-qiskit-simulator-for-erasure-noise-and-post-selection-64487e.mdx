# Oxford Quantum Circuits Releases Erado: An Open-Source Qiskit Simulator for Erasure Noise and Post-Selection

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/oxford-quantum-circuits-releases-erado-an-open-source-qiskit-simulator-for-erasure-noise-and-post-selection/) on September 18, 2026.

Oxford Quantum Circuits (OQC) has launched Erado, an open-source Qiskit simulator for erasure noise and post-selection, integrated into their QCaaS SDK. This tool allows researchers to simulate erasure-aware noise models and evaluate quantum error mitigation techniques, with accompanying research showing postselection can fully mitigate erasure noise below 3.0% error rates, outperforming single-rail architectures. Erado offers flexible simulation modes and precise parameter controls for analyzing the trade-offs of post-selection.
