# Leiden University and Riverlane Research Reveals Distance-Losing Flaw in Correlated Decoding Below Threshold

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/leiden-university-and-riverlane-research-reveals-distance-losing-flaw-in-correlated-decoding-below-threshold/) on October 23, 2026.

Leiden University and Riverlane research uncovered a flaw in correlated decoding, causing a loss of effective code distance and higher logical failure rates in quantum error correction. They introduced a new regularization parameter to fix this, restoring nominal distance scaling and improving accuracy without adding computational overhead. This discovery offers a crucial design principle for future quantum decoding architectures.
