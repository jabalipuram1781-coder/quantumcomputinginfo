# JIJ Expands Quantum-HPC and Industrial R&D Collaborations with Mitsubishi Electric, Q-CTRL, and Tinuviel Therapeutics

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/jij-expands-quantum-hpc-and-industrial-rd-collaborations-with-mitsubishi-electric-q-ctrl-and-tinuviel-therapeutics/) on November 14, 2026.

JIJ Inc. has expanded its quantum-HPC and industrial R&D collaborations with three new partnerships. These collaborations involve Mitsubishi Electric for thermal-fluid engineering simulation, Q-CTRL for electrical power-grid optimization, and Tinuviel Therapeutics for predictive gut microbiome modeling. JIJ's hybrid algorithmic stack will be used across these diverse applications.
