# Kipu Quantum and NTT DATA Partner to Scale Enterprise Quantum Computing and QML Applications

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/kipu-quantum-and-ntt-data-partner-to-scale-enterprise-quantum-computing-and-qml-applications/) on October 17, 2026.

Kipu Quantum and NTT DATA have partnered to advance enterprise quantum computing and QML solutions. The collaboration will integrate quantum algorithms into existing enterprise technology, leveraging Kipu Quantum's expertise in hardware-specific compression techniques. This partnership, which began with a pilot program at Komatsu Peru, aims to develop and deploy commercial algorithms for various applications, including supply chain logistics and financial risk modeling, initially in Latin America and then globally.
