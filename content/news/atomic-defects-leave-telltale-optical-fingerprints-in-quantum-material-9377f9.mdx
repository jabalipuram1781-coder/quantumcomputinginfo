# Atomic defects leave telltale optical fingerprints in quantum material

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-atomic-defects-telltale-optical-fingerprints.html) on November 9, 2026.

The tiny atomic defects so important for future quantum technologies can be difficult to spot. Fortunately, they leave behind some clues. The defects can alter how a material responds to light, producing a distinctive optical fingerprint that scientists can measure. The challenge is figuring out which fingerprints belong to which defects.
