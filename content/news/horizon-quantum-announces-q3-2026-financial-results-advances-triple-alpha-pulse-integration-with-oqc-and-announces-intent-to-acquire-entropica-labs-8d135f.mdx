# Horizon Quantum Announces Q3 2026 Financial Results: Advances Triple Alpha Pulse Integration with OQC and Announces Intent to Acquire Entropica Labs

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/horizon-quantum-announces-q3-2026-financial-results-advances-triple-alpha-pulse-integration-with-oqc-and-announces-intent-to-acquire-entropica-labs/) on November 11, 2026.

Horizon Quantum announced its Q3 2026 financial results, reporting no commercial revenue but a significant GAAP net income of $33.41 million due to a non-cash accounting gain. The company highlighted advancements in quantum computing software, including integration with Oxford Quantum Circuits and the development of multiplexed characterization techniques. Horizon Quantum also revealed its intent to acquire Entropica Labs for $27 million, aiming to enhance its quantum error correction capabilities.
