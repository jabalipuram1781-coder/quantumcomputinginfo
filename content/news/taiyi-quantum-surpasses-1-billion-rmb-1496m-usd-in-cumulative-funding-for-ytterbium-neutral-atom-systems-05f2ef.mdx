# Taiyi Quantum Surpasses ¥1 Billion RMB ($149.6M+ USD) in Cumulative Funding for Ytterbium Neutral-Atom Systems

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/taiyi-quantum-surpasses-%c2%a51-billion-rmb-149-6m-usd-in-cumulative-funding-for-ytterbium-neutral-atom-systems/) on October 20, 2026.

Taiyi Quantum, a Shanghai-based quantum hardware developer, has secured over ¥1 billion RMB (approx. $149.6M USD) in cumulative funding within nine months of its founding, achieving unicorn valuation status. The company specializes in Ytterbium neutral-atom arrays for fault-tolerant quantum computing and has made significant progress in hardware integration and open-sourced its LUOSHU compiler.
