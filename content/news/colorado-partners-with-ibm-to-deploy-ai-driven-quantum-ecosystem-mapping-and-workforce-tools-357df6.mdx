# Colorado Partners with IBM to Deploy AI-Driven Quantum Ecosystem Mapping and Workforce Tools

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/colorado-partners-with-ibm-to-deploy-ai-driven-quantum-ecosystem-mapping-and-workforce-tools/) on November 11, 2026.

In a two-year initiative, Colorado has partnered with the IBM Impact Accelerator to advance quantum technology. This collaboration, coordinated by the Colorado Office of Economic Development and International Trade (OEDIT), aims to enhance quantum readiness, analyze the ecosystem, and develop the workforce across the Mountain West. The project will utilize IBM's AI-driven analytics to map supply chains, model talent requirements, and provide public access to IBM Qiskit, ultimately supporting an estimated 11,000 new quantum jobs.
