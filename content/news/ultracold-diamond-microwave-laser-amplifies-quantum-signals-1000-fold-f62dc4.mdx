# Ultracold diamond microwave laser amplifies quantum signals 1,000-fold

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-ultracold-diamond-microwave-laser-amplifies.html) on November 16, 2026.

Researchers from the Okinawa Institute of Science and Technology (OIST) have created a spin-based microwave amplifier that operates near absolute zero, boosting signals by more than 1,000-fold while adding noise close to the bare minimum required by the laws of physics. As a more robust alternative to current state-of-the-art devices, the OIST device can handle much stronger signals and operate in magnetic fields, paving the way for practical quantum computing and higher-resolution sensing technologies....
