# Haiqu Unveils AgenticOS Platform for Automated, Scientifically Verified Quantum Research Workflows

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/haiqu-unveils-agenticos-platform-for-automated-scientifically-verified-quantum-research-workflows/) on October 7, 2026.

Haiqu has launched AgenticOS, an AI-driven platform that automates quantum computing research workflows. It uses specialized AI agents and a test-driven development approach to prevent errors and ensure scientific accuracy in complex calculations. The platform has successfully simulated a proton-transfer problem and a Fermi-Hubbard model, demonstrating its ability to deliver accurate results and optimize quantum circuits.
