> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-classicalquantum-interactions-complex-quantum-behavior.html) on November 10, 2026.

An international collaboration demonstrates that classical nonlinear dynamics can drive single- and two-photon states to thermalize, opening new avenues for simulating complex quantum many-body systems within accessible optical architectures.
