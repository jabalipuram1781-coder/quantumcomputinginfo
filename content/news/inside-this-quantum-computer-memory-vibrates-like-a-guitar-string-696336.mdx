# Inside this quantum computer, memory vibrates like a guitar string

> **Source:** Originally published on [ScienceDaily Quantum Computers](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/11/261109205416.htm) on November 12, 2026.

Researchers have created a quantum computer that stores information in microscopic vibrations, potentially making quantum machines smaller, more powerful, and better at preserving information. The breakthrough successfully demonstrated advanced quantum calculations and could help overcome major barriers to practical quantum computing.
