# TU Delft Achieves Direct Single-Atom Nuclear Magnetic Resonance via Scanning Tunneling Microscope Probe

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/tu-delft-achieves-direct-single-atom-nuclear-magnetic-resonance-via-scanning-tunneling-microscope-probe/) on October 31, 2026.

TU Delft physicists have achieved direct nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) control over a single surface-adsorbed atom, specifically a 47Ti atom. This was accomplished using an Electron-Nuclear Double Resonance (ENDOR) measurement scheme within an ultra-low-temperature scanning tunneling microscope. This breakthrough allows for coherent nuclear addressing without relying on electron-nuclear spin hybridization, opening doors for atomic-scale quantum memory registers and high-dimensional qudits.
