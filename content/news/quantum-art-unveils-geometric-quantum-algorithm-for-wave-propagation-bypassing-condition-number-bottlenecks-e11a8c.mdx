# Quantum Art Unveils Geometric Quantum Algorithm for Wave Propagation, Bypassing Condition-Number Bottlenecks

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantum-art-unveils-geometric-quantum-algorithm-for-wave-propagation-bypassing-condition-number-bottlenecks/) on October 27, 2026.

Quantum Art has developed a geometric quantum algorithm for wave propagation that bypasses condition-number bottlenecks, outperforming classical supercomputers. This method, detailed in a research preprint, directly prepares quantum states on the wave equation's resonant dispersion manifold, making it suitable for applications like acoustics and radar. Their trapped-ion hardware architecture is projected to efficiently execute these complex circuits using approximately 100 logical qubits for problems requiring 10^18 to 10^21 classical grid points.
