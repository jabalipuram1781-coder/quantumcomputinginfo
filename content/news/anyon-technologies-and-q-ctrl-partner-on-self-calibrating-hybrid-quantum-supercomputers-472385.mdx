# Anyon Technologies and Q-CTRL Partner on Self-Calibrating Hybrid Quantum Supercomputers

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/anyon-technologies-and-q-ctrl-partner-on-self-calibrating-hybrid-quantum-supercomputers/) on November 12, 2026.

Anyon Technologies and Q-CTRL have partnered to create self-calibrating hybrid quantum supercomputers. They are integrating Q-CTRL's autonomous calibration software, Boulder Opal, with Anyon's superconducting quantum computing systems, which use NVIDIA GPUs. This collaboration aims to automate the setup and maintenance of quantum processors, reducing operational costs and making quantum computing more accessible for commercial use by eliminating manual tuning.
