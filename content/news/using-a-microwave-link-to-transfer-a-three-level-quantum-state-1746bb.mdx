# Using a microwave link to transfer a three-level quantum state

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-microwave-link-quantum-state.html) on November 18, 2026.

Quantum technologies, devices and systems that process, store, transfer or collect information leveraging the laws of quantum mechanics, could outperform their classical counterparts on some real-world tasks. Many of these technologies encode quantum information in qubits, which can exist in either of two basic states or a superposition of them.
