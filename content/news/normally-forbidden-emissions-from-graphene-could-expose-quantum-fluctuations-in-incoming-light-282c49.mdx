# Normally forbidden emissions from graphene could expose quantum fluctuations in incoming light

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-forbidden-emissions-graphene-expose-quantum.html) on October 19, 2026.

Voltage fluctuations, light fluctuations, temperature fluctuations—any fluctuations are troublesome and thus undesirable. They don't just cause discomfort; in engineering and medical applications, they affect the accuracy of measurements or introduce errors in controls. Even at the minute level of quantum phenomena, fluctuations are considered undesirable.
