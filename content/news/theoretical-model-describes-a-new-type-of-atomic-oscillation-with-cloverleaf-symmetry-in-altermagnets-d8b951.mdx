# Theoretical model describes a new type of atomic oscillation with cloverleaf symmetry in altermagnets

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-theoretical-atomic-oscillation-cloverleaf-symmetry.html) on November 23, 2026.

A team of researchers at Martin Luther University Halle-Wittenberg (MLU) and the University of Münster has discovered a new type of atomic oscillation in altermagnets, which exhibit a striking cloverleaf symmetry. The findings were published in the journal Physical Review Letters.
