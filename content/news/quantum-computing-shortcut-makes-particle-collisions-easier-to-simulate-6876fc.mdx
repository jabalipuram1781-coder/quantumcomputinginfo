# Quantum computing shortcut makes particle collisions easier to simulate

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-quantum-shortcut-particle-collisions-easier.html) on October 7, 2026.

Collisions between particles at high energies can sometimes produce new particles and shed light on interactions between the fundamental constituents of matter. Simulating these collisions and their underlying processes could yield valuable insights into how matter behaves at extremely small scales.
