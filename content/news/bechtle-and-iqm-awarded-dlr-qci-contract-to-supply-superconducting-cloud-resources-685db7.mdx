# Bechtle and IQM Awarded DLR QCI Contract to Supply Superconducting Cloud Resources

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/bechtle-and-iqm-awarded-dlr-qci-contract-to-supply-superconducting-cloud-resources/) on October 21, 2026.

Bechtle and IQM Quantum Computers have secured a contract with the German Aerospace Center’s Quantum Computing Initiative (DLR QCI) to provide cloud-based superconducting quantum computing resources until June 2027. This agreement will give DLR QCI researchers access to IQM's quantum processors through the IQM Resonance™ cloud platform, expanding their hardware options for applications in various fields. The contract also includes a dedicated classical server for hybrid quantum-classical algorithms and ensures all data processing remains within Germany.
