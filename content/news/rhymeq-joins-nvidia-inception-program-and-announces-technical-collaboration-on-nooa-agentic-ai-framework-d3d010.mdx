# RhymeQ Joins NVIDIA Inception Program and Announces Technical Collaboration on NOOA Agentic AI Framework

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/rhymeq-joins-nvidia-inception-program-and-announces-technical-collaboration-on-nooa-agentic-ai-framework/) on October 23, 2026.

RhymeQ Technologies has joined the NVIDIA Inception program, aligning its RHYME abstraction layer with NVIDIA's CUDA-Q platform. They are also collaborating on research to integrate agentic AI with quantum computing using NVIDIA's NOOA framework. This initiative aims to enable AI agents to orchestrate workloads between classical GPUs and quantum processing units.
