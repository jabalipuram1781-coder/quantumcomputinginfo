# Qunova Computing Achieves Chemical Accuracy at 80 Qubits on Iron-Sulfur Cluster Using IBM Quantum System Two and Fugaku

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/qunova-computing-achieves-chemical-accuracy-at-80-qubits-on-iron-sulfur-cluster-using-ibm-quantum-system-two-and-fugaku/) on November 13, 2026.

Qunova Computing achieved a breakthrough in quantum chemistry by simulating an 80-qubit iron-sulfur cluster with chemical accuracy using a hybrid approach combining IBM Quantum System Two and the Fugaku supercomputer. Their HI-VQE algorithm reduced classical computing costs by approximately 300x while matching classical benchmarks. This advancement has significant implications for understanding complex biological and industrial processes.
