# Quantinuum and Oracle Launch OCI Quantum AI Compute Beta Program for Early Access to Helios

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantinuum-and-oracle-launch-oci-quantum-ai-compute-beta-program-for-early-access-to-helios/) on October 23, 2026.

Quantinuum and Oracle have launched the OCI Quantum AI Compute Beta Program, offering early cloud-based access to Quantinuum's Helios quantum processor for hybrid quantum-AI workflows. This program integrates Helios within Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) AI data centers, providing a browser-based Python development environment for various applications. The initiative will be further detailed at Oracle AI World 2026.
