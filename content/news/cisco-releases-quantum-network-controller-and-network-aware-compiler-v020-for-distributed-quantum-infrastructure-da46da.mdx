Cisco's Outshift division has launched the Quantum Network Controller and Network-Aware Quantum Compiler v0.2.0 to manage multi-node quantum networks. The Controller offers Entanglement-as-a-Service, enabling on-demand entanglement across diverse quantum hardware.
