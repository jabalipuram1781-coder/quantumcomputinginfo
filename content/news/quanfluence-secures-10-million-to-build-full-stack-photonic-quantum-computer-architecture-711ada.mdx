# Quanfluence Secures $10 Million to Build Full-Stack Photonic Quantum Computer Architecture

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quanfluence-secures-10-million-to-build-full-stack-photonic-quantum-computer-architecture/) on October 7, 2026.

Quanfluence, a Bengaluru-based deep technology startup, has secured $10 million in Series A funding led by Chiratae Ventures, with participation from Pi Ventures and Rainmatter by Zerodha. The company will use the investment to develop a full-stack photonic quantum processing system, integrating its photonic integrated circuits, electronic control silicon, and software algorithms. Quanfluence aims to launch initial photonic processors by 2027 and scalable fault-tolerant systems by 2029, reinforcing India's quantum ecosystem.
