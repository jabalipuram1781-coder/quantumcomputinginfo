# South Korea Launches Nationwide Open Quantum Testbed Expansion Led by KT and SK Broadband

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/south-korea-launches-nationwide-open-quantum-testbed-expansion-led-by-kt-and-sk-broadband/) on October 24, 2026.

South Korea is expanding its Open Quantum Testbed nationwide from 2026-2028, with a 7.4 billion won initial investment. KT Corporation and SK Broadband are leading the project, focusing on terrestrial, cross-border, and future quantum communication technologies. This initiative aims to establish a robust quantum communications infrastructure across the country and internationally.
