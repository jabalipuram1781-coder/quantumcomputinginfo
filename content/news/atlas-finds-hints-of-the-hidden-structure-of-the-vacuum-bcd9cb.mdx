# ATLAS finds hints of the hidden structure of the vacuum

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-atlas-hints-hidden-vacuum.html) on November 27, 2026.

Researchers at the University of Bonn, together with colleagues from Oxford and Prague, played a leading role in analyzing particle collisions at CERN's Large Hadron Collider. The ATLAS Collaboration observed more proton collisions with the particle pattern it was looking for than expected. This could point to a phenomenon predicted by the Standard Model of particle physics that has never been directly observed, known as QCD instanton processes.
