# Software advance brings complex quantum simulations to standard computers

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-software-advance-complex-quantum-simulations.html) on October 14, 2026.

A major software advance could aid the creation of bigger, more reliable quantum computers, a study suggests. Researchers have developed an algorithm that enables off-the-shelf computers to run quantum simulations previously only possible using the world's most powerful supercomputers.
