# Scientists build world''s most accurate atomic clock

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-scientists-world-accurate-atomic-clock.html) on September 23, 2026.

Take a second and turn it into trillions of moments. Measure each one. That's how precisely an atomic clock at Singapore's Centre for Quantum Technologies (CQT) keeps time—and with record-setting accuracy, according to results published in Nature on Sept. 23.
