# IBM Quantum and QuICS Deploy AI Agents to Discover Over 500 High-Efficiency Magic-State Factories

IBM Quantum and QuICS researchers have discovered over 500 new "magic-state factories" using AI agents and deterministic solvers. These factories produce non-Clifford states with significantly lower resource requirements, which is crucial for advancing fault-tolerant quantum computing.
