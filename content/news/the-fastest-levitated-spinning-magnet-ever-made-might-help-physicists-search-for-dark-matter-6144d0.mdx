# The fastest levitated spinning magnet ever made might help physicists search for dark matter

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-fastest-levitated-magnet-physicists-dark.html) on November 9, 2026.

Tiny levitated magnets are able to avoid contact friction, making them useful for detecting extremely weak forces and magnetic fields and acting as ultralow-noise quantum sensors and precision measurement tools. Combining levitation with extreme cold further reduces unwanted motion that can make faint signals hard to detect.
