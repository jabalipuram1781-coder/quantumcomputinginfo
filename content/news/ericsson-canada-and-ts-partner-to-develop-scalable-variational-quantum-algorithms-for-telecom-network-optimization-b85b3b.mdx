# Ericsson Canada and ÉTS Partner to Develop Scalable Variational Quantum Algorithms for Telecom Network Optimization

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/ericsson-canada-and-ets-partner-to-develop-scalable-variational-quantum-algorithms-for-telecom-network-optimization/) on October 23, 2026.

Ericsson Canada and ÉTS are collaborating on a research project to develop scalable quantum algorithms for telecom network optimization. The project, supported by Mitacs, focuses on advancing variational quantum algorithms (VQAs) to optimize dynamic routing, traffic scheduling, and resource allocation. Led by Professor Jacob Biamonte at ÉTS, the research aims to mitigate noise and improve convergence in quantum circuits.
