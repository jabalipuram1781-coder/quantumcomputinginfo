# Alice & Bob Demonstrates Multi-Hour Physical Bit-Flip Suppression on Single-Cat and Helium 2 Processors

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/alice-bob-demonstrates-multi-hour-physical-bit-flip-suppression-on-single-cat-and-helium-2-processors/) on November 2, 2026.

Alice & Bob has achieved a significant breakthrough in quantum computing, demonstrating macroscopic bit-flip lifetimes exceeding one hour on their bosonic cat-qubit hardware. Their single-qubit device showed an average idle bit-flip lifetime of 44 minutes, while their 12-qubit Helium 2 processor achieved lifetimes of over three to four hours. This advancement provides crucial experimental validation for their fault-tolerant quantum computing architecture and brings them closer to realizing active logical error correction.
