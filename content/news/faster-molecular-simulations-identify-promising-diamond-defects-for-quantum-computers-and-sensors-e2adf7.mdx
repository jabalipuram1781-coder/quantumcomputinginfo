# Faster molecular simulations identify promising diamond defects for quantum computers and sensors

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-faster-molecular-simulations-diamond-defects.html) on October 13, 2026.

A faster way of finding the best diamond-based materials for quantum computers has been developed by a researcher at the U.S. Department of Energy's (DOE) Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory (PPPL). The new approach has already identified promising materials for practical quantum sensors and computers that could one day transform scientific research as a whole. The research was recently published in the journal ACS Central Science and has already garnered attention from other experts in the field....
