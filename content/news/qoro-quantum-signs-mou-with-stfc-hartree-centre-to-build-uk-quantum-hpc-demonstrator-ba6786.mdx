# Qoro Quantum Signs MoU with STFC Hartree Centre to Build UK Quantum-HPC Demonstrator

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/qoro-quantum-signs-mou-with-stfc-hartree-centre-to-build-uk-quantum-hpc-demonstrator/) on September 12, 2026.

Qoro Quantum and STFC Hartree Centre have partnered to build a UK Quantum-HPC demonstrator, integrating Qoro's software stack into the Hartree Centre's HPC cluster. This collaboration aims to provide enterprise-grade hybrid quantum-HPC capabilities for UK industry and public sector research, utilizing Qoro's Divi SDK and Maestro simulation engine with the open-source QRMI for seamless integration and resource management. The initiative will focus on practical challenges in electronic structure theory, physical sciences modeling, and quantum chemistry, aligning with broader UK computing initiatives.
