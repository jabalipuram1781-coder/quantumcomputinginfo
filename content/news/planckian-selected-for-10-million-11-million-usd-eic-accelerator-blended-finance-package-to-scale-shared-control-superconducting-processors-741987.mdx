# Planckian Selected for €10 Million ($11 Million USD) EIC Accelerator Blended Finance Package to Scale Shared-Control Superconducting Processors

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/planckian-selected-for-e10-million-11-million-usd-eic-accelerator-blended-finance-package-to-scale-shared-control-superconducting-processors/) on November 17, 2026.

Planckian, an Italian superconducting quantum hardware developer, has been selected for the European Innovation Council (EIC) Accelerator, securing €10 million ($11 million USD) in blended financing. This funding will support the development of their shared-control superconducting processor architecture, which aims to overcome scaling limitations of conventional quantum computers. The company plans to use this investment to transition its design into an externally accessible quantum computing system.
