# U.S. Department of War Announces $350 Million Quantum Package, Highlighted by $150 Million OSC Loan Commitment for PsiQuantum

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/u-s-department-of-war-announces-350-million-quantum-package-highlighted-by-150-million-osc-loan-commitment-for-psiquantum/) on October 8, 2026.

PsiQuantum has secured a conditional loan commitment of up to $150 million from the U.S. Office of Strategic Capital to expand its PsiFactory in Milpitas, California. This funding will support the development of a domestic supply chain for quantum computing and follows other significant investments from DARPA and the U.S. Department of Commerce. PsiQuantum is also developing facilities in Illinois and Australia.
