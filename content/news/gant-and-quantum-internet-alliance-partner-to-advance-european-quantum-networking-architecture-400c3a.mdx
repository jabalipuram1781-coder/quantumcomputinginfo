# GÉANT and Quantum Internet Alliance Partner to Advance European Quantum Networking Architecture

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/geant-and-quantum-internet-alliance-partner-to-advance-european-quantum-networking-architecture/) on September 12, 2026.

GÉANT and the Quantum Internet Alliance (QIA) have partnered to advance European quantum networking by signing an MoU on September 8, 2026. This collaboration leverages GÉANT's optical network and QIA's quantum networking expertise to develop infrastructure, standards, and interoperability protocols, aiming to transition quantum communication prototypes into operational cross-border trials and establish a sovereign European Quantum Internet.
