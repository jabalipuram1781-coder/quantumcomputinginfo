# Light-activated molecules in porous crystals show potential for quantum chemical sensing

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-molecules-porous-crystals-potential-quantum.html) on November 13, 2026.

An international research team led by Nobuhiro Yanai from the University of Tokyo and Michael R. Wasielewski from Northwestern University has created polarized radical pairs, molecules with unpaired electrons whose spins align, in metal–organic frameworks (MOFs), porous materials consisting of metal ions linked by organic molecules.
