# Qambria Secures $2.4M Pre-Seed Funding to Commercialize Sub-Microsecond Classical Control Infrastructure for Fault-Tolerant Quantum Systems

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/qambria-secures-2-4m-pre-seed-funding-to-commercialize-sub-microsecond-classical-control-infrastructure-for-fault-tolerant-quantum-systems/) on September 25, 2026.

Qambria AG, a Swiss quantum infrastructure developer, has successfully closed a $2.4 million pre-seed funding round led by Syntropy. The company aims to commercialize a classical engine for quantum computing, focusing on software and control orchestration to integrate quantum processing units with high-performance computing. Qambria's IP-licensing model will help lower costs and complexity for quantum hardware manufacturers and data centers.
