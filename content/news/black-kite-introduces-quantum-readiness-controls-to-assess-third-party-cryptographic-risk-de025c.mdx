# Black Kite Introduces Quantum Readiness Controls to Assess Third-Party Cryptographic Risk

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/black-kite-introduces-quantum-readiness-controls-to-assess-third-party-cryptographic-risk/) on October 21, 2026.

Black Kite has launched Quantum Readiness, a new feature in its Cyber Rating engine that assesses third-party vendors' preparedness for post-quantum cryptography. This helps organizations identify vendors using quantum-resistant encryption and meet upcoming regulatory deadlines like OMB M-26-15 and EU NIS2. The tool aims to mitigate "harvest now, decrypt later" vulnerabilities before national PQC migration deadlines.
