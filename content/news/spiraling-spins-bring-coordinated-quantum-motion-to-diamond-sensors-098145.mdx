# Spiraling spins bring coordinated quantum motion to diamond sensors

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-spiraling-quantum-motion-diamond-sensors.html) on November 20, 2026.

Trapped within diamond's crystal lattice are tiny defects called nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers, where a nitrogen atom sits beside an empty gap where a carbon atom should be. Because of their quantum spins, electrons trapped at these defects behave like tiny magnets, turning diamond into a highly sensitive detector for magnetic fields.
