# Q-SAFE Solutions Unveils EU-27 Carrier-Grade Quantum-Safe Network Platform

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/q-safe-solutions-unveils-eu-27-carrier-grade-quantum-safe-network-platform/) on September 18, 2026.

Q-SAFE Solutions GmbH has launched a carrier-grade quantum-safe network platform in the EU-27, integrating Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) and Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) with centralized hybrid key management. This system operates over existing fiber-optic infrastructure, eliminating the need for dedicated dark fiber, and is designed for telecommunications providers, critical infrastructure, and government agencies. It aligns with EU technological sovereignty mandates and will be showcased at the European Commission’s QCI Days 2026.
