# Multi-Institutional Consortium Expands QRMI to Standardize Quantum-HPC Integration Across Major Workload Managers

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/multi-institutional-consortium-expands-qrmi-to-standardize-quantum-hpc-integration-across-major-workload-managers/) on October 10, 2026.

The Quantum Resource Management Interface (QRMI) is expanding to standardize quantum-HPC integration across major workload managers like Slurm, PBS Professional, and Kubernetes. This open-source middleware layer addresses challenges of integrating heterogeneous QPU hardware by providing a unified interface for abstracting and scheduling QPUs alongside classical resources. QRMI is already deployed in major supercomputing environments, including CINECA, Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, and the STFC Hartree Centre.
