# Germany Issues Joint Federal PQC Migration Guidelines to Align with EU Post-Quantum Roadmap

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/germany-issues-joint-federal-pqc-migration-guidelines-to-align-with-eu-post-quantum-roadmap/) on October 15, 2026.

Germany has released national guidelines for migrating to Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC), aligning with the EU's roadmap. The strategy, driven by advancements in AI and threats to classical encryption, outlines a three-phase migration timeline, prioritizing high-risk systems by 2030 and aiming for full migration by 2035. This initiative mandates hybrid cryptographic mechanisms and PQC readiness in risk management for critical infrastructure.
