# Diraq Expands U.S. Footprint with New Mexico Quantum R&D Laboratory to Advance DARPA QBI Milestones

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/diraq-expands-u-s-footprint-with-new-mexico-quantum-rd-laboratory-to-advance-darpa-qbi-milestones/) on September 30, 2026.

Diraq is expanding its U.S. operations by opening a new quantum R&D lab in Albuquerque, New Mexico, in collaboration with the New Mexico Technology and Innovation Office and Roadrunner Venture Studios. This expansion, supported by New Mexico's quantum technology allocation, will focus on characterizing high-density silicon spin-qubit chips for the DARPA Quantum Benchmarking Initiative. The lab, operational by late 2026, aims to advance utility-scale fault-tolerant quantum computing by leveraging CMOS fabrication for high-density qubit packing.
