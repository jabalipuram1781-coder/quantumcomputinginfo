# DigiCert Announces General Availability of Quantum Central Platform for PQC Migration Management

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/digicert-announces-general-availability-of-quantum-central-platform-for-pqc-migration-management/) on September 25, 2026.

DigiCert has launched Quantum Central, an enterprise management suite within its DigiCert ONE platform, to help organizations manage their transition to quantum-safe encryption. This tool consolidates cryptographic assets, tracks policy compliance, and automates remediation workflows, addressing the gap where most enterprises are planning PQC initiatives but few have deployed quantum-safe primitives. Quantum Central facilitates incremental PQC migration, providing auditable metrics and cryptographic bill of materials (CBOM) exports for regulatory compliance.
