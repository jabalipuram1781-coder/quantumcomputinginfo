# Messe Stuttgart Announces Winners of the Quantum Effects Award 2026 Across Four Hardware and Software Categories

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/messe-stuttgart-announces-winners-of-the-quantum-effects-award-2026-across-four-hardware-and-software-categories/) on September 29, 2026.

Messe Stuttgart announced the winners of the Quantum Effects Award 2026 across four categories: C12 & Thales for Quantum Computing Hardware, Fraunhofer FOKUS for Quantum Computing Software, QSENSATO SRL for Quantum Sensing, and PicoQuant GmbH for Quantum Communication. These awards recognize application-oriented technical developments at TRL 5 or higher, showcasing direct industry utility and pathways for commercial deployment in the quantum technology sector.
