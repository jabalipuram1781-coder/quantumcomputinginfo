# Mesa Quantum Raises $11.8M to Scale Chip-Scale Quantum Sensors and Alternative PNT Infrastructure

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/mesa-quantum-raises-11-8m-to-scale-chip-scale-quantum-sensors-and-alternative-pnt-infrastructure/) on September 25, 2026.

Mesa Quantum has raised $11.8 million in funding, led by Playground Global, bringing their total venture funding to nearly $16 million. This capital will accelerate the development and manufacturing of their chip-scale quantum sensors and alternative PNT infrastructure. Their technology, which includes chip-scale atomic clocks and quantum inertial sensors, provides precise timing and navigation independent of GPS, addressing vulnerabilities in satellite-based systems.
