# D-Wave Delivers Italy’s First Annealing Quantum Computer to Lombardy Infrastructure Hub

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/d-wave-delivers-italys-first-annealing-quantum-computer-to-lombardy-infrastructure-hub/) on October 15, 2026.

D-Wave has installed Italy's first on-premises Advantage2 annealing quantum computer at Villa Saporiti in Como. Funded by Swiss Quantum Technology SA (SQT) with a €10 million agreement, this 4,400+ qubit system will be accessible to European researchers and businesses both on-site and remotely via D-Wave's Leap cloud service. This initiative supports the Volta Declaration's goals for quantum computing and AI development in Europe, coinciding with the bicentennial of Alessandro Volta's death.
