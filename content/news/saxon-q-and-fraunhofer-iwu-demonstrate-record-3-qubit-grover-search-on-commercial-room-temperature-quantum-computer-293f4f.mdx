# SAXON Q and Fraunhofer IWU Demonstrate Record 3-Qubit Grover Search on Commercial Room-Temperature Quantum Computer

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/saxon-q-and-fraunhofer-iwu-demonstrate-record-3-qubit-grover-search-on-commercial-room-temperature-quantum-computer/) on October 14, 2026.

SAXON Q and Fraunhofer IWU have achieved a record-breaking 3-qubit Grover search on a commercial room-temperature quantum computer, outperforming previous room-temperature quantum searches and nearing cryogenic superconducting processors' benchmarks. The experiment, detailed in a pre-print paper, utilized a nitrogen-vacancy center diamond quantum computer operating at ambient conditions, showcasing a significant advancement in quantum computing hardware.
