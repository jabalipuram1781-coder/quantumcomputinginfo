# How electrons find their way to diamond''s quantum defects

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-electrons-diamond-quantum-defects.html) on November 18, 2026.

A diamond's most useful quantum properties come from defects, places where the regular arrangement of carbon atoms is broken. One of these, a nitrogen atom situated next to a vacancy caused by a missing carbon atom, has become a key component in quantum technology.
