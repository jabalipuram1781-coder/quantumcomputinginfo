# QuiX Quantum and Dutch Photonics Consortium Form National Mission for 100+ Qubit Computer

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quix-quantum-and-dutch-photonics-consortium-form-national-mission-for-100-qubit-computer/) on November 11, 2026.

QuiX Quantum is leading a Dutch national initiative to build a 100+ qubit universal quantum processor by December 2028. This three-year program, involving eight academic and industrial partners, focuses on developing manufacturing and architectural foundations for the quantum computer. The mission addresses key challenges like loss, detection, and high-speed control in photonic quantum systems, aiming to optimize sub-assemblies for fault-tolerant scaling.
