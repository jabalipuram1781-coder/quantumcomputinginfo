# Xanadu Secures Up to $10 Million in Additional DARPA Funding Under QBI Program

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/xanadu-secures-up-to-10-million-in-additional-darpa-funding-under-qbi-program/) on October 19, 2026.

Xanadu, a photonic quantum computing company, has secured up to $10 million in additional DARPA funding through the Quantum Benchmarking Initiative (QBI). This one-year contract extension will support Xanadu's hardware and software development, particularly for its fault-tolerant photonic architecture and the PennyLane open-source quantum software framework. The funding aims to accelerate the development of practical, fault-tolerant quantum computing for various industrial and government applications.
