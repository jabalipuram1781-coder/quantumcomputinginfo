# Zumo and Quantum Chain Partner to Deploy Quantum-Secure Financial Infrastructure in the UK

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/zumo-and-quantum-chain-partner-to-deploy-quantum-secure-financial-infrastructure-in-the-uk/) on October 7, 2026.

Zumo, a UK-based digital asset platform, has partnered with Quantum Chain to build quantum-safe financial infrastructure in the UK. This collaboration aims to protect regulated institutions, private networks, and digital payments from quantum computing and AI-driven cyber threats. The initiative will deploy post-quantum cryptography across central bank digital currencies (CBDCs), tokenized deposits, stablecoins, and private financial networks, aligning with the UK's upcoming cryptoasset regulations.
