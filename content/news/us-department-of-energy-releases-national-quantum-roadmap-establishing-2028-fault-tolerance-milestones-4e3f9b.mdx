# US Department of Energy Releases National Quantum Roadmap Establishing 2028 Fault-Tolerance Milestones

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/us-department-of-energy-releases-national-quantum-roadmap-establishing-2028-fault-tolerance-milestones/) on October 2, 2026.

The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) has released its National Quantum Roadmap, aiming for error-corrected quantum computing by 2028. This strategy shifts focus from qubit counts to demonstrated scientific utility, with a three-phphased framework to integrate quantum processing units into existing high-performance computing and AI infrastructure. The roadmap also emphasizes modality-neutral investments and new partnership models to accelerate technology transfer.
