# Quantum calculations reveal how spin defects lose energy

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-quantum-reveal-defects-energy.html) on October 15, 2026.

When light hits a tiny defect embedded in a solid material like diamond or silicon carbide, it can kick the defect's electrons into an excited state. These spin defects have the potential to be used as stable, readable qubits for quantum technologies, for example, quantum sensing. But modeling the full physics of their energetics over time—a key step in developing spin defects into new technologies—is a challenging task.
