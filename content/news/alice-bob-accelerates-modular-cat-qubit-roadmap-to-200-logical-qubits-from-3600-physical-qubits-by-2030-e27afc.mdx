# Alice & Bob Accelerates Modular Cat-Qubit Roadmap to 200 Logical Qubits from 3,600 Physical Qubits by 2030

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/alice-bob-accelerates-modular-cat-qubit-roadmap-to-200-logical-qubits-from-3600-physical-qubits-by-2030/) on November 18, 2026.

Alice & Bob has unveiled an accelerated quantum computing roadmap, aiming for 200 logical qubits from 3,600 physical cat qubits by 2030 in their Carbon system, and scaling to 2,048 logical qubits by 2034. This advancement is due to their cat qubits' ability to suppress bit-flip errors, reducing the physical-to-logical qubit ratio. The modular architecture and AI-assisted design contribute to bypassing single-fridge scaling limits, enabling complex simulations and cryptographic applications.
