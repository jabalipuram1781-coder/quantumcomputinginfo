# QC Ware Demonstrates Hybrid Quantum-Classical Chemistry Workflow with IBM Quantum Hardware

> **Source:** Originally published on [The Quantum Insider](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/07/qc-ware-hybrid-quantum-classical-chemistry-workflow-ibm-quantum/) on August 7, 2026.

Insider Brief Press release – QC Ware (“QC Ware” or the “Company”) today announced a technology demonstration of a hybrid quantum-classical computational chemistry workflow using Promethium® and IBM Quantum hardware. The demonstration calculated electrostatic interaction energy for nitric oxide reductase by combining GPU-accelerated molecular modeling and classical chemistry methods with quantum measurements performed on IBM’s 156-qubit Heron superconducting quantum processor.
