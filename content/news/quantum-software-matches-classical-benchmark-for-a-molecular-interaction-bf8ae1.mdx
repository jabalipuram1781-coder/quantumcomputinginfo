# Quantum software matches classical benchmark for a molecular interaction

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-quantum-software-classical-benchmark-molecular.html) on November 21, 2026.

Predicting how molecules interact is central to drug discovery: it offers important information about how a new drug might work in the human body, the side effects it can cause and more. Computationally modeling these interactions can reduce the need for lengthy and expensive lab work, but some interactions still remain beyond the reach of classical computers.
