# IonQ Demonstrates Scheduled qLDPC Error Correction to Accelerate Fault-Tolerant Gate Execution by up to 74x

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/ionq-demonstrates-scheduled-qldpc-error-correction-to-accelerate-fault-tolerant-gate-execution-by-up-to-74x/) on October 12, 2026.

IonQ researchers have developed a new hybrid physical/logical execution strategy for quantum error correction, significantly accelerating fault-tolerant quantum computing. This approach, detailed in a recent arXiv paper, utilizes scheduled qLDPC error correction and an adapted Logical Clifford Noise Reduction (CliNR) scheme. The innovation has achieved speedups of up to 74-fold for logical Clifford operations and up to 5-fold for logical Toffoli gates, addressing the operational latency bottleneck in quantum computing.
