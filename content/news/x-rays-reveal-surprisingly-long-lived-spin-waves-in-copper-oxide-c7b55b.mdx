# X-rays reveal surprisingly long-lived spin waves in copper oxide

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-rays-reveal-copper-oxide.html) on October 19, 2026.

Today's computer technology, based on silicon chips, is slowly reaching its physical limits. Components cannot be miniaturized indefinitely, and clock speeds cannot be increased indefinitely. At the same time, huge AI data centers are being built in many places, and these consume an immense amount of electricity. This is why scientists around the world are searching for novel materials that can replace or complement silicon technology and consume significantly less electricity.
