# University of Sussex and Universal Quantum Demonstrate Laser-Free Axial-Mode Entangling Gate on Doppler-Cooled Trapped Ions

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/university-of-sussex-and-universal-quantum-demonstrate-laser-free-axial-mode-entangling-gate-on-doppler-cooled-trapped-ions/) on November 26, 2026.

Researchers from the University of Sussex and Universal Quantum have demonstrated a high-fidelity, all-electronic two-qubit entangling gate using Doppler-cooled ytterbium ions. This laser-free gate operates on the axial stretch mode, achieving Bell-state fidelities above fault-tolerant thresholds without ground-state cooling. The innovation offers improved compatibility for quantum computing architectures by simplifying cooling processes and enhancing gate robustness.
