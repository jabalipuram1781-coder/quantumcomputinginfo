# Quantum skyrmions outlast entanglement, hinting at more resilient networks

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-quantum-skyrmions-outlast-entanglement-hinting.html) on October 30, 2026.

Quantum networks could one day connect quantum computers into more powerful systems and enable new forms of secure communication. But a fundamental obstacle stands in the way: Quantum information is extraordinarily fragile, and the entanglement between particles of light on which such networks would depend is easily disrupted.
