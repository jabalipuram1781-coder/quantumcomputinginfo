# Intel to Acquire Quantum Software Leader Classiq in Record $2.3 Billion Full-Stack Expansion

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/intel-to-acquire-quantum-software-leader-classiq-in-record-2-3-billion-full-stack-expansion/) on October 27, 2026.

Intel is set to acquire quantum software leader Classiq for $2.3 billion, marking the largest acquisition in the quantum computing industry. This deal, expected to close in the first half of 2027, combines Classiq's automated circuit synthesis engine with Intel's silicon spin-qubit manufacturing. The acquisition aims to overcome the software bottleneck in quantum computing by automating algorithm design and expanding Intel's quantum capabilities across various industries.
