Key findings show significant growth in QEC research, patent activity, and public funding, with a notable trend towards high-rate quantum low-density parity-check (qLDPC) codes. The report highlights challenges in real-time decoding and cross-layer system integration as the industry moves towards large-scale fault tolerance.
