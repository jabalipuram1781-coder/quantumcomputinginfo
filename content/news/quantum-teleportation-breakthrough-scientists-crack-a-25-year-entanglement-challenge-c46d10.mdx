# Quantum teleportation breakthrough: Scientists crack a 25-year entanglement challenge

> **Source:** Originally published on [ScienceDaily Quantum Computers](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260929053550.htm) on September 29, 2026.

Scientists have developed and experimentally demonstrated a long-sought method for identifying W states, an important form of multi-photon quantum entanglement. The technique could make complex entangled systems much easier to measure, opening new possibilities for quantum teleportation, communication, and computing.
