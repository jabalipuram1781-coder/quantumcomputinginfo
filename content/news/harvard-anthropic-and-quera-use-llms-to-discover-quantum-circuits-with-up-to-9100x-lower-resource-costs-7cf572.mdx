# Harvard, Anthropic, and QuEra Use LLMs to Discover Quantum Circuits with Up to 9,100x Lower Resource Costs

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/harvard-anthropic-and-quera-use-llms-to-discover-quantum-circuits-with-up-to-9100x-lower-resource-costs/) on October 17, 2026.

Researchers from Harvard, Anthropic, and QuEra Computing developed an AI framework that uses Large Language Models (LLMs) to discover and optimize fault-tolerant quantum circuits. This framework significantly reduces the resource costs of core computational primitives, achieving up to 9,100x lower costs compared to hand-crafted baselines. The LLMs achieved this by proposing complete Python programs for circuits, which were then evaluated and refined through an evolutionary process, leading to the discovery of new co-design principles and circuit architectures.
