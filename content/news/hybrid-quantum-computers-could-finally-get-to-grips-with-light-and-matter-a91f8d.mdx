# Hybrid quantum computers could finally get to grips with light and matter

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-hybrid-quantum.html) on October 12, 2026.

Many of nature's most important processes depend on how particles of matter interact with light and other force-carrying particles. So far, however, it has proven difficult for conventional computers to determine how individual particles behave in these interactions.
