# CO.LAB Selects Four Startups for Inaugural CO.LAB Q Commercialization Studio in Chattanooga

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/co-lab-selects-four-startups-for-inaugural-co-lab-q-commercialization-studio-in-chattanooga/) on November 7, 2026.

CO.LAB has launched its first CO.LAB Q commercialization studio in Chattanooga, featuring four startups: RidgeQuantum, Superquantum, esoteriq, and Kottackal Business Solutions. This 12-month program, starting November 2026, aims to commercialize quantum technologies by connecting these companies with regional quantum testbeds, technical partners, and potential customers. The program leverages Chattanooga's quantum infrastructure, including the EPB Quantum Center, and is supported by various partners like EPB, Quantinuum, and Oak Ridge National Laboratory.
