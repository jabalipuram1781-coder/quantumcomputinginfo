# X(2370) emerges as glueball-dominated particle in collider experiments

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-08-x2370-emerges-glueball-dominated-particle.html) on August 6, 2026.

At the International Conference on High Energy Physics in Brazil, the BESIII Collaboration report that, after 15 years of sustained research, it identified the dominant constituent of the X(2370) as a pseudoscalar glueball with spin-parity quantum numbers of 0⁻⁺.
