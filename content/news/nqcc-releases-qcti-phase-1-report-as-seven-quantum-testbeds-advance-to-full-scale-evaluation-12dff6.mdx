# NQCC Releases QCTI Phase 1 Report as Seven Quantum Testbeds Advance to Full-Scale Evaluation

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/nqcc-releases-qcti-phase-1-report-as-seven-quantum-testbeds-advance-to-full-scale-evaluation/) on November 7, 2026.

The UK's National Quantum Computing Centre (NQCC) has released its Quantum Computing Testbed Initiative (QCTI) Phase 1 Report, confirming the deployment of seven commercial quantum computing testbeds at the Harwell Campus. This multi-year program, financed by a £30 million award, establishes the NQCC as an "alpha customer" to evaluate quantum hardware architectures. The program is now transitioning to Phase 2: Testbed Technical Evaluation, which will focus on independent benchmarking and assessing suitability for specific computational tasks.
