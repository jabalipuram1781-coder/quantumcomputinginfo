# Photonic Inc. Unveils Project VANGUARD Proposal for CA$500M (US$359.1M) Semiconductor Facility in Canada

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/photonic-inc-unveils-project-vanguard-proposal-for-ca500m-us359-1m-semiconductor-facility-in-canada/) on September 14, 2026.

Photonic Inc. proposes Project VANGUARD, a CA$500 million (US$359.1 million) semiconductor facility in Canada, to address fabrication and packaging bottlenecks in quantum computing, AI, and other sectors. This multi-tenant facility, anchored in Vancouver, aims to create shared domestic infrastructure, reduce reliance on foreign foundries, and secure sovereign supply chains.
