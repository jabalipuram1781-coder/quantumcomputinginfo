# Horizon Quantum Signs Letter of Intent to Acquire Entropica Labs for $27 Million

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/horizon-quantum-signs-letter-of-intent-to-acquire-entropica-labs-for-27-million/) on November 6, 2026.

Horizon Quantum Holdings Ltd. intends to acquire Entropica Labs Pte. Ltd. for $27 million in an all-equity deal. This acquisition combines Horizon's quantum program synthesis with Entropica's quantum error correction software. The goal is to automate the translation of high-level code into fault-tolerant circuits, integrating application synthesis and error correction.
