# LG CNS and D-Wave Partner to Evaluate Quantum Annealing for Enterprise Combinatorial Optimization

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/lg-cns-and-d-wave-partner-to-evaluate-quantum-annealing-for-enterprise-combinatorial-optimization/) on October 28, 2026.

D-Wave Quantum Inc. and LG CNS have signed a non-binding MOU to explore quantum annealing for enterprise optimization in South Korea and Asia. This collaboration will evaluate D-Wave's quantum annealing technology with LG CNS's system integration to address complex combinatorial problems like scheduling, routing, and logistics. The partnership aims to demonstrate the commercial viability of quantum solutions over classical methods.
