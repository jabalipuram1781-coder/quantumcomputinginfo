# Quantinuum and GE Vernova Target High-Temperature Superconductivity Using Helios Trapped-Ion Hardware

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantinuum-and-ge-vernova-target-high-temperature-superconductivity-using-helios-trapped-ion-hardware/) on October 28, 2026.

Quantinuum and GE Vernova are collaborating to model high-temperature superconductivity (HTS) using Quantinuum's Helios quantum computer. This partnership aims to overcome limitations of classical simulations by directly executing Fermi–Hubbard simulations, leveraging Quantinuum's quantum hardware and GE Vernova's materials expertise. The research seeks to identify novel HTS materials with industrial applications in wind generators, marine propulsion, and grid infrastructure.
