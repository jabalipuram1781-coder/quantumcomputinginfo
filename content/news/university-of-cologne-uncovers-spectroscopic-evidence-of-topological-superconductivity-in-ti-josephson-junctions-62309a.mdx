# University of Cologne Uncovers Spectroscopic Evidence of Topological Superconductivity in TI Josephson Junctions

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/university-of-cologne-uncovers-spectroscopic-evidence-of-topological-superconductivity-in-ti-josephson-junctions/) on November 5, 2026.

Physicists at the University of Cologne have found direct spectroscopic evidence for topological superconductivity in topological insulator Josephson junctions. This research supports the Fu-Kane theoretical model and offers a way to achieve topological superconductivity and Majorana zero modes without relying on destructive magnetic fields. The study used tunneling spectroscopy to observe phase-controlled energy gap closing and reopening, a key signature of topological superconductivity.
