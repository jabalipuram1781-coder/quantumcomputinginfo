# All Nine Award Winners in Japan’s NEDO Quantum Challenge Leverage IBM Hardware; Four Dedicated Industry Sponsor Prizes Awarded

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/all-nine-award-winners-in-japans-nedo-quantum-challenge-leverage-ibm-hardware-four-dedicated-industry-sponsor-prizes-awarded/) on October 24, 2026.

In October 2026, Japan's NEDO Quantum Challenge announced the winners of its first season, recognizing nine projects for their advancements in quantum computing, all of which utilized IBM Quantum hardware. The competition aimed to bridge the gap between theoretical quantum algorithms and practical industrial applications. NEDO also outlined future plans, including case study releases and the launch of Season 2, to further support the commercialization of quantum technologies.
