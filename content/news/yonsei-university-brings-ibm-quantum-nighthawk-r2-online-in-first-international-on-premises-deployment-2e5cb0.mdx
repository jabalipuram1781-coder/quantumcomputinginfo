# Yonsei University Brings IBM Quantum Nighthawk r2 Online in First International On-Premises Deployment

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/yonsei-university-brings-ibm-quantum-nighthawk-r2-online-in-first-international-on-premises-deployment/) on November 6, 2026.

Yonsei University in South Korea has become the first international institution to install IBM's Quantum Nighthawk r2 processor, replacing the older IBM Eagle processor. This upgrade significantly enhances the quantum computing capabilities at the university's Quantum Computing Center, offering a substantial increase in circuit execution throughput and improved qubit architecture. The Nighthawk r2 will primarily support the Yonsei Bio-Quantum Initiative, focusing on research into cellular kinetics, neurodegenerative disorders, and materials discovery.
