# Diraq Finalizes $38 Million CHIPS R&D Award with U.S. Department of Commerce for Silicon Spin Quantum Chips

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/diraq-finalizes-38-million-chips-rd-award-with-u-s-department-of-commerce-for-silicon-spin-quantum-chips/) on November 27, 2026.

Diraq secured a $38 million CHIPS R&D award from the U.S. Department of Commerce to advance silicon spin quantum chips, establishing U.S. facilities and aiming for over two million physical qubits by 2031. This funding will accelerate U.S.-based fabrication, testing, and research for their CMOS-compatible quantum processors.
