# AI supercharges laser physics with a stunning 250x speed boost

> **Source:** Originally published on [ScienceDaily Quantum Computers](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/11/261111005913.htm) on November 13, 2026.

Researchers at Stanford, UCLA, and SLAC have developed an AI system that simulates complex laser interactions thousands of times faster than conventional methods. The breakthrough allows scientists to predict how laser pulses behave inside special crystals in just milliseconds, without sacrificing much accuracy. This could dramatically improve the precision and efficiency of powerful X-ray lasers used in cutting-edge scientific research.
