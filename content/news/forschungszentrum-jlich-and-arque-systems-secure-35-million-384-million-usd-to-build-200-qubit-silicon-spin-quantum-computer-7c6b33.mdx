# Forschungszentrum Jülich and ARQUE Systems Secure €35 Million ($38.4 Million USD) to Build 200-Qubit Silicon Spin Quantum Computer

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/forschungszentrum-julich-and-arque-systems-secure-e35-million-38-4-million-usd-to-build-200-qubit-silicon-spin-quantum-computer/) on November 24, 2026.

Forschungszentrum Jülich and ARQUE Systems have launched Q-STAR.NRW, a €35 million initiative to build a 200-qubit silicon spin quantum computer by the end of 2029. This project, primarily funded by German federal funds, will integrate the quantum computer into the Jülich UNified Infrastructure for Quantum Computing (JUNIQ). The computer will use ARQUE Systems' patented shuttling architecture for scalability and will provide academic and business researchers with access to test applications in various fields.
