# IBM Unveils 520-Qubit Nighthawk r3 Processor, 1,040-Qubit Linked Roadmap, and Managed Hybrid Workflows at QDC 2026

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/ibm-unveils-520-qubit-nighthawk-r3-processor-1040-qubit-linked-roadmap-and-managed-hybrid-workflows-at-qdc-2026/) on November 12, 2026.

IBM introduced the Nighthawk r3 processor with 520 programmable qubits and a roadmap to 1,040 qubits by 2027, demonstrating advancements in quantum computing hardware and software. The company also unveiled an expanded software stack, including Managed Workflows, and reported significant speedups and circuit executions with its Nighthawk r2 processors. These developments are geared towards achieving fault-tolerant quantum systems and addressing complex applications in chemistry, materials science, and optimization.
