# Atomic memory keeps distant ions entangled for about 10 seconds

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-atomic-memory-distant-ions-entangled.html) on October 21, 2026.

Quantum networks, systems that connect devices and allow them to exchange information encoded in the quantum states of particles, could enable more secure communications. To build these networks, physicists need to devise strategies that allow distant particles to share quantum states, preserving them long enough to perform calculations or enable communication between devices.
