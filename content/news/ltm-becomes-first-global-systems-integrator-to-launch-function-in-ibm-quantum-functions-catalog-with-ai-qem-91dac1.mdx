# LTM Becomes First Global Systems Integrator to Launch Function in IBM Quantum Functions Catalog with AI-QEM

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/ltm-becomes-first-global-systems-integrator-to-launch-function-in-ibm-quantum-functions-catalog-with-ai-qem/) on November 13, 2026.

LTM has launched its AI-QEM software in the IBM Quantum Functions Catalog, becoming the first global systems integrator to do so. This software utilizes an AI-powered approach to mitigate hardware noise in quantum circuits without additional quantum processing unit (QPU) runtime. It has demonstrated high accuracy on up to 100-qubit circuits and provides a more efficient alternative to conventional error-mitigation protocols.
