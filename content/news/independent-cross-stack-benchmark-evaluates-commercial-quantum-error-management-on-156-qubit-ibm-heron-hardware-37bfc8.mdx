# Independent Cross-Stack Benchmark Evaluates Commercial Quantum Error Management on 156-Qubit IBM Heron Hardware

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/independent-cross-stack-benchmark-evaluates-commercial-quantum-error-management-on-156-qubit-ibm-heron-hardware/) on September 24, 2026.

An independent benchmark on IBM Heron hardware evaluated commercial quantum error management tools. Q-CTRL and Qedma QESEM significantly reduced errors compared to IBM's native Qiskit Runtime, with Qedma achieving the highest precision at a higher QPU time cost, while Q-CTRL offered a balance of error reduction and runtime. The study highlights varying resource-accuracy profiles among the tested solutions.
