# D-Wave Enhances Stride Hybrid Solver and Releases Applied Optimization Learning Path

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/d-wave-enhances-stride-hybrid-solver-and-releases-applied-optimization-learning-path/) on November 4, 2026.

D-Wave has upgraded its Stride hybrid solver, significantly reducing solution times and improving quality for complex optimization problems like Traveling Salesperson and Assembly Line Balancing. They've also launched a new developer curriculum, "Applied Quantum Optimization," to help users leverage their Leap quantum cloud platform. This comes as D-Wave's technology, specifically an annealing-based quantum support vector machine, demonstrated high accuracy in a lung cancer detection study, outperforming gate-based quantum methods.
