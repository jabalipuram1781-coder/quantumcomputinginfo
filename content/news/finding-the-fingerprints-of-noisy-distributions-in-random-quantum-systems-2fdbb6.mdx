# Finding the fingerprints of noisy distributions in random quantum systems

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-fingerprints-noisy-random-quantum.html) on November 3, 2026.

Imagine shuffling a deck of cards where some of the cards are fading just because of the way they interact with the external environment as you shuffle them. Your job is to figure out the interplay between the shuffling, or "imposed randomness," due to the way you shuffle your cards and how that relates to the fading as cards are exposed to the external environment.
