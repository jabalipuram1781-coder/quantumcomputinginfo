# Plurilock Files Information Circular and Schedules Shareholder Vote for C$33.8M ($24M USD) Acquisition by Quantum eMotion

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/plurilock-files-information-circular-and-schedules-shareholder-vote-for-c33-8m-24m-usd-acquisition-by-quantum-emotion/) on October 21, 2026.

Plurilock Security Inc. will hold a shareholder meeting on November 18, 2026, to vote on its acquisition by Quantum eMotion Corp. for C$33.8 million (~$24 million USD). Plurilock shareholders will receive C$0.084 in cash and 0.0763 of a Quantum eMotion common share for each Plurilock share. This acquisition aims to integrate Quantum eMotion's quantum random number generators into Plurilock's cybersecurity solutions.
