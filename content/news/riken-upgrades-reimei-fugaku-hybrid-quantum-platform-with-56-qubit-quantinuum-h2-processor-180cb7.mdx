# RIKEN Upgrades Reimei-Fugaku Hybrid Quantum Platform with 56-Qubit Quantinuum H2 Processor

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/riken-upgrades-reimei-fugaku-hybrid-quantum-platform-with-56-qubit-quantinuum-h2-processor/) on November 10, 2026.

RIKEN has upgraded its Reimei-Fugaku hybrid quantum platform with Quantinuum's 56-qubit System Model H2 trapped-ion quantum computer, replacing the 20-qubit H1. This upgrade enhances physical capacity, gate fidelity, and computational active space, allowing for more complex quantum chemistry simulations. The platform links Quantinuum's quantum computer directly to the Fugaku supercomputer, creating a powerful testbed for hybrid quantum-classical computing.
