# Alice & Bob Reports Phase 1 Results of France’s PROQCIMA Quantum Program

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/alice-bob-reports-phase-1-results-of-frances-proqcima-quantum-program/) on October 21, 2026.

Alice & Bob has released Phase 1 results for France's PROQCIMA quantum program, detailing technical milestones achieved with their fault-tolerant quantum computing technology. These include operating multi-qubit chips, executing noise-biased CNOT gates, and implementing quantum error correction cycles. The program aims for 1,000 logical qubits by 2032 with an increased budget.
