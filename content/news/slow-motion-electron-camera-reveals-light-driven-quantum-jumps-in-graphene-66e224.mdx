# Slow-motion electron camera reveals light-driven quantum jumps in graphene

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-motion-electron-camera-reveals-driven.html) on October 28, 2026.

Using a new slow-motion electron camera, physicists from Regensburg, Marburg and Jülich revealed how intense electric fields drive electrons in graphene, causing them to jump across energy gaps in a seemingly paradoxical manner. This was made possible by a novel combination of laser technology and electron microscopy.
