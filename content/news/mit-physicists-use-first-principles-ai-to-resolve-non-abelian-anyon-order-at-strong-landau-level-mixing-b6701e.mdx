# MIT Physicists Use First-Principles AI to Resolve Non-Abelian Anyon Order at Strong Landau Level Mixing

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/mit-physicists-use-first-principles-ai-to-resolve-non-abelian-anyon-order-at-strong-landau-level-mixing/) on October 17, 2026.

MIT researchers used a specialized self-attention neural network to solve complex equations related to quantum particles, confirming the existence of anti-Pfaffian topological states. This breakthrough provides a roadmap for building noise-resistant topological qubits in materials like monolayer WSe2 and semiconductor quantum wells.
