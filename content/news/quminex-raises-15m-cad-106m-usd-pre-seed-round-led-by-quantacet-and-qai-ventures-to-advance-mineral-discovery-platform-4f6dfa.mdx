# Quminex Raises $1.5M CAD ($1.06M USD) Pre-Seed Round Led by Quantacet and QAI Ventures to Advance Mineral Discovery Platform

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quminex-raises-1-5m-cad-1-06m-usd-pre-seed-round-led-by-quantacet-and-qai-ventures-to-advance-mineral-discovery-platform/) on October 20, 2026.

Quminex, a Quebec-based exploration technology startup, has successfully raised $1.5 million CAD (approximately $1.06 million USD) in pre-seed funding. This investment, led by Quantacet and QAI Ventures, will accelerate the commercial development and field deployment of its proprietary Quminex Discovery Platform™. The platform aims to modernize mineral discovery by using machine learning and high-performance computing to transform geological data into explainable mineral system models.
