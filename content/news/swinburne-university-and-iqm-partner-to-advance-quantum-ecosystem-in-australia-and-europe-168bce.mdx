# Swinburne University and IQM Partner to Advance Quantum Ecosystem in Australia and Europe

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/swinburne-university-and-iqm-partner-to-advance-quantum-ecosystem-in-australia-and-europe/) on October 13, 2026.

Swinburne University of Technology and IQM Quantum Computers have partnered to advance quantum science and technology across Australia, Europe, and the Indo-Pacific. This collaboration will focus on workforce development through joint training and internships, technology translation via co-development of quantum applications, and international exchange programs for researchers and students. The partnership aims to bridge Swinburne's research with IQM's commercial expertise in superconducting quantum systems.
