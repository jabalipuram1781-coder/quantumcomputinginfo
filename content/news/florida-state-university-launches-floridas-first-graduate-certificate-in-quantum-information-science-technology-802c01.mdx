# Florida State University Launches Florida’s First Graduate Certificate in Quantum Information Science & Technology

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/florida-state-university-launches-floridas-first-graduate-certificate-in-quantum-information-science-technology/) on August 14, 2026.

Florida State University (FSU) has announced the launch of Florida’s first formal graduate credential in quantum information science and engineering: the Graduate Certificate in Quantum Information Science & Technology (QIST). Administered by the FSU Quantum Initiative, the 14-credit-hour interdisciplinary program is accepting applications through October 1, 2026, for its inaugural Spring 2027 enrollment cohort.
