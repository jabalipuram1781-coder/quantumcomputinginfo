# Q-CTRL Deploys Fire Opal Dynamics Simulator on IBM Quantum Functions, Achieving 12× Speedup on Nighthawk

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/q-ctrl-deploys-fire-opal-dynamics-simulator-on-ibm-quantum-functions-achieving-12x-speedup-on-nighthawk/) on November 12, 2026.

Q-CTRL has deployed its Fire Opal Dynamics Simulator on IBM Quantum Functions, achieving a 12x speedup on Nighthawk r2 processors compared to Heron r3 systems for large-scale fermionic many-body physics simulations. This allows researchers to execute complex simulations with a single API call, significantly accelerating computational chemistry and materials modeling. The Dynamics Simulator, available in the IBM Quantum Functions Catalog, employs advanced techniques like deterministic crosstalk suppression to maintain high fidelity and overcome limitations of classical tensor networks.
