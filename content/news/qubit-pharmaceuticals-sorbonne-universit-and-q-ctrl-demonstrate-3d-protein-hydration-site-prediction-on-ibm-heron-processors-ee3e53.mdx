# Qubit Pharmaceuticals, Sorbonne Université, and Q-CTRL Demonstrate 3D Protein Hydration-Site Prediction on IBM Heron Processors

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/qubit-pharmaceuticals-sorbonne-universite-and-q-ctrl-demonstrate-3d-protein-hydration-site-prediction-on-ibm-heron-processors/) on November 21, 2026.

Researchers from Qubit Pharmaceuticals, Sorbonne Université, and Q-CTRL successfully demonstrated 3D protein hydration-site prediction on IBM Heron quantum processors, utilizing up to 123 qubits to identify optimal water placements in drug-target proteins. Their quantum approach outperformed classical methods in certain scenarios, finding lower-energy solutions and achieving higher optimal sampling probabilities. This research paves the way for practical computer-aided drug discovery, with projections indicating utility with approximately 900 qubits.
