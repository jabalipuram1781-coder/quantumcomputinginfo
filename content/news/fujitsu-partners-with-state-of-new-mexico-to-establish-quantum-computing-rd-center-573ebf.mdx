# Fujitsu Partners with State of New Mexico to Establish Quantum Computing R&D Center

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/fujitsu-partners-with-state-of-new-mexico-to-establish-quantum-computing-rd-center/) on October 9, 2026.

Fujitsu is partnering with the State of New Mexico to establish a quantum computing R&D center, focusing on hardware, software, and algorithm design. This collaboration expands on existing research with New Mexico universities and integrates with national laboratories to develop hybrid computing workflows. New Mexico is investing significantly in quantum initiatives, including a $150 million matching fund, to support this partnership and advance fault-tolerant quantum algorithm design.
