# Diamond quantum sensors can detect heart magnetism at room temperature without skin contact

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-09-diamond-quantum-sensors-heart-magnetism.html) on September 17, 2026.

Physicists at Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz (JGU) have developed a technology that uses quantum sensors to measure biomagnetic signals, such as heart activity. Researchers from the DIAQNOS (DIAmond-based Quantum Sensing for NeurOSurgery) flagship project, coordinated by Dr. Arne Wickenbrock in Mainz, have demonstrated the potential of quantum technology for future medical applications.
