# qBraid Unveils qBraid OS and Launches Hosted GH200 GPU Simulators at NVIDIA GTC Berlin

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/qbraid-unveils-qbraid-os-and-launches-hosted-gh200-gpu-simulators-at-nvidia-gtc-berlin/) on October 22, 2026.

At NVIDIA GTC Berlin, qBraid unveiled qBraid OS, an agentic operating system for managing quantum processing units, and launched two hosted GPU-accelerated quantum simulators on NVIDIA GH200 Grace Hopper Superchips. The qBraid OS integrates with NVIDIA CUDA-Q and NVQLink for real-time QEC decoding and classical co-processing, while the gpu-sv and gpu-tn simulators offer significant performance improvements for quantum circuit simulation, supporting up to 35 and 500 qubits respectively.
