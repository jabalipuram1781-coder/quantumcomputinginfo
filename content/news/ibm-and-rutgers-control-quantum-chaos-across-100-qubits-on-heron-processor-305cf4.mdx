# IBM and Rutgers Control Quantum Chaos Across 100 Qubits on Heron Processor

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/ibm-and-rutgers-control-quantum-chaos-across-100-qubits-on-heron-processor/) on October 22, 2026.

IBM and Rutgers researchers have demonstrated real-time quantum feedback control across 100 qubits on an IBM Quantum Heron processor. Their study, published in Nature Physics, implemented adaptive quantum circuits to validate non-unitary dynamics crucial for scalable quantum error correction. They observed a control-induced phase transition between chaotic and controlled phases, marking a significant step in experimental quantum control and hardware certification.
