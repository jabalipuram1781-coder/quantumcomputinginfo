# University of Auckland, NIST, and UMD Demonstrate Octave-Spanning Chip-Scale Microcomb in Nature

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/university-of-auckland-nist-and-umd-demonstrate-octave-spanning-chip-scale-microcomb-in-nature/) on October 9, 2026.

Researchers from the University of Auckland, NIST, UMD, and UC Santa Barbara have developed a chip-integrated optical frequency comb that addresses long-standing stability and control issues in microcomb metrology. They achieved this by inverting traditional frequency comb architectures, using two pump lasers an octave apart to generate a self-aligned, octave-spanning microcomb on a single chip. This innovation has significant implications for deployable optical atomic clocks, GPS-denied quantum navigation, and precision quantum sensing, with a provisional patent filed for commercialization.
