# Ultrathin kagome material opens a route to study unusual quantum states

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-ultrathin-kagome-material-route-unusual.html) on November 2, 2026.

How atoms are arranged inside materials can influence the behavior of electrons and the resulting electronic states. Kagome lattices, repeating arrangements of atoms forming triangles that share corners (with hexagonal spaces between them), have been linked with the emergence of unusual electronic states.
