# Carahsoft Builds Federal Quantum Catalog with IonQ and PQSecure Partnerships

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/carahsoft-builds-federal-quantum-catalog-with-ionq-and-pqsecure-partnerships/) on November 11, 2026.

Carahsoft has partnered with IonQ and PQSecure to provide U.S. federal agencies with streamlined procurement of quantum computing hardware and post-quantum cryptographic solutions. This collaboration aims to standardize access to active quantum technology and quantum-resistant security measures, facilitating the integration of these advanced capabilities into government IT and cybersecurity frameworks. The move signifies a shift towards more structured acquisition of quantum assets for federal agencies.
