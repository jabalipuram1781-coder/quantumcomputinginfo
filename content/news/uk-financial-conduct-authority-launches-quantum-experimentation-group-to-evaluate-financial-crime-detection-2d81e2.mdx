# UK Financial Conduct Authority Launches Quantum Experimentation Group to Evaluate Financial Crime Detection

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/uk-financial-conduct-authority-launches-quantum-experimentation-group-to-evaluate-financial-crime-detection/) on November 7, 2026.

The UK's Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) is launching the Quantum Experimentation Group (QEG) in Q1 2027 to assess if quantum machine learning (QML) can improve financial crime detection. This initiative will bring together financial institutions, quantum tech providers, and research labs to benchmark quantum algorithms against classical methods using a synthetic dataset, addressing data governance concerns. The QEG also aims to inform regulatory oversight for quantum technologies.
