# Quantinuum and HQS Simulate 21-Spin NMR Spectrum on 42 Qubits With 1,400+ Two-Qubit Gate Circuits

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantinuum-and-hqs-simulate-21-spin-nmr-spectrum-on-42-qubits-with-1400-two-qubit-gate-circuits/) on October 9, 2026.

Quantinuum and HQS Quantum Simulations successfully simulated a 21-spin NMR spectrum on Quantinuum's 42-qubit quantum computer, the H2-1. This simulation, which modeled 1,2-di-tert-butyl-diphosphane, demonstrated the viability of deep digital Hamiltonian simulation for complex NMR challenges, even reproducing a subtle double-peak structure that previous systems struggled with. This advancement, detailed in a preprint on arXiv, marks a significant step for quantum computing in scientific research.
