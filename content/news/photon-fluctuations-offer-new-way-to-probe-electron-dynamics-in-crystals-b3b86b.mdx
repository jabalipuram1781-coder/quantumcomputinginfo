# Photon fluctuations offer new way to probe electron dynamics in crystals

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-photon-fluctuations-probe-electron-dynamics.html) on November 18, 2026.

Intense laser light can make a crystal emit new colors at frequencies many times higher than that of the original beam. This process, known as high-harmonic generation, provides a powerful way to investigate the rapid motion of electrons in solids. Researchers usually study the brightness and spectrum of the emitted light. Yet those measurements capture only part of the available information. Fluctuations between individual laser pulses can also reveal how the emission is produced.
