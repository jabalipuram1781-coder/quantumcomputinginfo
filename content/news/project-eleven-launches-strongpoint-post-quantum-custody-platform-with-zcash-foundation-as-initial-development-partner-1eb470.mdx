# Project Eleven Launches Strongpoint Post-Quantum Custody Platform with Zcash Foundation as Initial Development Partner

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/project-eleven-launches-strongpoint-post-quantum-custody-platform-with-zcash-foundation-as-initial-development-partner/) on October 7, 2026.

Project Eleven has launched Strongpoint, an institutional custody platform designed for post-quantum digital asset security, with the Zcash Foundation as a development partner. The platform aims to provide a crypto-agile control layer for blockchain networks transitioning to post-quantum cryptography. Strongpoint is expected to be deployed in Q4 2026, building on Project Eleven's earlier funding and acquisition of Riva Labs.
