# ''Carpet'' growth method yields long-sought 2D quantum material

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-carpet-growth-method-yields-sought.html) on October 13, 2026.

For decades, physicists have sought a practical two-dimensional topological insulator: an ultrathin material whose interior blocks electricity but whose edges conduct it. In ordinary wires, electrons constantly bounce off imperfections, losing energy. But along the edges of a 2D topological insulator, electrons with opposite spins travel in opposite directions, like cars in one-way lanes, so they rarely scatter and lose energy.
