# Quantum X Labs Expands Intellectual Property and R&D Across Neutral-Atom Hardware, Rydberg Sensing, and Circuit Security

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantum-x-labs-expands-intellectual-property-and-rd-across-neutral-atom-hardware-rydberg-sensing-and-circuit-security/) on October 10, 2026.

Quantum X Labs Inc. announced new intellectual property filings and R&D in quantum technology. This includes a patent application for structured-beam Zeeman slower and atomic push-beam systems for neutral-atom quantum computing, and a laboratory program integrating Ramsey-CPT atomic clocks with Rydberg atom RF sensors. Additionally, a patent was filed for watermarking quantum circuits using Weyl (KAK) coordinate decomposition for IP protection.
