# Laser-driven magnetic state defies equilibrium rules

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-laser-driven-magnetic-state-defies.html) on November 10, 2026.

Researchers at the Department of Energy's (DOE's) Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL) and their collaborators used lasers and neutrons to observe a long-lived quantum state in a magnetic material, opening a new way to study materials systems driven far from their equilibrium. Manipulation of quantum magnets is important for advancing quantum information science and could reduce loss of transported information, improve memory devices and benefit other quantum technologies.
