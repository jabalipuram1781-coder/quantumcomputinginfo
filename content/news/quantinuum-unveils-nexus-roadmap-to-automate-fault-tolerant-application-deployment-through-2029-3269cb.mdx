# Quantinuum Unveils Nexus Roadmap to Automate Fault-Tolerant Application Deployment Through 2029

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantinuum-unveils-nexus-roadmap-to-automate-fault-tolerant-application-deployment-through-2029/) on November 6, 2026.

Quantinuum has unveiled its Nexus roadmap, outlining a platform to automate fault-tolerant quantum application deployment through 2029. This platform will transition developers from physical qubit experimentation to logical, fault-tolerant execution, aligning with Quantinuum's hardware progression. Nexus will progressively automate quantum error correction, runtime telemetry, and hybrid classical-quantum orchestration, with tools organized across Develop, Optimize, and Deploy lifecycle phases.
