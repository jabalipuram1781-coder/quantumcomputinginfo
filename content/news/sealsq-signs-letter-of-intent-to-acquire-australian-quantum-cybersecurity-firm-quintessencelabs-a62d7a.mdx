# SEALSQ Signs Letter of Intent to Acquire Australian Quantum Cybersecurity Firm QuintessenceLabs

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/sealsq-signs-letter-of-intent-to-acquire-australian-quantum-cybersecurity-firm-quintessencelabs/) on November 21, 2026.

SEALSQ, a semiconductor and post-quantum security firm, plans to acquire Australian quantum cybersecurity company QuintessenceLabs. The acquisition, a mix of cash and SEALSQ shares, aims to combine SEALSQ's hardware security with QuintessenceLabs' quantum cybersecurity solutions. The deal is expected to close in the first half of 2027, following due diligence and final agreements.
