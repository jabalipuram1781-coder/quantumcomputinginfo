# QCentroid Unveils “Missions” on NVIDIA CUDA-Q to Benchmark Classical, Quantum-Inspired, and QPU Workloads

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/qcentroid-unveils-missions-on-nvidia-cuda-q-to-benchmark-classical-quantum-inspired-and-qpu-workloads/) on October 24, 2026.

QCentroid launched "Missions," a new platform integrated with NVIDIA CUDA-Q, to evaluate the best solutions for enterprise computational challenges. It uses autonomous AI to determine if classical, quantum-inspired, or QPU algorithms are most effective, based on business objectives and operational constraints. The platform also includes a validation step to ensure performance claims are accurate and recommends retaining classical architectures if they are more cost-effective.
