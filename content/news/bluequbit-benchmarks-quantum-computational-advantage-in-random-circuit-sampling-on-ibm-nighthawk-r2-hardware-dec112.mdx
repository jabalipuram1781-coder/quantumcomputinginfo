# BlueQubit Benchmarks Quantum Computational Advantage in Random-Circuit Sampling on IBM Nighthawk r2 Hardware

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/bluequbit-benchmarks-quantum-computational-advantage-in-random-circuit-sampling-on-ibm-nighthawk-r2-hardware/) on October 29, 2026.

BlueQubit, in collaboration with EPFL and the XPRIZE Foundation, has demonstrated quantum computational advantage using IBM Quantum's 120-qubit ibm_phoenix processor. Their random-circuit sampling experiment, involving 61 active qubits, achieved a fidelity that would take over a century for classical supercomputers to replicate. This study highlights significant architectural improvements in IBM processors and validates the classical hardness of their quantum benchmarks.
