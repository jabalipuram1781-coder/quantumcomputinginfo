# Testing Einstein''s laws from the bottom of the Mediterranean Sea

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-einstein-laws-bottom-mediterranean-sea.html) on November 4, 2026.

Are the laws of physics truly the same everywhere in the universe? This fundamental principle, known as Lorentz invariance, lies at the heart of Einstein's theory of relativity. Yet some theories that seek to reconcile gravity with quantum physics suggest that it might not always hold. To investigate this possibility, researchers from the international KM3NeT collaboration analyzed 5,828 atmospheric neutrino candidate events detected by KM3NeT/ORCA, a neutrino telescope located deep in the Mediterranean Sea....
