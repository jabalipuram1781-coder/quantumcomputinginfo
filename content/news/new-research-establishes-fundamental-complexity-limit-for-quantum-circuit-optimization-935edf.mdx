# New research establishes fundamental complexity limit for quantum circuit optimization

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-11-fundamental-complexity-limit-quantum-circuit.html) on November 27, 2026.

Quantum circuit optimization is an important research area, as the computational resources required to execute quantum algorithms remain a major challenge. In particular, two-qubit operations are generally associated with higher error rates than single-qubit gates on current quantum hardware, making reducing the number of two-qubit gates an important objective.
