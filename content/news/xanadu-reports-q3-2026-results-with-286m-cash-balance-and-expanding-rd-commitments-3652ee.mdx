# Xanadu Reports Q3 2026 Results with $286M Cash Balance and Expanding R&D Commitments

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/xanadu-reports-q3-2026-results-with-286m-cash-balance-and-expanding-rd-commitments/) on November 11, 2026.

Xanadu reported a 32% year-over-year revenue increase to $1.31 million in Q3 2026, driven by DARPA contracts and commercial partnerships. Despite an expanded operating loss due to increased R&D, the company maintains a strong liquidity position with $286.5 million in cash and significant funding commitments. Operationally, Xanadu is focused on reducing photonic error rates through industrial partnerships to advance its logical qubit roadmap.
