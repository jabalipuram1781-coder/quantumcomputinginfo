# IBM Research Automates QEC Decoder Chip Design Using Open AI Agent Factories at 11.5x Lower Inference Cost

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/ibm-research-automates-qec-decoder-chip-design-using-open-ai-agent-factories-at-11-5x-lower-inference-cost/) on October 15, 2026.

IBM Research has developed an AI agent factory to automate the design of QEC decoder chips, achieving up to 204x hardware speedup and 11.5x lower AI inference costs. This system utilizes autonomous multi-agent search and open-weight models, making QEC chip design more economically practical for fault-tolerant quantum systems. The agent factory explores architectural transformations to optimize decoding algorithms for high-rate qLDPC codes.
