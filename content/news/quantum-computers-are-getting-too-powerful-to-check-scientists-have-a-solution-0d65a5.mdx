# Quantum computers are getting too powerful to check. Scientists have a solution

> **Source:** Originally published on [ScienceDaily Quantum Computers](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/11/261115085557.htm) on November 17, 2026.

Scientists have developed a new way to determine whether quantum simulations can be trusted, solving a growing problem as quantum computers become too powerful for conventional machines to check. Demonstrated on a 51-qubit system, the technique measures uncertainty and provides verifiable error margins that could help unlock the full potential of quantum computing.
