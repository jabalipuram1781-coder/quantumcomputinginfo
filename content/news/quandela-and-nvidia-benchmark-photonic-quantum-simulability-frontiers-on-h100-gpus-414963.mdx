# Quandela and NVIDIA Benchmark Photonic Quantum Simulability Frontiers on H100 GPUs

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quandela-and-nvidia-benchmark-photonic-quantum-simulability-frontiers-on-h100-gpus/) on November 18, 2026.

Quandela, CNRS, and NVIDIA have established a "simulability frontier" for photonic quantum processors using NVIDIA H100 GPUs. Their research defines a unified metric for when quantum processors outperform classical simulations, moving beyond varying photon count thresholds. They've also released an open-source library, photonic-engines, to support this work.
