Insider Brief PRESS RELEASE — FirstQFM today announced results from applying FirstQFM’s quantum foundation model to a QuantWare superconducting quantum processor at the Israeli Quantum Computing Center (IQCC). FirstQFM’s foundation model builds on top of Quantum Machines’ native calibration stack, and generated single-qubit gates that reduced execution time by 33% on a QuantWare Contralto-D21 QPU. […]
