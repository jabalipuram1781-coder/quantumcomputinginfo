# Brazil’s Inteli Becomes Accredited ICT and Partners with IBM Quantum to Research Decision-Making Under Uncertainty

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/brazils-inteli-becomes-accredited-ict-and-partners-with-ibm-quantum-to-research-decision-making-under-uncertainty/) on October 23, 2026.

Inteli, a Brazilian higher education institute, has become an accredited ICT and partnered with IBM Quantum. This collaboration grants Inteli researchers cloud access to IBM Quantum systems to explore quantum algorithms for decision-making under uncertainty, with a focus on practical industrial applications in Latin America. The partnership aims to advance quantum computing capabilities and workforce development in the region.
