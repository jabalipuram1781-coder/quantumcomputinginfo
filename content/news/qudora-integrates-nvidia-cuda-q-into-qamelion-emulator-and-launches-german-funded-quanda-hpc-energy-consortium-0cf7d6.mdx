# QUDORA Integrates NVIDIA CUDA-Q into Qamelion Emulator and Launches German-Funded QUANDA HPC Energy Consortium

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/qudora-integrates-nvidia-cuda-q-into-qamelion-emulator-and-launches-german-funded-quanda-hpc-energy-consortium/) on November 17, 2026.

QUDORA Technologies has integrated NVIDIA CUDA-Q into its Qamelion emulator, allowing developers to execute hybrid quantum-classical workflows and benchmark circuits under realistic trapped-ion noise. Additionally, QUDORA launched the QUANDA consortium, a German-funded project to automate trapped-ion access within high-performance computing centers for quantum machine learning applications in the energy sector.
