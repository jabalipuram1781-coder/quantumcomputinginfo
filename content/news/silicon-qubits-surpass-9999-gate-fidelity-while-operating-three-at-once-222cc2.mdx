# Silicon qubits surpass 99.99% gate fidelity while operating three at once

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-silicon-qubits-surpass-gate-fidelity.html) on October 15, 2026.

Silicon spin qubits are a promising platform for building scalable quantum computers as they are compatible with the semiconductor manufacturing technologies used to produce today's computer chips. However, they have an important weakness: silicon spin qubits typically lose accuracy when several qubits are under parallel control. To be useful, quantum computers will need to operate many qubits simultaneously without compromising the accuracy of their operations.
