# FirstQFM Foundation Model Cuts Quantum Gate Times by 33% on QuantWare QPU via NVIDIA Computing

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/firstqfm-foundation-model-cuts-quantum-gate-times-by-33-on-quantware-qpu-via-nvidia-computing/) on October 15, 2026.

FirstQFM AB has achieved a 33% reduction in single-qubit gate execution times on QuantWare’s 21-qubit Contralto-D21 superconducting quantum processor, without compromising gate fidelity. This was accomplished using their patented quantum foundation model technology, which integrates with Quantum Machines’ OPX control hardware and QUA calibration stack. The model, developed with NVIDIA’s accelerated computing infrastructure, also improved two-qubit Bell-state fidelities.
