# Quantinuum and RIKEN Execute End-to-End Hybrid Quantum-HPC Chemistry on Fugaku and Reimei

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/quantinuum-and-riken-execute-end-to-end-hybrid-quantum-hpc-chemistry-on-fugaku-and-reimei/) on November 10, 2026.

Quantinuum and RIKEN have successfully integrated Japan's Fugaku supercomputer with Quantinuum's Reimei quantum processor to perform hybrid quantum-classical chemistry simulations. This collaboration, detailed in an arXiv preprint, achieved accurate biomolecular excited-state energy calculations, showcasing a new workflow for complex chemical reactions. The project highlights direct API orchestration, error detection, and hybrid data exchange between a supercomputing center and a quantum coprocessor.
