# When simulations fall short, this spectrometer learns for itself

> **Source:** Originally published on [Phys.org Quantum Physics](https://phys.org/news/2026-10-simulations-fall-short-spectrometer.html) on October 28, 2026.

An NMR spectrometer can reveal molecular structure down to the atomic level, but weak signals limit what it can see. Now, Aarhus University researchers have used AI to let the spectrometer learn the best pulse sequence directly from the sample.
