# Infleqtion Secures £9 Million ($12 Million USD) Innovate UK Award to Transition Rydberg Quantum RF Sensing to Operational Deployment

> **Source:** Originally published on [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/infleqtion-secures-9-million-12-million-usd-innovate-uk-award-to-transition-rydberg-quantum-rf-sensing-to-operational-deployment/) on October 14, 2026.

Infleqtion has secured a £9 million Innovate UK award to advance its Quantum Radio Frequency (QRF) direction-finding system, Quantum Spectrum-DF. This funding, part of the Quantum Missions Primer program, supports the transition of the Rydberg-atom-based system from prototype to operational deployment. Developed with Leonardo, the technology uses optical techniques for broadband, passive direction finding, offering a compact alternative to traditional antenna arrays for defense and infrastructure security.
