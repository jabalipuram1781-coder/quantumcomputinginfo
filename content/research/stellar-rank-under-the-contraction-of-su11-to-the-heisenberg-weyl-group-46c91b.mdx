> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00133) on October 2, 2026.

arXiv:2610.00133v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Under the contraction of $SU(1,1)$ to the Heisenberg-Weyl group, the zeros of the Husimi function of a state redistribute according to a single scale, and we determine which of them survive. The stellar rank of a single-mode bosonic pure state, the number of these zeros, characterises the Gaussian states on the plane through the vanishing of the rank. On the Poincar\'e disk, where the same construction applies to the discrete series of $SU(1,1)$, it does not, because the zero set leaves a zero-free factor undetermined. We therefore ask how the zero structure behaves in the contraction limit rather than how it should be classified at fixed Bargmann index.
