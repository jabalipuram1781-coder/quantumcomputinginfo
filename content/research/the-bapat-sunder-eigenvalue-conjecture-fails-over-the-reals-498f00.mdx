# The Bapat-Sunder eigenvalue conjecture fails over the reals

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23269) on November 19, 2026.

arXiv:2611.23269v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In 1986 Bapat and Sunder asked whether $\operatorname\{per\}(A)$ is the largest eigenvalue of $F_A=[a_\{i,j\}\operatorname\{per\}(A(i,j))]$ for every positive semidefinite Hermitian $A$, where $A(i,j)$ deletes row $i$ and column $j$. Drury disproved this over the complex field in 2018 and conjectured that the property holds for real positive semidefinite matrices. We disprove Drury's conjecture with a rank-$4$, $32\times32$ integer Gram matrix and an integer vector whose Rayleigh quotient exceeds $\operatorname\{per\}(A)$; verification uses integer arithmetic.
