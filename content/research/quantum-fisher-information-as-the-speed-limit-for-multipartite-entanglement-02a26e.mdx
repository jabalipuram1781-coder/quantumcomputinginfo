# Quantum Fisher Information as the Speed Limit for Multipartite Entanglement

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31853) on September 29, 2026.

arXiv:2609.31853v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We derive a universal upper bound on the speed of multipartite entanglement generation. For arbitrary differentiable pure multipartite states undergoing parameter-dependent evolution, we prove that the rate of change of generalized concurrence is bounded by the square root of the quantum Fisher information (QFI). The proof follows directly from the symmetric logarithmic derivative formalism, revealing the bound as a consequence of quantum estimation theory, while a complementary Schmidt-spectrum analysis identifies the conditions for saturation and yields a tighter bound for rank-two bipartitions.
