# Mitigating large circuits with a learned small-circuit mitigator

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28261) on November 24, 2026.

arXiv:2611.28261v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Learning-based quantum error mitigation infers noise-free results from noisy measurement data. However, exact training labels become prohibitively expensive to compute as circuits grow, and the generalization capability of a trained model to infer accurate results on circuits of depths and sizes it was not trained on poses a challenge. We show that circuit cutting allows such a model to be reused on circuits larger than those it was trained on. We benchmark this approach against zero-noise extrapolation, Clifford data regression, and probabilistic error cancellation on simulated circuits several times wider and deeper than the training circuits.
