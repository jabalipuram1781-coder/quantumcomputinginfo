# Scrambling leads to entanglement cost in NLQC

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23378) on November 19, 2026.

arXiv:2611.23378v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In a non-local quantum computation (NLQC), shared entanglement along with a single simultaneous round of quantum communication is used to implement a joint channel. NLQC has many applications, most of which benefit from an understanding of lower bounds on the entanglement needed to implement a given channel as an NLQC. However, the properties of a channel that lead to it having an entanglement cost in NLQC have remained unclear, with most lower bounds only applying to specially constructed examples. Here, we give a new lower bound technique for unitaries based on scrambling: we find that a unitary has zero entanglement cost if and only if it does not scramble.
