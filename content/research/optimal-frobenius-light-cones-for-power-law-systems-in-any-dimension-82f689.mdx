# Optimal Frobenius light cones for power-law systems in any dimension

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18829) on November 17, 2026.

arXiv:2611.18829v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We prove optimal Frobenius Lieb-Robinson bounds for power-law systems with power-law exponent $\alpha$ in any dimension $d$, in particular showing that the algebraic to linear light cone transition occurs at $\alpha=d+1$. The proof uses a spectral clipping method and concentration result to allow for running a multiscale operator-norm argument which produces the optimal Frobenius norm exponent. Additionally, we prove there is a Frobenius light cone transition from polylogarithmic to algebraic at the summability threshold $\alpha=d$, and a transition from decaying in volume to non-decaying in volume at $\alpha=d/2$....
