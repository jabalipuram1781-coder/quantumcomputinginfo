# Detector-Frame Quantum Limits for Heterodyne Timing in Relativistic Optical Links

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13963) on October 14, 2026.

arXiv:2610.13963v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Long-baseline optical links for space interferometry and satellite quantum networks rely on heterodyne phase tracking under Doppler motion and gravitational frequency shifts. These relativistic shifts can generate large detector-frame beat-frequency offsets over finite readout windows and thereby constrain the temporal precision of front-end phase readout. We develop a detector-frame framework for this problem using an effective two-mode signal--local-oscillator model in which relativistic motion rescales the local-oscillator frequency.
