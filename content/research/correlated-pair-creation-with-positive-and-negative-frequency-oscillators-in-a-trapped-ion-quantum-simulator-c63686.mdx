# Correlated Pair Creation with Positive- and Negative-Frequency Oscillators in a Trapped-Ion Quantum Simulator

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12579) on October 12, 2026.

arXiv:2610.12579v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Spin-dependent forces enable independent control over the effective frequencies of bosonic motional modes and their coupling strengths to spins in trapped-ion spin-boson quantum simulators. We show that the relative signs of these effective frequencies provide an important programmable resource for engineering distinct multimode dynamics. Focusing on a two-mode quantum Rabi model, changing the frequencies of the two modes from the same sign to opposite signs reorganizes the resonance structure of the spin-mediated bosonic interaction, converting the dominant resonant process from excitation exchange to correlated pair creation.
