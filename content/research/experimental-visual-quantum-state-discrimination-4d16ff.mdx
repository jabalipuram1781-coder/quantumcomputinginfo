# Experimental visual quantum state discrimination

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10282) on November 10, 2026.

This approach is inherently suboptimal in photon-limited and noisy conditions, where critical information is irreversibly lost at the measurement stage. We address this gap by introducing AI-driven optimization of quantum measurements on the incoming photons, learning how to extract the maximum possible information for a given computer vision task from each incoming photon.
