# Distant Bell States via Measured Spin Chain Dynamics

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28325) on November 24, 2026.

arXiv:2611.28325v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Preparing distant qubits in Bell states is a core requirement for quantum networking and distributed quantum computation. The nonequilibrium unitary dynamics of spin chains have been envisaged as a convenient quantum channel for intra-chip communications. Typically it establishes mixed entangled states between its remote ends, to be purified by entanglement distillation after several uses of the channel to obtain Bell states. Here we show how the establishment of entanglement via spin chain dynamics can be (a) sped up and (b) made to yield a pure entangled state after a single usage of the channel, which is upgradable to a Bell-state without the complexity of purification.
