# Black holes as black boxes: a query lower bound on python''s lunch decoding

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21723) on November 18, 2026.

arXiv:2611.21723v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The python's lunch conjecture relates the complexity of bulk reconstruction in holographic tensor networks to an exponential cost associated with post-selection. We establish a tight query lower bound for this decoding problem in a Haar random oracle model, with a python's lunch circuit that applies Haar random unitary $U$ to a logical input and fixed ancillas, followed by post-selection on $m$ output qubits. Given one copy of the normalized encoded state and coherent oracle access to $U$ and $U^\dagger$, including controlled queries, we prove that recovering even a single logical bit with constant average advantage requires $\Omega(2^\{m/2\})$ queries.
