# Passive memories beyond the Ising model: from SFQ to GKP

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25030) on November 20, 2026.

arXiv:2611.25030v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Self-correcting quantum memories are spin Hamiltonians that aim to generalize the classical memory of the magnetic hard disk (i.e. ferromagnetic Ising model) to protect against both bit flips and phase flips. Beyond the magnetic hard disk, there are other passive classical memories that are used in modern computers, notably: the flash memory in laptops (which traps charge on a conducting island), and the flux storage loop in single-flux quantum (SFQ) logic (which traps flux in a superconducting loop). In this paper we review how storage and writing work in these two classical memories, drawing analogies to the charge qubit and the flux qubit.
