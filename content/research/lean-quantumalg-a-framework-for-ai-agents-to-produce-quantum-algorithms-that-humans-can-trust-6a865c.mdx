# Lean-QuantumAlg: A Framework for AI Agents to Produce Quantum Algorithms that Humans Can Trust

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30216) on October 23, 2026.

arXiv:2610.30216v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: AI agents are increasingly capable of constructing quantum algorithms and proofs, yet machine-checked proofs alone cannot guarantee that the intended problem is solved or that resource claims match the verified computation.Here we introduce Lean-QuantumAlg, a Lean 4 framework for trustworthy AI-assisted development of quantum algorithms.Its Algorithm-Solves-Problem contract binds correctness, success probability, borrowed-state restoration and quantum and classical resource bounds to a single execution, while separating the guarantees that Lean checks from the problem specifications and execution models that humans review.
