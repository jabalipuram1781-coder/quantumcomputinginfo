# Engineering Ergodicity-Breaking and Stable Emergent Conservation Laws in a Generic Quantum Many-body System Under Strong Periodic Drive

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12551) on October 12, 2026.

arXiv:2610.12551v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A periodically driven quantum many-body system is believed to approach a locally infinite-temperature-like state as dictated by the ergodicity hypothesis, when all the couplings/parameters are of the same order. When the drive is stronger than a threshold value, a large class of systems is shown to break this mandate, exhibiting Dynamical Freezing (DF): ergodicity is broken due to the emergence of local operators with permanent memory of their initial values (PRX 11, 021008, 2021). However, an exception to DF has recently been noted in a particular setting (PRX 16, 011041, 2026).
