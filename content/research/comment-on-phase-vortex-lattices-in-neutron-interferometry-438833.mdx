# Comment on phase vortex lattices in neutron interferometry

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28316) on November 24, 2026.

arXiv:2611.28316v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present a phase-based theoretical description of the four-plate crystal neutron interferometer (LLLL geometry) originally introduced by Heinrich et al. [Z. Phys. B 72, 357 (1988)]. The LLLL interferometer can be interpreted as two coupled LLL interferometric loops that share partial beam paths, leading to characteristic interference phenomena beyond the standard three-plate configuration. Using a coherent beam-splitting model we derive the forward intensity in terms of loop phase sums and recover the cosine-sum structure reported in Refs.[1,2]. Based on these results we analyze the phase dependence of the forward beam in the presence of localized phase objects, such as wedges inserted into individual partial beams.
