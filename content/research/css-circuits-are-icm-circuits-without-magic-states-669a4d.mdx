# CSS circuits are ICM circuits without magic states

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21685) on November 18, 2026.

arXiv:2611.21685v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Gidney and Jones recently introduced ``CSS circuits'', generated by $X$, $Z$, $\MX$, $\MZ$ and CNOT, and showed that they can be sampled with $O(1)$ work per gate. They suspected that the class had been seen before. We point out that it is, up to a normal form, the initialisation--CNOT--measurement (ICM) form introduced in 2015 for compiling fault-tolerant circuits, restricted to $\ket\{0\}$ and $\ket\{+\}$ initialisations. The ICM construction answers constructively their question about ``non-CSS-ness as a resource'': every Clifford$+T$ circuit compiles to a CSS circuit acting on $\ket\{Y\}$ and $\ket\{A\}$ input states....
