# Quantum Partial Information Decompositions, an Operational Order on Ensembles, and Direct Quantifications of Objective Information

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20694) on October 16, 2026.

arXiv:2610.20694v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The partial information decomposition (PID) framework is an extension of classical information theory that enables a systematic analysis of the structure of information in multipartite systems. We advance a simple extension of this framework enabling the construction of multipartite quantum PIDs (QPIDs); generalizing recent, exclusively bipartite, extensions. We construct two QPIDs of the Holevo quantity. First a naive QPID based on the classical minimum mutual information. Second a sophisticated QPID enabled by our development of an operational partial order on quantum ensembles in terms of ensemble (inter-)convertability.
