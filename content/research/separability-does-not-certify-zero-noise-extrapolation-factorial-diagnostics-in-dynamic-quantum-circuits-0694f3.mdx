# Separability does not certify zero-noise extrapolation: Factorial diagnostics in dynamic quantum circuits

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28705) on October 22, 2026.

arXiv:2610.28705v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Separately amplifying gate, latency, and mid-circuit measurement errors can simplify zero-noise extrapolation, but a weak interaction between amplification knobs does not establish an accurate zero-noise limit. We distinguish observable separability, response-model adequacy, and calibration of that limit, and test them in a pre-specified $2^3$ factorial experiment on three IBM dynamic circuits. For the primary echoed spectator observable, the latency-measurement coefficient is $b_\{LM\}=+0.0007$ with a nominal 95% bootstrap interval $[-0.0033,+0.0049]$.
