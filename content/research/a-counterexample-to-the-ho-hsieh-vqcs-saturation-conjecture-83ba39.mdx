# A counterexample to the Ho-Hsieh VQCS saturation conjecture

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31308) on November 25, 2026.

arXiv:2611.31308v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We show that there exists a 4-qubit state that can be prepared by a variational quantum-classical simulation (VQCS) circuit ansatz containing 3 layers, but not with 2 layers. This disproves the strong form of a conjecture presented by Ho and Hsieh stating that the expressivity of VQCS on $L$ qubits saturates at $L/2$ layers. The weak form of the conjecture concerning the preparation of the ground state remains open.
