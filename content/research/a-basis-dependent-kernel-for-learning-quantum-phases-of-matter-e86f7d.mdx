# A Basis-Dependent Kernel for Learning Quantum Phases of Matter

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25048) on November 20, 2026.

arXiv:2611.25048v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Identifying the phases of a quantum many-body system as its parameters vary is a central problem in condensed-matter physics. Data-driven approaches address this task by comparing quantum states, for instance through their fidelity, and locating transitions where these comparisons change abruptly. In this work, we introduce the inverse participation ratio (IPR) kernel, a kernel method built for learning quantum phases of matter from IPRs in physically motivated bases. Unlike fidelity-based kernels, the IPR kernel can identify states with physically meaningful similarity even when they are orthogonal.
