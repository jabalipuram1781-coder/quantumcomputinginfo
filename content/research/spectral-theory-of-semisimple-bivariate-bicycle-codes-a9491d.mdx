# Spectral Theory of Semisimple Bivariate Bicycle Codes

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27565) on August 31, 2026.

arXiv:2608.27565v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Extending the classical theory of two-dimensional cyclic codes, we develop an algebraic approach to bivariate bicycle codes. Using Frobenius-orbit idempotents, formulas for logical dimensions are derived and lower bounds on minimum distances are established. A systematic theory of code symmetries is formulated to construct a structured block-monomial subgroup of coordinate permutations. Several explicit examples show how to generate these codes from first principles without relying on numerical searches. An appendix extends the analysis to BCH-based product constructions....
