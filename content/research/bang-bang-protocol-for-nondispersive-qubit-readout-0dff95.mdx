# Bang-bang protocol for nondispersive qubit readout

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23681) on August 26, 2026.

arXiv:2608.23681v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Fast, precise, and quantum-non-demolition (QND) readout of superconducting qubits is a fundamental component of high-fidelity quantum sensing and computation. Conventional approaches typically operate in the dispersive regime, where the qubit-resonator coupling $g$ is weak compared to the detuning $\Delta$. While exhibiting good QND properties, the readout rate is limited to $\sim g^2\sqrt\{N\}/\Delta\ll g$, where $N$ is the number of photons in the resonator. QND readout in the nondispersive regime, where the readout rate reaches its full potential $\sim g$, relies on parameter sweeps that may encounter resonances, leading to measurement-induced state transitions (MIST).
