# Atmospheric Turbulence-Aware Optimization of Generalized Kennedy Receivers for Coherent-State BPSK Quantum Communication

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05471) on September 9, 2026.

arXiv:2609.05471v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We investigate generalized Kennedy receivers for coherent-state BPSK communication over turbulent free-space optical channels. An ergodic mutual-information framework is developed to jointly optimize the receiver displacement and input prior probabilities under Gamma--Gamma fading.
