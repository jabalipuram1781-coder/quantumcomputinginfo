# Surviving readout errors and incomplete postselection in monitored quantum circuits

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20712) on October 16, 2026.

arXiv:2610.20712v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study how readout errors affect dynamics in monitored quantum circuits. Such errors make postselection effectively incomplete, conditioning trajectories on imperfect rather than true records. Focusing on U(1)-symmetric circuits, we find that the charge-sharpening transition survives at a finite error rate, with the sharp phase's vanishing fluctuations replaced by a finite steady-state plateau. The charge fluctuations show a shallow minimum near the ideal transition point as a function of measurement rate, signaling the competition between measurement-induced purification and noise-induced mixing.
