# Spatially Dense, Continuous-Variable Quantum Computing with Solid State Spin Nonlinearities

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12504) on August 14, 2026.

arXiv:2608.12504v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Nanomechanical structures have been investigated as a method of achieving long-lived quantum excitations at radio frequencies. Their high quality factors are especially intriguing as a medium for bosonic encoding of quantum information. However, to leading order, mechanical modes typically lack the nonlinearities necessary to achieve interaction between bosonic channels and thus are limited in their ability to scale to the many-qubit regime necessary for practical quantum computing. In this work, we propose and describe an approach for bosonic quantum information processing that uses strain-sensitive solid-state spins as nonlinear elements to produce the relevant nonclassical mechanical states.
