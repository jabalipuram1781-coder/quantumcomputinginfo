arXiv:2608.19249v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: System-level resource estimation is a key component of fault-tolerant quantum computing (FTQC) toolchains. Its efficiency depends on how global error tolerance is allocated across logical operations, T-state distillation, and rotation synthesis to minimize physical resource overhead. The commonly used uniform-allocation strategy ignores circuit-specific structure and can overprovision inactive or less critical subsystems, leading to inflated space-time estimates. Prior work aims to address this limitation using supervised models trained on offline-generated datasets.
