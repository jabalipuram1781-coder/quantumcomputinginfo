# Modeling Quantum Error Detection with Transition Matrices

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12073) on September 14, 2026.

arXiv:2609.12073v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We construct an explicit transition matrix that exactly describes code-adapted basis-population dynamics of stabilizer codes under circuit-level stochastic Pauli noise. The matrix is expressed in a code-adapted basis that captures probability flow between logical-basis labels and syndrome sectors. For quantum error detection (QED), we incorporate post-selection by aggregating rejected outcomes into an absorbing state, so that a single transition matrix represents a full clock cycle of gates, syndrome extraction, and post-selection. Additionally, we characterize when protocol symmetries permit exact lumping to simpler, more interpretable models.
