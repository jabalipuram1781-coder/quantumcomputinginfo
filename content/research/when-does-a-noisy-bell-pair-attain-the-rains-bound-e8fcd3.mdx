# When does a noisy Bell pair attain the Rains bound?

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33317) on November 26, 2026.

arXiv:2611.33317v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Two entangled Bell-diagonal qubit states with the same singlet fraction have the same Rains bound. We prove that their attainable rates under operations preserving the positive partial transpose can nevertheless differ: the bound is attained if and only if one of the Bell probabilities vanishes. Every mixed entangled isotropic state, in every finite dimension, lies strictly below its bound. This refutes the attainment suggested by finite-block numerics, and places the two-way quantum capacity of the qubit depolarizing channel strictly below its relative-entropy bound....
