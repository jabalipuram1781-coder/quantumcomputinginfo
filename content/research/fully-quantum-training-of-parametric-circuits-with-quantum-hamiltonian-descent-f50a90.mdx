# Fully quantum training of parametric circuits with Quantum Hamiltonian Descent

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33144) on October 27, 2026.

arXiv:2610.33144v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Parametric quantum circuits are usually trained by classical, local optimizers, which use the quantum computer only to evaluate the cost function, or its gradient, one point at a time. Quantum Hamiltonian Descent (QHD) takes a different route: in this quantum algorithm for continuous optimization, the parameters become the position of a quantum particle, with the cost function as its potential, and two time schedules control kinetic and potential energy so that the wavefunction explores the whole landscape and tunnels towards the global minimum.
