# Quantum banking system for secure cash withdrawal

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03706) on November 4, 2026.

arXiv:2611.03706v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A quantum-secure withdrawal framework is presented by replacing reproducible classical credentials with quantum tokens for authentication. The protocol integrates token generation, authentication, and verification into the existing banking workflow using experimentally motivated quantum control techniques. This mitigates card cloning, credential theft, and counterfeiting.
