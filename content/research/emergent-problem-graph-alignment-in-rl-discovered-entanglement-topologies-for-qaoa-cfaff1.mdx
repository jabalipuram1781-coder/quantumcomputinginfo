# Emergent Problem-Graph Alignment in RL-Discovered Entanglement Topologies for QAOA

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07686) on August 11, 2026.

arXiv:2608.07686v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In the Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA), the entanglement topology, where qubit pairs are connected by two-qubit gates, is conventionally set equal to the edge set of the problem graph. This coupling ties circuit design to explicit problem knowledge and may not yield the most trainable circuit under limited optimization budgets. We investigate whether a reinforcement learning (RL) agent can discover more effective entanglement topologies for QAOA-based MaxCut optimization without direct access to the problem graph.
