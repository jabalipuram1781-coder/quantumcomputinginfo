# Stabilizer R\''enyi Entropy Without a Phase Reference

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18955) on October 15, 2026.

arXiv:2610.18955v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Stabilizer R\'enyi entropy quantifies magic relative to a complete Pauli frame. In settings such as helicity states of massless particles, unsynchronized qubit registers, energy eigenbases, and systems under superselection rules, the computational basis may be fixed while the transverse Pauli axes remain unspecified. The stabilizer R\'enyi entropy then depends on the choice of those axes. We define a phase-averaged stabilizer R\'enyi entropy by averaging the Pauli moments before taking the logarithm. It is exactly invariant under independent local rephasings. For pure states, it vanishes precisely when the state is a stabilizer state in every phase convention.
