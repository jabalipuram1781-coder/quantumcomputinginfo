# A Causal Schr\"odinger-Newton Theory that Conserves Energy and Momentum

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31247) on November 25, 2026.

arXiv:2611.31247v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: This paper provides a theoretical justification for using the Causal-Conditional Schr\"odinger-Newton theory (CCSN) as a phenomenological model for classical gravity sourced by quantum matter. In CCSN, the gravitational field is driven not by expectation values in the global quantum state, but by conditional expectation values of the stress-energy tensor evaluated on local states determined by measurement records in the causal past. Because these local states are defined operationally from measurement records, the formulation is independent of any particular interpretation of quantum measurement while leading to unambiguous phenomenological dynamics.
