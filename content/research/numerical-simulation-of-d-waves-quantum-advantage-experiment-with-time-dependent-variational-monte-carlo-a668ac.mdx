arXiv:2609.01719v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Programmable quantum annealers can realize real-time dynamics of frustrated transverse-field Ising models on large, nontrivial graphs. Recent work by King et al. argued that the classical simulation of such experiments would require exponential computational resources for classical methods such as tensor networks and neural quantum states. Here, we numerically simulate the D-Wave spin-glass annealing protocol with time-dependent variational Monte Carlo (t-VMC) using a correlator state tailored to spin-glass dynamics.
