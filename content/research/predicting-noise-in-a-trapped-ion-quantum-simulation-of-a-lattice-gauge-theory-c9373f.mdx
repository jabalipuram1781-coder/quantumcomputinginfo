# Predicting Noise in a Trapped-ion Quantum Simulation of a Lattice Gauge Theory

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03823) on October 6, 2026.

arXiv:2610.03823v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A recent trapped-ion experiment realised a lattice gauge theory in which the internal states of the ions carry the gauge field and their vibrations carry bosonic matter. In that experiment, a charge moving around a loop was frozen in place by Aharonov--Bohm interference. The noise of such a device is usually simulated after the measurements have been made. Here we show that it can be predicted before the machine is run. For each quantity that the experiment measures, we derive, from the reported noise rates alone, a formula for how it decays together with a bound it can never exceed.
