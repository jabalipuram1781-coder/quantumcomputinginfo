# State-selective dynamical freezing in driven Rydberg atom chains

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28215) on November 24, 2026.

arXiv:2611.28215v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Dynamical freezing, the near-complete suppression of dynamics at special drive frequencies, offers a route to mitigating Floquet heating in periodically driven quantum systems. In interacting systems, however, the energy cost of each transition depends on the many-body configuration, so that the freezing condition itself becomes state dependent. We study this dependence in periodically driven Rydberg atom chains under single- and bi-frequency driving. Using Floquet perturbation theory, we show that each spin flip is characterised by a single interference amplitude set by its local interaction shift, and that a state freezes only where the amplitudes of all its available flips vanish simultaneously.
