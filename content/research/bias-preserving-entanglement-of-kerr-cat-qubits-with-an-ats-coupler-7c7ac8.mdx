# Bias-Preserving Entanglement of Kerr-Cat Qubits with an ATS Coupler

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28749) on October 22, 2026.

arXiv:2610.28749v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Kerr-cat qubits offer a hardware-efficient route to fault-tolerant quantum computing by suppressing bit-flip errors exponentially, while phase-flip errors increase only linearly with the mean photon number. To maintain this protection, entangling operations must be bias-preserving, ensuring that phase-flip errors occurring during the gate do not propagate into bit-flip errors. In this paper, we propose an asymmetrically threaded SQUID (ATS) parametric coupler to implement high-fidelity, bias-preserving entangling gates between Kerr-cat qubits. By engineering a photon-number asymmetry between the control and target modes, we enable fast gate operations that suppress non-adiabatic leakage without complex pulse shaping.
