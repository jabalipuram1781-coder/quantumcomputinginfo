# Visualizing two qubits

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08897) on October 8, 2026.

arXiv:2610.08897v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: I propose a simple visualization for pure two-qubit states, constructed from three components: two Bloch balls for the marginal qubits (the mixed states obtained by ignoring the other qubit, respectively) and an entanglement component consisting of a rotoreflection and a contraction. I prove that this picture accurately captures the local and global structure of the relevant state space without gaps or degeneracies: the underlying parametrization is a global diffeomorphism onto the state space. Finally, I use this picture to visualize conditional states, the entanglement degree(s) of freedom, and single qubit rotations....
