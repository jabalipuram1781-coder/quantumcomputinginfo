# Physical Realization of the Luczka Model: Inducing Interaction and Entanglement Between Spins

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31248) on November 25, 2026.

arXiv:2611.31248v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The Luczka model describes the interaction of spins with a common bosonic environment in the pure-dephasing regime, in which the bosonic modes couple directly to only one spin component. Due to its specific form of interaction, the model is exactly solvable and has been widely used to describe spin decoherence and effective spin-spin interactions mediated by a common bosonic environment. However, to the best of our knowledge, its physical realization and the specific conditions under which such an interaction can be implemented have not yet been addressed.
