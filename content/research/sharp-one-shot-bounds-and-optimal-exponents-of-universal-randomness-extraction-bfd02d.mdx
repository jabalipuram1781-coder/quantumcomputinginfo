# Sharp one-shot bounds and optimal exponents of universal randomness extraction

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33270) on November 26, 2026.

arXiv:2611.33270v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We introduce techniques for the study of randomness extraction against quantum side information (privacy amplification) by relating this problem to variants of hypothesis testing tasks with quadratic constraints. Building also on the recently introduced measured smooth entropy approach of [Regula and Tomamichel, arXiv:2603.04493], we precisely determine the optimal scaling of the error of quantum privacy amplification in several regimes. Our results apply to the standard operational security criterion based on trace distance and focus in particular on universal classes of hash functions.
