# Analytic spread complexity in three-level systems and the spacing-ratio distribution

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17417) on November 16, 2026.

arXiv:2611.17417v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The spread complexity characterizes the spreading of a quantum state over the Krylov basis and has been proposed to capture the correlations between energy levels. How such correlations manifest in the dynamics has been studied mainly numerically for large matrices. Here we consider three-level systems, the smallest setting with spacing correlations, and derive exact results for the spread complexity, the Krylov variance, and the Krylov entropy. At infinite temperature, the Krylov basis depends on the spectrum only through the ratio of consecutive level spacings, and the spectral variance enters only as an overall time scale.
