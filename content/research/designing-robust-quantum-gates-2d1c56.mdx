# Designing Robust Quantum Gates

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24039) on October 20, 2026.

arXiv:2610.24039v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: For given control channels, quantum control theory determines whether quantum gates can be realized, but not their resilience to errors. We address this problem by establishing existence criteria for robust gate implementation in $d$-level systems. Our criteria reveal that implementability of all gates is not enough: a universal family of robust gates exists if and only if the driven Hamiltonians share no common symmetry. When these criteria hold, we can make gates resilient to any combination of static perturbations, uniform Hamiltonian rescaling, perturbations at a finite set of selected frequencies, and perturbations with arbitrary selected waveforms.
