# Foliated surface codes for optimal skip-gate leakage correction

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28219) on November 24, 2026.

arXiv:2611.28219v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Skip-gate leakage --- such as atom loss in neutral-atom arrays or trapped ions, or photon loss in superconducting dual-rail qubits --- takes a qubit out of its computational subspace and removes subsequent gates acting on it. If leakage is only detectable end-of-the-line at qubit measurement, error correction circuits must be designed to periodically measure all qubits, as in SWAP-based circuits and foliation. We show that the best SWAP-based surface code, although optimal against space-like skip-gate leakage errors, is suboptimal against time-like leakage errors.
