# Benchmarking the computational power of quantum computers

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12146) on September 14, 2026.

arXiv:2609.12146v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum computing hardware is advancing rapidly toward utility-scale machines that will enable scientific breakthroughs. Many teams are pursuing distinct and difficult-to-compare routes to this goal, using different qubit technologies and logical architectures. Tracking progress toward quantum utility therefore requires rigorous benchmarks that measure computational capability relative to utility-scale challenge problems and enable fair comparison across disparate platforms.
