# Invertible subalgebras for chiral semion topological order

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18953) on October 15, 2026.

arXiv:2610.18953v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Chiral topological order is incompatible with strictly two-dimensional local commuting-projector Hamiltonians on finite-dimensional tensor-product Hilbert spaces. Invertible subalgebras (ISAs) provide an alternative framework. We construct an ISA supporting chiral semions, together with its anti-semion commutant, on a square lattice with one qubit per edge. We give explicit non-Pauli generators for both algebras and prove that together they generate all local operators on the Hilbert space. Starting from the $\mathbb\{Z\}_4$ toric code, we condense the boson $e^2m^2$ and identify the semion and anti-semion algebras.
