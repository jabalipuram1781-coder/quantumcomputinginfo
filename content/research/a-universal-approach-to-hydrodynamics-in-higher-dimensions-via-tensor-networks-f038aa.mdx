> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40776) on November 2, 2026.

arXiv:2610.40776v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Extracting late-time hydrodynamic transport properties directly from microscopic quantum models, especially in dimensions beyond one, is believed to be classically intractable. This owes, in part, to the extensive growth of entanglement in higher dimensions and often a mismatch between geometric and tensor network locality. Here, we extend the Density Matrix Truncation (DMT) algorithm to accurately and quantitatively predict high-temperature transport coefficients (e.g. diffusion constants) in up to three dimensions. Additionally, we demonstrate the ability to capture the slow crossover dynamics characteristic of weakly broken integrability.
