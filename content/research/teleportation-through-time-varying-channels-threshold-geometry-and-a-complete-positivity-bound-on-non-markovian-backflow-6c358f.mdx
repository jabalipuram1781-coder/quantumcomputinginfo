# Teleportation through time-varying channels: threshold geometry and a complete-positivity bound on non-Markovian backflow

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11314) on August 13, 2026.

arXiv:2608.11314v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A Bell pair distributed through a link that combines amplitude damping with dephasing at time-dependent rates has dynamics that separate into a fixed part and a moving one. The negativity, fully entangled fraction, discord, and average teleportation fidelity depend on time only through the accumulated damping parameters $p(t)$ and $q(t)$. Every threshold is therefore a curve fixed in the unit square, and the rates select nothing but a trajectory across it.
