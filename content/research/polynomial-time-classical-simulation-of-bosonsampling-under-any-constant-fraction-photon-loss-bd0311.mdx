# Polynomial-Time Classical Simulation of BosonSampling under Any Constant-Fraction Photon Loss

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27065) on October 21, 2026.

arXiv:2610.27065v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Understanding how photon loss changes the classical complexity of BosonSampling is central to assessing the robustness of near-term photonic quantum advantage. In this work, we present a polynomial-time classical simulation algorithm for lossy BosonSampling that applies whenever a constant fraction of the input photons survives. We consider the standard fixed-loss model, where $N$ input photons propagate through an $M$-mode interferometer and exactly $N-n$ photons are lost uniformly at random, leaving $n$ surviving photons at the output.
