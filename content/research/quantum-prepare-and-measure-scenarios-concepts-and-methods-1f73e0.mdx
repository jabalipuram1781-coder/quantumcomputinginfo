# Quantum prepare-and-measure scenarios: concepts and methods

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20725) on October 16, 2026.

arXiv:2610.20725v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Prepare-and-measure scenarios are among the simplest and most widely used settings for quantum communication. A sender prepares a physical system in one of several possible states, which a receiver then measures. Despite this simple structure, these scenarios offer a rich framework for exploring the differences between classical and quantum communication, revealing quantum advantages, and developing protocols for quantum cryptography and device certification. In this tutorial, we give a ground-up introduction to prepare-and-measure scenarios and the methods used to study them.
