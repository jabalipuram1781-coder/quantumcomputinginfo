# Quantum violation of Causal Friendliness in the instrumental scenario

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17431) on November 16, 2026.

arXiv:2611.17431v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: How far can quantum theory depart from a classical worldview? Bell inequality violations show that no theory can simultaneously uphold absoluteness of observed events, freedom of choice, relativistic causality, and classical causal explanations. While generalized notions of causality, capable of incorporating quantum resources, can account for Bell correlations, the Local Friendliness theorem showed that, in extended Wigner's friend scenarios, the conflict between quantum theory and causal explanations persists even after classical causality is abandoned.
