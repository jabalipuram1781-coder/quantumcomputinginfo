# Hamiltonian Learning at Scale

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23707) on August 26, 2026.

arXiv:2608.23707v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Learning a quantum system's Hamiltonian is crucial for understanding and controlling its dynamics and has recently become a topic of widespread interest. To understand the learning protocol's error tolerances, i.e. its stability in the presence of inevitable errors, this work utilizes tensor network techniques to emulate Hamiltonian learning workflows at scale and with noise. Specifically, we employed a Hamiltonian learning technique based on approximate stationary states which are constructed using matrix product tools. We provide analytic bounds on the Hamiltonian learning estimation errors and perform numerical simulations that highlight learning error's stability under two families of errors.
