# TLS loss in superconducting resonators on epitaxial hBN thin films

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12556) on October 12, 2026.

arXiv:2610.12556v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The integration of low-loss dielectrics into superconducting circuits is of importance to the functionality of quantum computers and sensors. In this work, we fabricate and measure niobium resonators atop few-layer, epitaxial hexagonal boron nitride (hBN) grown by Molecular Beam Epitaxy on a sapphire substrate and compare against a control counterpart without hBN. We conduct radiofrequency measurements which probe the responses to power, temperature, and temporal fluctuations of two-level systems.
