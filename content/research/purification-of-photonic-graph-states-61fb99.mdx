# Purification of photonic graph states

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01710) on September 3, 2026.

arXiv:2609.01710v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Graph states constitute the main building block for quantum computing with photons. Quantum emitters with a hosted spin can deterministically generate photonic graph states, strongly lowering the overhead of multiplexing highly probabilistic linear-optics graph state generation. However, they generally suffer from various noise sources, resulting in reduced fidelities of the produced states. To mitigate this issue, we develop purification schemes for entangled photonic states. We first develop purification schemes to purify arbitrary photonic GHZ and other CSS states, which we generalize to all photonic graph states and stabilizer states.
