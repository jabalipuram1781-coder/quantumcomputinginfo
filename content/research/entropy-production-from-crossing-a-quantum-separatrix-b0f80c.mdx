# Entropy production from crossing a quantum separatrix

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26737) on November 23, 2026.

arXiv:2611.26737v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: If the Hamiltonian of a large quantum system depends on a variable parameter, its parameter-dependent energy spectrum may often form a dense lattice of avoided crossings. If the parameter slowly changes, the system's time evolution through the avoided crossings tends in the semi-classical limit to change from adiabatic to diabatic across a narrow energy range that has recently been defined as a quantum separatrix. Here we show that in the extreme semi-classical limit of a quasi-continuously dense spectrum, all quantum separatrices form a universal one-parameter family, and that evolution through the separatrix becomes efficiently ergodizing.
