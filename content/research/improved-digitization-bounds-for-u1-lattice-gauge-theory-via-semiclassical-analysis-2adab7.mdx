# Improved Digitization Bounds for U(1) Lattice Gauge Theory via Semiclassical Analysis

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26720) on November 23, 2026.

arXiv:2611.26720v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Simulating lattice gauge theories on a digital quantum computer or using tensor network methods requires a finite-dimensional approximation of the gauge field, a process often called digitization. Controlling the resulting error is essential for assessing the accuracy and resource requirements of these simulations. In this work, we derive a digitization error bound for low-lying eigenstates of the Kogut--Susskind Hamiltonian for pure U(1) gauge theory and translate it into a bound on time-evolution error. Compared with existing bounds, our result achieves exponentially improved dependence on the inverse lattice spacing $1/a$ and evolution time $T$, together with improved dependence on the inverse coupling $1/g$.
