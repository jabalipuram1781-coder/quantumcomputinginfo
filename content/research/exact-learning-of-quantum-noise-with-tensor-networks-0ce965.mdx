# Exact learning of quantum noise with tensor networks

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00169) on September 2, 2026.

arXiv:2609.00169v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Accurate noise models are essential for high-performance quantum error correction, yet characterizing the noise of a quantum device typically requires dedicated experiments. We present a variational framework that learns the noise model directly from quantum-error-correction syndrome and logical-observable data collected during error-corrected memory experiments. The fault-event probabilities are treated as variational parameters and optimized via gradient descent to minimize the binary cross-entropy between the decoder's predictions and experimental logical-observable outcomes.
