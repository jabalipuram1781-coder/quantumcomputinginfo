# Certifying closed labs in device-independent tests of indefinite causality

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31365) on November 25, 2026.

arXiv:2611.31365v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Indefinite causal order allows processes in which the order of events is not predetermined, enabling information-processing advantages unattainable with fixed causal structures. A long-standing open problem is whether such processes can be certified without trusting the devices used to demonstrate them, analogous to device-independent certifications of Bell nonlocality. Causal inequalities offer a route towards this goal, since their violation is intended to rule out all definite causal structures. However, fully device-independent certification of indefinite causal order remains elusive.
