# Cavity models of non-Abelian gauge theories

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27070) on October 21, 2026.

arXiv:2610.27070v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In quantum optics and related fields, cavity quantum electrodynamics provides a powerful framework for modeling the low-energy properties of bound charges coupled to electromagnetic fields, using a minimal set of atomic levels and isolated photonic modes. Here, we develop a similar framework for non-Abelian gauge theories, focusing on the pure-gauge dynamics of SU(2) fields as the conceptually simplest example. Starting from the corresponding Yang-Mills Lagrangian, we derive an SU(2) cavity Hamiltonian in the Coulomb gauge, represented in terms of only three localized bosonic modes.
