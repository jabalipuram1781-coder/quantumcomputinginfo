# Warm-Start Quantum Computation for Energy Readout and State Preparation

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12485) on October 12, 2026.

arXiv:2610.12485v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Estimating a ground-state energy and preparing a ground state both split into a front-end that supplies an approximate state $|\chi\rangle$ and a back-end that reads or refines it. We argue, and measure at every step, that the only thing crossing that split is the front-end's spectral weight distribution $p_n=|\langle E_n|\chi\rangle|^2$ over the eigenstates of the problem Hamiltonian $H_P$. Everything downstream is priced in it, so the front-end is a swappable module, and we swap it seven times without altering anything to its right. We call the arrangement WARP.
