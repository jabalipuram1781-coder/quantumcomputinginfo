# Gap-Protected Heisenberg-Limited Squeezing with Locally Interacting Multi-Level Spins

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02314) on October 5, 2026.

arXiv:2610.02314v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We demonstrate a general mechanism to generate scalable, metrologically useful entanglement in the form of generalized spin squeezing in multi-level (qudit) spin systems with power-law interactions. We demonstrate this for spin-changing dynamics, extending the physics of spin-nematic squeezing known from spinor BECs to spatially extended lattice geometries. In the absence of all-to-all connectivity, the dynamics generally suffers from leakage into non-collective finite-momentum modes, destroying Heisenberg scaling.
