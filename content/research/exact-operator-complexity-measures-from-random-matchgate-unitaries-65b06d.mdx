# Exact Operator Complexity Measures from Random Matchgate Unitaries

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38300) on October 1, 2026.

arXiv:2609.38300v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Matchgate circuits describe classically tractable dynamics in a physically relevant setting while nonetheless exhibiting features characteristic of complex quantum systems, such as high amounts of magic. This invites the broader question: to what extent are typical complexity measures saturated under trivial dynamics? Here, we address this by studying the behaviour of different Heisenberg-picture complexity measures in random matchgate ensembles. Our main technical contribution is a tractable and generalisible free-fermionic unitary Weingarten calculus formulated entirely in the Majorana basis.
