# Qupertino: Pure MLX Array Kernels versus Hand-Tuned Metal Shaders for Quantum Circuit Simulation on Apple Silicon

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19147) on September 18, 2026.

arXiv:2609.19147v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present Qupertino, an open-source quantum circuit simulator for Apple Silicon, and use it to ask how far a simulator written purely in MLX array operations can go and what remains for hand-tuned Metal shaders. The framework ships two measured tiers. The pure tier dispatches structured gates to specialized MLX kernels: diagonal gates run as broadcast phase multiplies, controlled gates as masked half-state updates, and SWAP as an axis permutation; a paired dense-path ablation attributes a 25-33x speedup to this dispatch alone.
