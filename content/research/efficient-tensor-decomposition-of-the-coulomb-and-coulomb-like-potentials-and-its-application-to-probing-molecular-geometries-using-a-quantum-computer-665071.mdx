# Efficient Tensor Decomposition of the Coulomb and Coulomb-like Potentials and Its Application to Probing Molecular Geometries Using a Quantum Computer

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26740) on November 23, 2026.

arXiv:2611.26740v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present an efficient canonical polyadic decomposition of the Coulomb and Coulomb-like potentials in real-space grid representations suited to quantum computing applications. By decomposing the matrix elements of Coulomb-like potentials into low-rank Cartesian components, we achieve $O(N^\{1/3\})$ T-gate scaling per Coulomb term, where $N$ is the number of grid points (basis functions) per electron, while maintaining high accuracy. This opens a path to significantly reducing the number of basis functions (i.e., qubits) required compared to previous methods by utilizing improved representations of the Coulomb potential that converge faster with grid spacing.
