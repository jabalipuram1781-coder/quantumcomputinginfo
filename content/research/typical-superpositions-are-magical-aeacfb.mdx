# Typical Superpositions are Magical

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22476) on October 19, 2026.

arXiv:2610.22476v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The non-stabilizerness, or "magic", of a quantum state is a common metric for the hardness of simulation on a classical computer, and is often quantified by the stabilizer R\'enyi entropy (SRE). We show how random superpositions of classically simulable states saturate the SRE to the symmetry-restricted Haar-random maximum.
