# The Entire Quantum Computer on a Classical Leash

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31775) on October 26, 2026.

arXiv:2610.31775v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We show that a classical client with no quantum hardware can use a single untrusted and potentially malicious quantum server as if it were its own trusted quantum computer. Through the server, the client can privately choose and run quantum computations, prepare quantum states, and process quantum states supplied by other parties. It can also keep quantum memory on the server, hidden from the server itself, and use that memory in later computations so its quantum state persists across rounds of interaction. Together, these capabilities let a purely classical party act as a quantum party in a variety of interactive protocols. We expect this virtual quantum computer to be a broadly useful tool.
