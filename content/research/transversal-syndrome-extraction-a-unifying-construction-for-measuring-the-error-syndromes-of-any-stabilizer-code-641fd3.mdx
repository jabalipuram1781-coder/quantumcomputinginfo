# Transversal syndrome extraction: A unifying construction for measuring the error syndromes of any stabilizer code

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30279) on October 23, 2026.

arXiv:2610.30279v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Fault-tolerant syndrome extraction is fundamental to stabilizer-code-based quantum error correction, yet existing general protocols incur substantial costs in either circuit depth or width. In this work, we design ancilla stabilizer states for a general transversal circuit to construct a family of fault-tolerant syndrome-extraction gadgets that interpolate between Shor and Steane syndrome extraction for any stabilizer code. The result is a configurable trade-off between circuit depth and width....
