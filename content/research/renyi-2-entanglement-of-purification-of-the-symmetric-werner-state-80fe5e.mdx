# R\''enyi-2 entanglement of purification of the symmetric Werner state

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00144) on November 3, 2026.

arXiv:2611.00144v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: This paper determines exactly the R\'enyi-2 entanglement of purification of the normalized symmetric Werner state in every local dimension $d\ge2$ \begin\{equation*\} \mathrm\{EoP\}_2(W_\{+,d\})=\log_2\frac\{4d(d+1)\}\{d^2+3d+4\}. \end\{equation*\} We establish additivity for tensor power and, more generally, additivity for finite products of symmetric Werner states with different local dimensions. A canonical purification and its products achieve these values globally, even when arbitrary joint purifications with unrestricted finite dimensional auxiliary systems are allowed.
