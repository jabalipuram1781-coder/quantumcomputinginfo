> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22489) on October 19, 2026.

arXiv:2610.22489v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum states over time were introduced, in part, to extend Bayesian probability to quantum theory and have since found applications across diverse areas, ranging from quantum causality and open-system dynamics to tests of macroscopic realism. While axiomatic characterizations of these states exist for Markovian dynamics, a general framework accommodating non-Markovian dynamics is still lacking. Moreover, extending this framework beyond quantum mechanics to general physical theories also remains elusive. In this work, we develop an axiomatic framework for multi-time states within a class of operational probabilistic theories (OPTs) that includes quantum mechanics as a special case.
