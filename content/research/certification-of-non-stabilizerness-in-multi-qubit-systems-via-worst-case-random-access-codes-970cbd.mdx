# Certification of non-stabilizerness in multi-qubit systems via worst-case random access codes

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26705) on November 23, 2026.

arXiv:2611.26705v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Recent works have proposed semi-device-independent frameworks to certify non-stabilizerness, but the complexity of the stabilizer polytope makes these methods infeasible beyond a few qubits. In this work, we address this limitation by moving to partially characterized measurements in the context of worst-case random access codes. We first establish a simple condition, which we term the stabilizer pigeonhole criterion, that certifies non-stabilizerness in $m$-qubit pure states, and show how it can be applied to reduced states. As an application, we certify magic in $W$ states of arbitrary size using only eight preparations and three single-qubit measurement settings.
