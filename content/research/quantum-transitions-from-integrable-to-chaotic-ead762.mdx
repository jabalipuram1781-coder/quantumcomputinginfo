# Quantum Transitions From Integrable to Chaotic

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18777) on November 17, 2026.

arXiv:2611.18777v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The quantum integrable-to-chaotic transition is commonly pictured as the quantum counterpart of the classical Kolmogorov-Arnold-Moser (KAM) scenario: a phaseout transition in which a family of regular eigenstates coexists with, and gradually yields to, chaotic eigenstates. Here we show that this is not the only fundamental route from integrability to chaos in quantum systems. We identify a second elementary transition---the morphing transition---which has no classical counterpart: all eigenstates hybridize and turn chaotic concurrently, so that no regular subsequence survives at any stage of the crossover.
