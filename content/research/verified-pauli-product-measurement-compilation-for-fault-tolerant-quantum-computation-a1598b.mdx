# Verified Pauli-Product Measurement Compilation for Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computation

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31694) on October 26, 2026.

arXiv:2610.31694v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Fault-tolerant quantum compilers implement gates through Pauli-product measurements and classical feedback. Verifying such a compiler requires reasoning about the quantum state and the recorded outcomes together: a correction reads an outcome, a gate's internal outcomes must be hidden from its caller, and an application's outcomes must survive optimization. We introduce QPPM, a language for Pauli-product measurement programs, with a weakest-precondition semantics over store-indexed matrix assertions, following quantum Hoare logic with classical variables.
