# Classical Capacity and Entanglement Cost of the Amplitude Damping Channel

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28592) on September 25, 2026.

arXiv:2609.28592v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Determining a noisy quantum channel's classical capacity and entanglement cost generally requires regularization over many channel uses. We remove both regularizations for every qubit-to-qubit channel admitting a pure output. For each such channel, Holevo information, channel entanglement of formation, and parallel entanglement cost are additive with those of any finite-dimensional partner channel. This class includes all qubit-to-qubit channels of Kraus rank at most two.
