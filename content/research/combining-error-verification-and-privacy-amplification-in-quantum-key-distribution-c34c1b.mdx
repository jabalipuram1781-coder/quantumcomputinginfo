# Combining Error Verification and Privacy Amplification in Quantum Key Distribution

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10702) on October 9, 2026.

arXiv:2610.10702v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) protocols typically involve two separate steps of error-verification and privacy amplification. Both steps involve the application of a hash function sampled from a suitable hash family to classical data. A potentially simpler alternative is to implement a single-step hashing procedure, announce and compare a fixed number of bits of its output for error-verification, and retain the remaining bits as the final output key. This reduces the complexity of classical postprocessing, the amount of classical communication, and the amount of randomness needed in the protocol.
