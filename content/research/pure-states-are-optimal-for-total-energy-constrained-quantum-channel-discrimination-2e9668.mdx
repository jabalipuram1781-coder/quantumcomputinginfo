# Pure states are optimal for total energy-constrained quantum channel discrimination

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30295) on October 23, 2026.

arXiv:2610.30295v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We consider the discrimination of two quantum channels acting on bosonic modes, with a probe state of fixed mean photon number and no ancilla. We prove that pure probes are optimal: for every mixed probe there is a pure probe with exactly the same mean photon number that performs at least as well. The result holds for arbitrary channels and for every figure of merit built from the Chernoff quantities $\Tr[\rho_1^s\rho_2^\{1-s\}]$, including the quantum Chernoff exponent, as well as for the single-shot error probability.
