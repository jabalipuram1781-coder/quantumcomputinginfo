# Entanglement-Aware Quantum Circuit Optimization through Globally Optimal Partitioning

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03845) on November 4, 2026.

arXiv:2611.03845v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We introduce a scalable framework for globally optimal quantum-circuit partitioning and entanglement-aware block resynthesis. The partitioning problem is formulated as an integer linear program over qubit-feasible convex subcircuits, supporting objectives such as minimizing the number of partitions or a weighted execution cost. The resulting partition selection is exact over the enumerated feasible candidates and their computed costs when the ILP is solved to proven optimality. The selected partitions are resynthesized using an operator-Schmidt rank (OSR) based synthesis technique, which exploits their internal entanglement structure.
