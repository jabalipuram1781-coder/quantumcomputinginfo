# Optically-induced electric Aharonov-Bohm effect

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17435) on November 16, 2026.

arXiv:2611.17435v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Although it was proposed almost 70 years ago, the electric version of the Aharonov-Bohm (AB) effect, where the interference pattern of quantum charged particles is affected by electric fields that do not interact locally with them, was never experimentally implemented. Here we propose a feasible way to experimentally verify such an important effect, for an electron double-slit interferometer with the slits carved in a nonlinear crystal. The AB phase is generated by a quasi-DC electric field induced in the crystal with the passage of a light pulse (the optical rectification effect) while the electron is superposed in the two slits.
