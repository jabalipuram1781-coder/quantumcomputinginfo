# R\''enyi multi-entropy of graph states and the $n -> 1$ limit

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27080) on October 21, 2026.

arXiv:2610.27080v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: R\'enyi multi-entropy extends the R\'enyi entropy to pure states of more than two parties. It is defined by a replica contraction at integer replica number $n$, and the multi-entropy is its $n\to1$ limit. In holography this limit is conjectured to compute the area of a minimal soap-film web in the bulk. Understanding multi-entropy for general states, however, is harder. Unlike the bipartite case, the replica contraction is not a moment of a density matrix, and the continuation to $n=1$ has to be found by other means. We study this problem for qubit graph states.
