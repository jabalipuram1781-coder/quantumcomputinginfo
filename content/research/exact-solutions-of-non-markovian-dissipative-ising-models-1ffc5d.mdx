# Exact solutions of non-Markovian dissipative Ising models

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12546) on October 12, 2026.

arXiv:2610.12546v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Applications of quantum devices and simulators require an accurate understanding of their dynamics as well as the effects of environmental noise. While some sources of noise can be modeled as Markovian dissipation, coupling to resonators or vibrational modes often produces non-Markovian dynamics as well, making it difficult to characterize the system, particularly for interacting many-body systems. Here, we identify a broad class of exactly solvable many-body quantum systems with both non-Markovian dynamics and Markovian dissipation. We consider a prototypical model of spins coupled to lossy bosonic modes that mediate Ising-type interactions.
