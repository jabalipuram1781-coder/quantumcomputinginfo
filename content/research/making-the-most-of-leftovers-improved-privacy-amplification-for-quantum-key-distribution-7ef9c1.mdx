# Making the most of leftovers: Improved privacy amplification for quantum key distribution

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38306) on October 1, 2026.

arXiv:2609.38306v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The amount of secret key that can be obtained from a quantum key distribution run depends on both the physically observed error rates and the mathematical bounds used to certify security. For finite datasets, conservative bounds force users to discard a substantial fraction of the potentially available key. Here we further refine and extend the privacy amplification bounds achievable through the recent leftover hash lemma of Regula and Tomamichel [arXiv:2603.04493] and incorporate them into the security analysis of quantum key distribution based on entropic uncertainty relations.
