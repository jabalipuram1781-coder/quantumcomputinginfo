# Collective Superconducting-Qubit Interfaces for Microwave-to-Optical Quantum Transduction

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23383) on November 19, 2026.

arXiv:2611.23383v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Microwave-to-optical quantum transduction is essential for connecting superconducting quantum processors through optical networks. Mechanically mediated transducers, however, remain limited by weak electromechanical coupling, dissipation, and added noise. In this work, we study a transducer in which an ensemble of superconducting qubits couples collectively to a shared optomechanical resonator. For weak signals, we show that ensemble size and optical pump strength together set the conversion efficiency, added noise, and bandwidth, with pump heating limiting efficiency to a finite optimal pump strength.
