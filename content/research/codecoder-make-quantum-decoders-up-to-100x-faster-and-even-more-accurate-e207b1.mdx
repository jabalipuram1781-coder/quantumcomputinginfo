# CoDecoder: Make Quantum Decoders Up to 100x Faster and Even More Accurate

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28748) on October 22, 2026.

arXiv:2610.28748v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Fault-tolerant quantum computers need fast and accurate error decoding. We present CoDecoder, a small neural frontend that composes with an existing decoder to improve accuracy and reduce mean decoding time. It predicts each shot's logical outcome, retains confident predictions and routes the remaining shots to the unchanged fallback. A logical-operator-aware output head scores shifted logical operators, combining equivalent logical lines on surface codes and deciding logical qubits jointly on bivariate-bicycle (BB) codes. Fused GPU inference and efficient routing reduce the overhead paid on every shot.
