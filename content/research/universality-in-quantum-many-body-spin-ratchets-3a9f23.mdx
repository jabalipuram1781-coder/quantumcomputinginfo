# Universality in Quantum Many-Body Spin Ratchets

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08590) on November 9, 2026.

arXiv:2611.08590v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We consider a class of quantum circuits, known as quantum many-body spin ratchets, which act on alternating spin chains with two distinct spin types on even and odd sites. This setup breaks parity symmetry and exhibits chiral transport. It has been shown that the drift velocity in the case of integrable, SU(2)-symmetric quantum spin ratchets is universal, and conjectured that the same holds in the non-integrable case. Here, we prove that this universal drift is exact at all times, requiring only translation invariance and U(1) symmetry. Furthermore, we prove that it is sufficient for the circuit to be translation-invariant on average by deriving the same result in the random case.
