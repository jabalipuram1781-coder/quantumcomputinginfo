# Structure-factor witnesses of thermal entanglement: exact optimal covariance criteria for the XY chain

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33284) on November 26, 2026.

arXiv:2611.33284v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Which entanglement witness is optimal for a thermal many-body state, and what does its structure reveal about the equilibrium physics? We address these questions for the spin-$1/2$ XY chain in a transverse field, using the covariance-matrix criterion (CMC), which requires only one- and two-spin correlations. We show that for thermal states the optimal CMC witness is fixed by the minima over momentum of the three static spin structure factors, which are accessible to scattering experiments and can be evaluated directly in the thermodynamic limit. The optimal witnesses select the collective spin modes with the smallest thermal fluctuations, with their form dictated by symmetry rather than by criticality.
