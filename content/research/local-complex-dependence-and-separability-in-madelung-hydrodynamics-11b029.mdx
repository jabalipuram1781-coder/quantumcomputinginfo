# Local Complex Dependence and Separability in Madelung Hydrodynamics

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10019) on August 12, 2026.

arXiv:2608.10019v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: For a many-particle pure state in a fixed position representation, we consider the mixed cross-particle derivatives of the logarithm of the wave function. Their real part is one half of the Holland-Wang local dependence function of the configuration density, while their imaginary part is the cross-Jacobian of the Madelung velocity field. On a node-free product region, vanishing of all cross blocks throughout the region is equivalent to local multiplicative separability.
