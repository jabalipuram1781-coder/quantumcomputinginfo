arXiv:2610.03726v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A Bell violation by material probes certifies nonlocal behavior, but it does not identify which internal subsystem carried or was measured to produce that behavior. We determine the additional information required to attribute such a violation to a gravitational field. For any setting-independent field-to-probe channel completed before the Bell settings are chosen, an entanglement-breaking channel makes all induced field measurements jointly measurable. A static Newtonian phase pickup belongs to this class, even with arbitrary adaptive probe control.
