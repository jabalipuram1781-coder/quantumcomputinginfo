# Gaussian-augmented bosonic matrix-product states: theory and applications

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28753) on August 3, 2026.

arXiv:2607.28753v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We propose and analyze the structure of a family of bosonic quantum many-body states that have the following features: (i) they include all pure Gaussian states and finite-dimensional matrix-product states as subclasses; (ii) their expectation values can be efficiently computed, allowing them to be used, among other things, for variational calculations; (iii) they admit exact parent Hamiltonians expressed as simple functions of the bosonic creation and annihilation operators....
