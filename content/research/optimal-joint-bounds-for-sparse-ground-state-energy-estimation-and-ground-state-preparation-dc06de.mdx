# Optimal Joint Bounds for Sparse Ground-State Energy Estimation and Ground-State Preparation

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23387) on November 19, 2026.

arXiv:2611.23387v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Ground-state energy estimation (GSEE) and ground-state preparation (GSP) are fundamental problems in quantum computing. Given a Hamiltonian $H$ with $\|H\|\le1$ and a guiding state $|\psi\rangle$ with overlap at least $\gamma>0$ with the ground space of $H$, the goal in GSEE is to estimate the ground-state energy of $H$ to additive error $\delta$ with failure probability at most $\varepsilon$, while in GSP it is to prepare a state with ground-space weight at least $1-\varepsilon$, given a gap lower bound $\Delta$. Their costs are measured by quantum query complexity, i.e., the number of oracle calls needed to access $H$.
