# A Proposal for Quantum Reference Frames in Quantum Field Theory and their Experimental Exploration

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21740) on November 18, 2026.

arXiv:2611.21740v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We construct quantum reference frames (QRFs) in quantum field theory, using localized modes of a scalar field as frames for its shift symmetry. We import tools from relativistic quantum information to explicitly compute entropies and correlation measures of relational observables, investigating their frame dependence and the new features arising from the field-theoretic character of the system. Unlike QRFs for systems with finitely many degrees of freedom, localized field-theoretic QRFs are necessarily mixed in physically relevant states and generically correlated with the subsystems they describe.
