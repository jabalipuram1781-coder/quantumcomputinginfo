# QEDist: Practical Exact Distance Certification for Stabilizer Codes

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21709) on November 18, 2026.

arXiv:2611.21709v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present QEDist, a method and software package that certifies the distance of qubit stabilizer codes. Building on tree-search methods, QEDist constructs candidate logical operators one qubit at a time, using sparse parity checks, stabilizer equivalence, and verified symmetries to reduce the work. For a cutoff $W$, a completed search either finds a nontrivial logical operator of weight at most $W$ or proves that none exists. Because satisfying one check can violate another, we use both satisfied and unsatisfied checks to lower-bound the weight needed to complete a logical operator, and prune branches whose bound exceeds the remaining budget, which substantially reduces net search time.
