# Flux control of measurement back-action and Leggett-Garg correlations in chiral quantum walks

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12519) on August 14, 2026.

arXiv:2608.12519v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Gauge-invariant fluxes control interference in chiral continuous-time quantum walks. We investigate how they affect sequential measurements at a single vertex, using a dichotomic observable that distinguishes return to that vertex from occupation of its complement. For a walker initially localized at the measured vertex, the complete two-time statistics, including measurement back-action and Leggett--Garg correlators, are determined exactly by the return amplitude, connecting temporal correlations to the local spectral measure and gauge-invariant closed-walk interference.
