> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37772) on October 30, 2026.

arXiv:2610.37772v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Grover's algorithm is a textbook example of quantum advantage, providing a quadratic query speedup over classical methods for unstructured search. In this Letter, we characterize the non-stabilizerness, or magic, developed throughout Grover's protocol, quantifying quantum complexity with respect to the efficiently simulable stabilizer states. First, we compute the compilation-independent magic at the end of each Grover iteration, deriving an analytic prediction for the stabilizer R\'enyi entropy and proving that it remains intensive as the system size grows.
