# Symplectic Reck: In-Situ Learning of Gaussian Quantum Operations

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10206) on November 10, 2026.

arXiv:2611.10206v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Programmable photonic circuits are a natural platform for physical learning, where hardware parameters are trained directly from measured feedback. We propose a physical learning algorithm and architecture for continuous-variable quantum systems where a photonic circuit is trained in situ to invert an unknown lossless Gaussian quantum operation. We prove that a generic symplectic matrix $S\in\mathrm\{Sp\}(2N,\mathbb\{R\})$ admits a decomposition into a triangular array of two-mode symplectic gates on neighboring modes, followed by single-mode symplectic gates.
