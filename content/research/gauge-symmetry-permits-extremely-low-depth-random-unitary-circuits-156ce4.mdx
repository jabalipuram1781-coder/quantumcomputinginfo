arXiv:2611.33312v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Continuous global symmetries can obstruct logarithmic-depth symmetric unitary $k$-designs and pseudorandom unitaries (PRUs) through long-range correlations. We ask whether gauge symmetries impose analogous limitations.
