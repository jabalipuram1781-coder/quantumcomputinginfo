# Interacting quantum reference frames

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28732) on October 22, 2026.

arXiv:2610.28732v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We formulate quantum reference frames within the Dirac quantization of gauge theories, where physical states are obtained from a kinematical Hilbert space $\mathcal\{H\}_\{\rm kin\}$ by group averaging. We assume the gauge group $G$ is unimodular, and we define a quantum reference frame as a system of coherent states associated with a group $G_R$ of frame orientations acting on a tensor factor of $\mathcal\{H\}_\{\rm kin\}$. In direct analogy with the Faddeev-Popov procedure in path integrals, we show that the physical inner product can be written in terms of a choice of frame orientation with an appropriate operator measure, but that the physical Hilbert space is independent of this choice of frame.
