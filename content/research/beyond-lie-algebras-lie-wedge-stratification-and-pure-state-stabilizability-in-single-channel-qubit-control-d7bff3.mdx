# Beyond Lie Algebras: Lie-Wedge Stratification and Pure-State Stabilizability in Single-Channel Qubit Control

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00046) on October 2, 2026.

arXiv:2610.00046v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Lie algebras describe how control generators combine, but in open systems they erase the distinction between reversible control and irreversible dissipation. Lie wedges retain this information. We study drift-free single-qubit systems with bidirectional Hamiltonian controls and at most one independently actuated dissipator generated by a traceless jump operator. In this work, we prove that they fall into thirteen structural strata defined by the generated algebra and its Lie-wedge geometry. The control algebra and four channel properties determine the stratum; in each wedge, the reversible directions are precisely the coherent controls.
