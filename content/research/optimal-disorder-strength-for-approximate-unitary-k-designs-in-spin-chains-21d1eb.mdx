# Optimal Disorder Strength for Approximate Unitary $k$-Designs in Spin Chains

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18954) on October 15, 2026.

arXiv:2610.18954v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum simulators with disordered spin Hamiltonians offer a natural route to Haar-like unitary ensembles, but face a competition. Disorder enhances randomness, yet strong disorder suppresses dynamics and localizes the system in the computational basis. This suggests an optimal intermediate disorder strength for approximating a unitary $k$-design, which has so far been identified mainly empirically. Here, we develop a perturbative theory for the moment gap $\Delta^\{(k)\}$, the deviation of the $k$-th moment operator from its Haar value, for the full spectrum of the interacting XXZ chain in a fixed-magnetization sector.
