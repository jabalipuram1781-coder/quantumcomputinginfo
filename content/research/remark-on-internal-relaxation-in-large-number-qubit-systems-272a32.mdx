# Remark on internal relaxation in large number qubit systems

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21681) on November 18, 2026.

arXiv:2611.21681v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: For a time $t_\{c\}$ of calculation in an $N$ qubit quantum computer that is much shorter than the intrinsic Heisenberg time $t_H$, $t_c\ll t_H$, the dynamics of the system is effectively irreversible and the couplings between the qubits give rise to a relaxation time scale $\tau_r$. If $t_H\gg t_c >\sim \tau_r$ one looses quantum coherence exponentially fast. Since $t_H$ scales exponentially with $N$ there is a threshold $N_c$ such that for $N\gg N_c$ one must be aware of possible intrinsic relaxation during calculation.
