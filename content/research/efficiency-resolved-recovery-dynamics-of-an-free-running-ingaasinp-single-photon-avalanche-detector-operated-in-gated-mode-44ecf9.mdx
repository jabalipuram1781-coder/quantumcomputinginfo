# Efficiency-Resolved Recovery Dynamics of an Free-Running InGaAs/InP Single Photon Avalanche Detector Operated in Gated Mode

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17694) on September 17, 2026.

arXiv:2609.17694v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Detection efficiency in gated-mode single-photon avalanche detectors recovers gradually after each gate edge, limiting attainable count rates, yet quantitative recovery models exist only for free-running operation. We extract the gated recovery time constant of a free-running InGaAs/InP detector at 1550 nm by sweeping the gate frequency from 100 kHz to 1 MHz and globally fitting count rates across four dead times, requiring neither a pulsed laser nor a time-to-digital converter. The recovery time decreases linearly with detection efficiency, from 301 to 161 ns between 10 % and 25 %, at 9.3 ns per percentage point.
