# Exploring Quantum Phase Transitions Beyond Landau Theory on a Quantum Processor

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22482) on October 19, 2026.

arXiv:2610.22482v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Direct continuous quantum phase transitions between phases that break distinct symmetries challenge the conventional Landau-Ginzburg-Wilson paradigm and provide a framework for deconfined quantum criticality. Here, we investigate a Landau-forbidden ferromagnetic-to-valence-bond-solid (FM-VBS) transition in a frustrated one-dimensional spin chain using superconducting quantum processors. Combining matrix-product-state-assisted circuit preparation with quantum-hardware measurements, we resolve the suppression of long-range ferromagnetic correlations and the redistribution of competing-order fluctuations across the transition.
