# Storage, Scrambling, and Loss of Information in the Quantum Reservoir Computing Paradigm

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07677) on August 11, 2026.

arXiv:2608.07677v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The suitability of a quantum reservoir computing (QRC) platform for a given time-series processing task is closely tied to the dynamical properties of its computational substrate and design. Information is injected into, processed by, and read from this substrate, and finally passed to a linear readout layer which is trained to perform a specific task. In this work we introduce a classical-quantum state derived from the process tensor representing the dynamical part of this process for typical QRC protocols found in the literature.
