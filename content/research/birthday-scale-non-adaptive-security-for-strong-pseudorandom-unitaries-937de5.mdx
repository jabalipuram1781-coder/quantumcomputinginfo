# Birthday-Scale Non-Adaptive Security for Strong Pseudorandom Unitaries

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25038) on November 20, 2026.

arXiv:2611.25038v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Pseudorandom unitaries (PRUs) are efficiently implementable unitary ensembles that are computationally indistinguishable from Haar-random unitaries, providing efficient substitutes for Haar-randomness in quantum information and cryptography. Strong PRUs require this indistinguishability to hold even when the adversary is given access to all of $U$, $U^\dagger$, $U^*$, and $U^T$. In this work, we prove birthday-scale non-adaptive security for the DPFC (Clifford-Phase-Permutation-Clifford) strong PRU candidate. For an $N$-dimensional unitary, allowing arbitrary entangled inputs and coherent superpositions of query types, we show that DPFC can withstand $t \approx \sqrt\{N\}$ queries.
