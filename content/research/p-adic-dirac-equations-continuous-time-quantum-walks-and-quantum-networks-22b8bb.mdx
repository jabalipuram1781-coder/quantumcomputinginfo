# p-Adic Dirac Equations, Continuous-Time Quantum Walks, and Quantum Networks

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04358) on September 7, 2026.

arXiv:2609.04358v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We introduce a new class of p-adic Dirac equations, formulated in the standard axiomatic framework of quantum mechanics, in which the ordinary spatial derivatives are replaced by non-local operators built from arbitrary integrable kernels.
