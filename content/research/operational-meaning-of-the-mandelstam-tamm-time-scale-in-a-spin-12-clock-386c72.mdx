# Operational Meaning of the Mandelstam-Tamm Time Scale in a Spin-1/2 Clock

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24056) on October 20, 2026.

arXiv:2610.24056v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Georgiev's analysis of a freely precessing spin correctly emphasizes that the Mandelstam-Tamm quantity $\Delta T=\Delta\hat A/|d\langle\hat A\rangle/d t|$ is not the variance of a time operator. He concludes, however, that its divergence near the extrema of the clock signal is devoid of physical meaning and that $\Delta T$ does not disclose how well the system measures the passage of time. For the same projective $\hat\sigma_x$ readout, we show that $\Delta T$ equals the inverse square root of the classical Fisher information in one binary outcome at regular operating points.
