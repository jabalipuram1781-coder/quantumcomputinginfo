# Entanglement Geometry Beyond Polygons: Graph-Theoretic Entanglement Inequalities

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25019) on November 20, 2026.

arXiv:2611.25019v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Entanglement entropies of pure multipartite states can be constrained by organizing subsystem cuts as edges (hyperedges) of graphs (hypergraphs). Starting from the one-party entanglement polygon inequality, we derive these constraints from strong subadditivity and pure-state complementarity, with optimal coefficients and explicit equality conditions. Our central result determines, for a distinguished two-party cut and an arbitrary weighted family of cuts, the optimal polygon coefficient: the smallest total weight of the cuts separating a member of the distinguished pair from a remaining party.
