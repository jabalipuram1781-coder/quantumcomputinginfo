> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26695) on November 23, 2026.

arXiv:2611.26695v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Gauge theories constitute the basis of our microscopic description of fundamental physical laws, and they are a cornerstone of contemporary scientific research. Yet, how such systems thermalize far from equilibrium remains a fundamental open question at the crossroads of statistical, high-energy, and condensed-matter physics. In this work, we investigate the thermalization properties of non-Abelian lattice gauge theories, focusing on the discrete gauge group $D_3$, which is ideally suited for current qudit-based quantum simulators. Specifically, we study a linear plaquette chain in a gauge-fixed formulation that removes unphysical degrees of freedom.
