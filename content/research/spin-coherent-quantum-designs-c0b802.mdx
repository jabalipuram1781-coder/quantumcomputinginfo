arXiv:2608.11310v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Coherent states bridge the gap between quantum and classical physics, but their overcomplete and nonorthogonal nature makes it difficult to identify the minimal discrete set needed to reconstruct quantum information. We show that the canonical finite coherent-state formula reconstructs every operator in the rank-$S$ sector exactly if and only if the sampling points form a spherical $(2J+S)$-design.
