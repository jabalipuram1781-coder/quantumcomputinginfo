# Parameter-Efficient Multilinear Readout for Quantum Reservoir Computing

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13974) on October 14, 2026.

arXiv:2610.13974v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum Reservoir Computing (QRC) uses fixed quantum dynamics as a high-dimensional feature map. Still, standard pipelines flatten reservoir measurements and train a linear readout, ignoring the natural tensor structure across time steps, qubits, and observables. We propose a multilinear readout for quantum reservoir computing (ML-QRC), a tensor-structured neural readout compressed through mode-wise projections. Relative to a dense hidden-width-64 neural readout, this reduces the feature-to-hidden parameter count from $64TnF$ to $d(T+n+F)+64d^3$, giving a $12.1\times$ feature-block compression at $T=50$, $n=6$, $d=8$, and $F=n+\binom\{n\}\{2\}$.
