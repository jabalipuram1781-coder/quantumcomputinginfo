# Look Mum, No Hands! Quantum State Preparation without Time-Dependent Control

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23382) on November 19, 2026.

arXiv:2611.23382v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: How much can be prepared with a quantum system whose Hamiltonian cannot be modulated in time? Common intuition suggests that the answer is not much. Without a knob to turn during the evolution, a system seems confined to a fixed trajectory through its state space, while time-dependent control can steer it almost anywhere. We put this intuition to the test by studying autonomous control, i.e., state preparation with a time-invariant Hamiltonian, where the only freedom lies in the static coupling strengths and the evolution time.
