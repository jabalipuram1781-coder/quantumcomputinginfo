# Quantum synchronization of spin-1 system: Enhanced synchronization due to additive Lindblad operators

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28808) on September 1, 2026.

arXiv:2608.28808v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We experimentally investigate quantum synchronization for one of the simplest possible quantum systems, namely an externally driven few-level system with equally spaced energy levels effectively acting as a spin-1 system. Coupling to excited auxiliary states, we realize additive effective Lindblad operators that are associated with non-conventional dissipative pathways, which are shown to enhance, in certain parameter regimes, quantum synchronization. The experimental set-up, which utilizes cold $^\{87\}$Rb atoms in a MOT, and associated synchronization extraction protocol are benchmarked carefully through dedicated simulations.
