# Quantum Many-Body Metrology of Rotation Sensing with Strong Interactions

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04082) on August 6, 2026.

arXiv:2608.04082v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study the ultimate quantum limit of rotation sensing with a few strongly interacting bosons confined in a quasi-one-dimensional ring trap with two weak links. It is demonstrated that a self-consistent many-body solution of the problem is required to correctly predict the ultimate sensitivity of this strongly correlated many-body gyroscope to rotation. For both small rotation velocities and small particle numbers, the many-body quantum Fisher information becomes maximal for large interaction couplings, showing the potential of strongly interacting miniaturized many-body sensors to precisely estimate slow rotations with high spatial resolution....
