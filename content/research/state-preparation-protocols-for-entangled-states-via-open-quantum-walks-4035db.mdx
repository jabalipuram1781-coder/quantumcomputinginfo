# State Preparation Protocols for Entangled States via Open Quantum Walks

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07695) on August 11, 2026.

arXiv:2608.07695v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Open quantum walks couple transitions on a graph to quantum operations on an internal degree of freedom. We use this structure to formulate protocols for quantum state preparation with nonunitary Kraus operators. We construct a ring-shaped OQW preparing an ensemble of Dicke states, with W states appearing as the single-excitation case, from which any individual Dicke state is recovered by postselecting the walker position; its convergence is governed by the spectral gap of the underlying Markov chain, for which we obtain a closed-form approximate expression.
