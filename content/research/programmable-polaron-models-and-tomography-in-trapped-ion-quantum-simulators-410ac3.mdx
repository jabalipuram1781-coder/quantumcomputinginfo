> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18970) on October 15, 2026.

arXiv:2610.18970v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Strong coupling between mobile charge carriers or excitons and lattice vibrations gives rise to polarons: quasiparticles formed by charge carriers dressed by a cloud of phonon excitations. Polaron formation determines transport properties of systems ranging from transition-metal oxides and organic semiconductors to light-harvesting complexes. In excitonic systems that admit a hard-core boson description, mapping the excitations to pseudospins provides a natural framework for studying polaron physics, including the nonperturbative regime in which hopping amplitudes, phonon energies, and coupling strengths are comparable.
