# Beyond the Quantum Promise: A Security Analysis of Classical Control in Quantum Key Distribution

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07626) on August 11, 2026.

arXiv:2608.07626v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) protocols provide information-theoretic security by using quantum mechanical principles. Yet QKD is fundamentally a hybrid protocol: its security depends on the correct integration of the quantum phase with classical post-processing. While ETSI and ITUT specifications standardize QKD architectures and interfaces, they evaluate protocol security in isolation, leaving cross-layer interactions as an underexplored attack surface. This paper introduces a formal verification framework that holistically models QKD protocols based on ETSI and ITUT QKD specifications.
