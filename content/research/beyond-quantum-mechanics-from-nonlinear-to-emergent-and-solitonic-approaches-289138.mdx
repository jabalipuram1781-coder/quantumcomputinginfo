> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31304) on November 25, 2026.

arXiv:2611.31304v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: This thesis investigates the role of nonlinearity in approaches beyond standard quantum mechanics, following a path from modifications of quantum dynamics to the possibility of an emergent statistical description. We first examine Weinberg's nonlinear formulation and its implications for the statistical interpretation of quantum states. We then discuss de Broglie-Bohm theory and the Madelung formulation, which provide a framework for comparing guided trajectories with ensemble descriptions involving momentum fluctuations.
