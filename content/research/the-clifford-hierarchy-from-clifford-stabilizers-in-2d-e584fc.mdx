# The Clifford Hierarchy from Clifford Stabilizers in 2D

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25025) on November 20, 2026.

arXiv:2611.25025v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The Bravyi-K\"onig theorem limits the constant-depth logical gates of 2D topological Pauli stabilizer codes to be in the Clifford group. Known qubit codes with Clifford stabilizers exceed it by only one level of the Clifford hierarchy, and this was conjectured to hold generally. We show that this bound can be completely bypassed, and that topological codes with Clifford stabilizers in 2D can realize transversal logical gates at any level of the Clifford hierarchy. We present two explicit constructions of codes based on copies of the quantum doubles of $D_4$ and $\mathbb\{Z\}_2$, with $k$ logical qubits and a logical $C^\{k-1\}Z$ gate, which is at level $k$ of the Clifford hierarchy.
