> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20670) on October 16, 2026.

arXiv:2610.20670v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Continuous-variable quantum information processing with mechanical oscillators requires a uni- versal gate set, including a genuine non-Gaussian evolution. While circuit quantum acoustodynamics (cQAD) experiments have realized bilinear and quartic (four-wave mixing) operations by coupling mechanical modes to transmon qubits, the intrinsic Kerr nonlinearity of these devices precludes access to odd-order effective Hamiltonians such as trisqueezing. Here we propose a cQAD architec- ture in which a mechanical mode, hosted in a high-overtone bulk acoustic-wave resonator (HBAR), is coupled to a superconducting nonlinear asymmetric inductive element (SNAIL).
