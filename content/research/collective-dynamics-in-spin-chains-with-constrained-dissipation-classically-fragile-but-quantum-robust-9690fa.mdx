# Collective Dynamics in Spin Chains with Constrained Dissipation: Classically Fragile but Quantum Robust

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38333) on October 1, 2026.

arXiv:2609.38333v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Collective non-equilibrium phenomena in many-body systems are strongly influenced by fluctuations, particularly in the vicinity of phase transitions, where the interplay between classical and quantum effects can alter cooperative behavior. Here, we investigate this classical-quantum competition in a kinetically constrained spin chain, the dissipative far-East model. In absence of fluctuations, the system exhibits emergent collective dynamics, resulting in phase coexistence. We find that this phenomenon is robust to quantum fluctuations, which further stabilize it, whereas classical fluctuations suppress phase coexistence at long times.
