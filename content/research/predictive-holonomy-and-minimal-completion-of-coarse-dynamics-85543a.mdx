# Predictive Holonomy and Minimal Completion of Coarse Dynamics

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13951) on October 14, 2026.

arXiv:2610.13951v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A coarse description of a dynamical system generally fails to evolve autonomously: evolving and coarse-graining in different orders gives different predictions, and the compressed propagator fails the semigroup law. We show that this path dependence is the observable boundary of missing state.
