# Bilinear correlations in Fluctuating Gaussian States with anti-unitary symmetries

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01724) on September 3, 2026.

arXiv:2609.01724v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study bilinear order-parameter correlations in a general class of sign problem-free systems that are described by what we call Fluctuating Gaussian States (FGS) with anti-unitary symmetries, where the weight of each Gaussian measurement in the space-time path integral is positive-definite.
