# Universal Quantum Computation in a Wigner''s Friend Framework

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18839) on November 17, 2026.

arXiv:2611.18839v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The Wigner's Friend thought experiment illustrates a core tension in quantum mechanics: an observer inside an isolated laboratory (the Friend) measures a definite outcome, while an external observer (Wigner) describes the laboratory as a coherent superposition. Here we show that this discrepancy, usually studied as a conflict between descriptions, has a direct computational consequence. We namely propose a protocol by which Wigner can turn the Friend's laboratory into a universal quantum computer, whereby the Friend is restricted to apply simple classical operations within the laboratory.
