# The Limit and Its Ground. QBism, Scientific Realism, and the Born Rule

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22159) on September 22, 2026.

arXiv:2609.22159v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: QBists insist that theirs is a realist position while denying that the quantum formalism represents a mind-independent world. The insistence is hard to place. The realism debate is throughout a dispute about represented content, so a theory that denies standing in the representation relation appears not to take up a position within that debate at all. This paper examines the most developed attempt to make good on the QBist claim--David Glick's perspectival normative realism--and argues that it fails instructively.
