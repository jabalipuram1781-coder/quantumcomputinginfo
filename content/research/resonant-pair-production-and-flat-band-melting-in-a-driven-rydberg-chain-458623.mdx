# Resonant pair production and flat band melting in a driven Rydberg chain

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22532) on October 19, 2026.

arXiv:2610.22532v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study a Rydberg chain subjected to a two-tone periodic drive of the detuning $\Delta(t)$ and Rabi frequency $\Omega(t)$ at frequencies $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2=\nu \omega_1$ respectively with $\nu \in \mathbf\{Z\}$. In the strong Rydberg blockade limit, such a system reduces to the PXP model and exhibits exact Floquet flat band. Here we determine, using a time-dependent Schrieffer-Wolff transformation, the domain of validity of such PXP dynamics once the blockade-violating processes are taken into account. We study the melting of such flat bands in the presence of strong, but finite, blockade strength.
