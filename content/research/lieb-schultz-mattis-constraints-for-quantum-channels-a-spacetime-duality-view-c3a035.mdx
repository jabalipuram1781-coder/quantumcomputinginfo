# Lieb-Schultz-Mattis Constraints for Quantum Channels: A Spacetime-Duality View

> **Source:** Originally published on [arXiv quant-ph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26266) on August 28, 2026.

arXiv:2608.26266v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quantum anomalies strongly constrain the possible behavior of many-body systems. A prime example is the Lieb-Schultz-Mattis (LSM) theorem, which relates UV symmetry and filling constraints to IR features of the energy spectrum and ground-state structure. Here, we ask how LSM constraints shape dynamical signatures and temporal correlations in open quantum systems.
